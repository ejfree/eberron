## Who You Are

Nax is a freelance courier and occasional acquisitions specialist. She delivers packages that other couriers prefer not to ask about, to recipients who prefer not to be named. She arrived at the College of Drystone this morning with a delivery — and has been unable to locate the recipient. Or the exit. Both things trouble her equally.

**Why you're here:** You're still in the building because you haven't found who you're delivering to. Roondar's situation is a good reason to stay useful while you figure it out.

## In a Fight

**Shortsword:** Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** piercing damage.

**Dagger (throw up to 20 ft.):** Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d4 + 3** piercing damage.

---

**Sneak Attack** *(once per turn)*
You deal an extra **2d6 damage** whenever:
- You have **advantage** on the attack (two dice, keep the higher), OR
- An ally is standing next to your target

Get into position. Sneak attacks are how you do real damage.

**Cunning Action** *(Bonus Action — every turn)*
Every turn you can use your bonus action to do one of: **Dash** (double move), **Disengage** (move without triggering attacks), or **Hide** (disappear if there's somewhere to hide).

**Fast Hands** *(Thief)*
Use your Cunning Action bonus to also use Thieves' Tools, pick a pocket, or interact with an object.

**Uncanny Dodge** *(Reaction)*
When an enemy you can see hits you, use your reaction to halve the damage.

## Outside a Fight

You are professionally quiet and professionally dishonest.

**Stealth +7:** Very hard to spot when you don't want to be.
**Sleight of Hand +7:** Pick pockets, palm objects, swap things out.
**Deception +4:** Lying is a skill. You have it.
**Perception +4:** You notice things before they notice you.
**Thieves' Tools +7:** Open locks, disable traps.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Dexterity increases from 16 to **18 (+4)**.

- Attack bonus: +5 → **+6**
- Damage bonus: +3 → **+4**
- AC: 15 → **16**
- HP increases by **6** (new total: **27**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Toughness:** CON 13 → **15 (+2)**
- HP increases by **7** instead (new total: **28**)
- A bit more durability if you end up in melee

**Option C — Feat: Lucky**
Fortune bends toward you.
- Gain **3 luck points** per long rest
- Spend a point to **reroll any attack roll, ability check, or saving throw** (yours or targeting you) and choose either result
- The most flexible insurance in the game
