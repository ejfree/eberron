## Who You Are

Seraphina is a researcher specializing in the theory of inherited magical bloodlines — specifically, what happens when those bloodlines interact in unexpected ways. She has a personal investment in this subject. Her fiendish tiefling heritage and her family's draconic bloodline do some unusual things together, and she has spent years trying to understand it academically rather than be alarmed by it. She is sharp, curious, and runs slightly warm to the touch.

**Why you're here:** You were given a visiting researcher pass to study the college's archives on draconic magic theory. You were deeply absorbed in a manuscript when the incident started. You finished the paragraph first. Then you went to help.

## In a Fight

**Fire Bolt** *(Cantrip — unlimited, range 120 ft.)*
Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **2d10** fire damage. Scaled up from normal — your Draconic Fire Affinity means your fire spells ignore resistance.

**Scorching Ray** *(2nd-level spell)*
Three separate rays of fire: each roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **2d6** fire damage per ray. Focus them all on one target or spread them.

**Fireball** *(3rd-level spell)*
A 20-foot-radius sphere of fire centered on a point within 150 ft. Every creature inside makes a DEX save vs. DC **13**. Fail: **8d6** fire damage. Success: half. Use this carefully indoors.

---

**Metamagic — Subtle Spell:** Cast a spell with no verbal or somatic components — no one can tell you're casting. Useful for discretion.

**Metamagic — Twinned Spell:** Target a spell that normally hits one creature to hit a second creature for the same cost. (Only works on single-target spells.)

**Tiefling Magic:**

- *Thaumaturgy* (cantrip): Minor effects — booming voice, flickering flames, shaking ground
- *Hellish Rebuke* (1/day): When damaged, use your reaction to fire back **2d10 fire** at the attacker (DEX save vs. DC **13** for half)
- *Darkness* (1/day): 15 ft. sphere of magical darkness

**Mage Armor** *(1st-level spell)*
Touch yourself (or an ally without armor). AC becomes 13 + DEX modifier for 8 hours. On Seraphina, this matches her natural Draconic Resilience — no real change unless she uses it on an ally.
