## Who You Are

Gorix is a professional soldier who mustered out of a mercenary company and moved into private security. Blue dragonborn — scales the color of a clear sky before a storm, and a calm that people who don't know him mistake for disinterest. He is methodical, keeps notes, and has an ongoing personal theory that libraries are places where things go wrong. He is adding to that file today.

**Why you're here:** You were posted at the college as perimeter security for a visiting delegation. Your shift ended. The delegation hasn't left. You went inside to check on them. You are now very inside.

## In a Fight

**Longsword** *(Dueling style — +2 damage with one-handed weapon and shield)*
Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d8 + 5** slashing damage.

**Javelin** *(throw up to 30 ft.)*
Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** piercing damage.

**Lightning Breath** *(Recharges on long rest)*
Exhale a 5-foot-wide, 30-foot-long line of lightning. Every creature in the line makes a DEX save vs. DC **12**. On fail: **2d6** lightning. On success: half. No attack roll needed — just point and breathe.

**Improved Critical:** You score a critical hit on a **19 or 20**. On a crit, roll all damage dice twice.

---

**Second Wind** *(Bonus Action — once per short or long rest)*
Regain **1d10 + 3** HP. Use this when you're hurting but don't want to burn a potion.

**Action Surge** *(once per short or long rest)*
Take a complete extra action on your turn. Two attacks. Whatever you need. Use it for a critical moment.

## Outside a Fight

**Athletics +5:** Climbing, grappling, shoving, forcing entry — you're good at all of it.
**Intimidation +3:** The scales and the composed manner do most of the work.
**Perception +3:** Trained to watch. You notice things.
**Survival +3:** Field navigation, tracking basics, handling outdoor conditions.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Strength increases from 17 to **19 (+4)**.

- Longsword attack: +5 → **+6**
- Damage bonus: +5 → **+6** (1d8+6 with Dueling style)
- HP increases by **8** (new total: **34**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Endurance:** CON 14 → **16 (+3)**
- HP increases by **9** instead (new total: **35**)
- More durable in the front line

**Option C — Feat: Alert**
You are never caught off guard.
- Permanent **+5 to Initiative** — you almost always act before enemies
- You **cannot be surprised**
- Hidden enemies do not gain advantage on attacks against you
