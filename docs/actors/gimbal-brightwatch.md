## Who You Are

Gimbal is a Zilargo city guard assigned to the College of Drystone district. She is short even by gnome standards, relentlessly cheerful, and has a habit of making promises she then feels personally obligated to keep. She swore an oath of devotion to the Sovereign Host at sixteen and has been quietly righteous about it ever since — not preachy, just steady.

**Why you're here:** You're on the college beat today. Roondar's problem is technically your problem.

## In a Fight

**Longsword:** Roll d20 + **4**. On a hit: **1d8 + 2** slashing damage.

---

**Divine Smite** *(spend a spell slot on a hit)*
After you hit with a melee attack, you can spend a spell slot to add radiant damage:
- 1st-level slot: **+2d8** radiant
- 2nd-level slot: **+3d8** radiant

Save smites for critical hits — when you crit, the extra dice also double.

**Lay on Hands** *(pool of 15 HP — refills on long rest)*
Touch a creature and restore HP from your pool. You choose how many points to spend. You can also spend 5 points to cure one disease or poison instead.

**Channel Divinity — Sacred Weapon** *(once per rest)*
As an action, your weapon glows with holy light for 1 minute. Add **+2** to all attack rolls with it. It emits bright light in a 20 ft. radius.

**Divine Sense** *(4 uses per long rest)*
As an action, sense the presence of celestials, fiends, and undead within 60 ft. Useful for knowing what you're dealing with.

**Bless** *(1st-level spell, concentration)*
Up to 3 allies within 30 ft. add **1d4** to every attack roll and saving throw. Cast this before big fights. Lasts 1 minute.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Strength increases from 15 to **17 (+3)**.

- Longsword attack: +4 → **+5**
- Damage bonus: +2 → **+3**
- HP increases by **8** (new total: **36**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Presence:** CHA 14 → **16 (+3)**
- Spell Save DC: 12 → **13**
- Sacred Weapon attack bonus goes from +2 → **+3**
- More commanding in conversations
- HP same as the recommended upgrade

**Option C — Feat: Tough**
You are significantly harder to kill.
- Gain **+2 HP per level**, applied retroactively — **+8 HP** at level 4 (new total: **44**)
- No stat changes, just considerably more hit points
