## Quick-Balancing Guide

For groups of 3–6 players, recommend at least one of each:

- **Frontline** (absorbs hits): Barbarian, Paladin, or Fighter
- **Healer/Support**: Cleric, Bard, Paladin, or Druid
- **Ranged damage**: Wizard, Warlock, Sorcerer, Ranger, or Rogue
- **Skills/Social**: Bard, Rogue, or anyone with social expertise

For **very new players**, steer toward: **Barbarian** (Thordak or Gruksha), **Fighter** (Brennan or Gorix), or **Bard** (Lyra or Lute). These have fewer moving parts and strong clear actions each turn.

For players who **want options**: Paladin (Smite when you want, heal when you need to), Battle Master Fighter (Superiority Dice add choices), or Warlock (simple at-will blasting with meaningful slots for big moments).

## Duplicate Class Notes

Two Fighters (Brennan/Zibble and Gorix), two Barbarians (Thordak/Gruksha), two Rogues (Nax/Pip/Rook), two Paladins (Ser Aldric/Gimbal/Lady Isolde), two Warlocks (Zara/Vex), two Monks (Plink/Fen). These play differently enough to avoid mechanical overlap — Battle Master vs Champion Fighter, Berserker vs Totem Warrior Barbarian, etc. Running duplicates in the same group is fine.

## Card Blurbs — All 25 Characters

*One blurb per character. Print on the back of the 5×7 portrait card.*

---

**Zibble Cogsworth**
*Rock Gnome | Fighter — Champion | Soldier*

College security guard. On shift when the complaint came in — Roondar grabbed him because he was the nearest warm body with a weapon. Stocky, efficient, and deeply unimpressed by most things. He's handled worse. Usually on purpose.

---

**Pezzi Sparklefingers**
*Rock Gnome | Wizard — School of Evocation | Sage*

Junior researcher in Schematics & Prototypes, two doors from Roondar's office. Studies mechanical-magical engineering and smells faintly of scorched paper. Was in the hallway when the mop handle started moving. She is professionally curious about this.

---

**Nax Flickertongue**
*Forest Gnome | Rogue — Thief | Criminal*

Freelance courier and occasional acquisitions specialist. Arrived at the college with a delivery and can't find the recipient or the exit — both problems equally troubling. Helping with the mop situation keeps her useful while she figures out both.

---

**Bramble Wicklow**
*Rock Gnome | Cleric — Knowledge Domain | Sage*

Senior archivist for the restricted collection. Knows where everything is filed, what it means, and what it implies. Borrowed by Roondar to take notes. Now taking notes on a mimic situation, which is considerably more interesting than the previous project.

---

**Lute Brasspin**
*Rock Gnome | Bard — College of Lore | Entertainer*

The college's unofficial social hub — knows everyone, has a story about everything, composes satirical ballads about college politics. Heard there was a mop incident. Came immediately. This is going in the next song.

---

**Tick Rootwhisper**
*Forest Gnome | Ranger — Hunter | Outlander*

Patrols the college's outer grounds and service corridors. Grew up outside Korranberg and notices things lifelong city-gnomes have learned to ignore. Was sweeping the 4th floor service corridor when Roondar's runner found her. A creature loose in a closet is her jurisdiction.

---

**Gimbal Brightwatch**
*Rock Gnome | Paladin — Oath of Devotion | Folk Hero*

Zilargo city guard assigned to the College of Drystone district. Short even by gnome standards, relentlessly cheerful, and has a habit of making promises she then feels personally obligated to keep. Roondar's problem is technically her problem.

---

**Mossy Thorngate**
*Forest Gnome | Druid — Circle of the Land | Hermit*

Naturalist visiting from a rural commune outside Korranberg. Studies how living things find their way into places they're not supposed to be. Was examining a specimen when the situation escalated. Stayed because the mimics are interesting.

---

**Vex Gloomtangle**
*Rock Gnome | Warlock — The Great Old One | Sage*

Graduate student in theoretical arcane cognition. At some point during his research, something noticed him noticing it. He made a pact. He doesn't fully understand the terms. He wants to know what made these mimics break free.

---

**Plink Stillwater**
*Rock Gnome | Monk — Way of the Open Hand | Acolyte*

Visiting from a small monastery to use the college's 4th floor resonance chamber. Serene to an extent that slightly unnerves people. Was meditating nearby when the commotion started. Found the mop situation philosophically interesting. Stayed.

---

**Brennan Ashvale**
*Human | Fighter — Battle Master | Soldier*

Professional soldier-for-hire who has been doing this long enough that almost nothing surprises him. Hired to escort someone to the college for a meeting that is running very late. Has been waiting in the hallway for two hours when Roondar's situation became apparent.

---

**Lyra Brightstring**
*Half-Elf | Bard — College of Lore | Entertainer*

Traveling journalist and performer working on a feature about the Library of Korranberg. Was touring the facilities with a college administrator when the screaming started. Took notes. The mop incident is a better story than the one she was sent to cover.

---

**Thordak Ironfoot**
*Mountain Dwarf | Barbarian — Path of the Berserker | Outlander*

Freight hauler who made a delivery to the college this morning and then couldn't find the exit. Has been in this building for four hours. Found Roondar's problem while trying to find the stairs. Stayed because someone needs to deal with it.

---

**Aelindra Moonwhisper**
*High Elf | Wizard — School of Evocation | Sage*

Visiting scholar from a prestigious elven academy on a research exchange. Studies how Zil gnomes integrate arcane theory with practical engineering. Was in the restricted stacks on a research visa when the incident started. Came to observe. Is now part of the observation.

---

**Zara Ashwood**
*Tiefling | Warlock — The Fiend | Criminal*

Independent contractor: unusual jobs, sensitive deliveries, arcane problem-solving for clients who prefer not to be named. Has a contact at the college she's waiting to meet. They are significantly late. Was passing time in the library when the commotion started.

---

**Ser Aldric Vane**
*Human | Paladin — Oath of Devotion | Noble*

Knight sworn to a minor noble house in Breland. Disciplined, courteous, takes his responsibilities seriously. Escorted a trade partner to a meeting at the college. The meeting never started. The commotion from the lower levels reached him two hours later.

---

**Pip Thistledown**
*Lightfoot Halfling | Rogue — Thief | Criminal*

Freelance acquisitions specialist — meaning they steal things, professionally, for clients who ask no questions. Hired to retrieve a document from the restricted archives. Found it. Then the lockdown began. The document is sewn into the coat lining. This is a secondary concern.

---

**Gorix Stonescale**
*Dragonborn (Blue) | Fighter — Champion | Soldier*

Professional soldier turned private security. Posted at the college as perimeter security for a visiting delegation. Shift ended. Delegation hadn't left. Went inside to check on them. Now very inside.

---

**Gruksha Bonecrusher**
*Half-Orc | Barbarian — Path of the Totem Warrior | Outlander*

Bounty recovery specialist. Three days tracking a bail jumper. The trail led here. Was inside the college making quiet inquiries when the lockdown started. Her target is still in the building somewhere. So is whatever is making that noise. Both problems, one location.

---

**Sylvara Dawnwind**
*Wood Elf | Ranger — Hunter | Outlander*

Tracker and scout from the Eldeen Reaches. Noticed the morning freight delivery had wrong signs — sounds, smells, subtle movement in crates that didn't match the manifest — before anyone inside the building did. Entered to investigate. Was inside when the lockdown started. Annoyingly correct.

---

**Brother Caelan**
*Human | Cleric — Trickery Domain | Acolyte*

Traveling cleric of the Traveler — deity of change, deception, and clever solutions. Arrived with a pilgrim group, slipped away to the library instead. Cheerful about this in a way that makes people slightly nervous. Has been here ever since and does not regret it.

---

**Seraphina Ember**
*Tiefling | Sorcerer — Draconic Bloodline (Fire) | Sage*

Researcher specializing in inherited magical bloodlines — specifically what happens when tiefling and draconic heritage interact. Deeply absorbed in a manuscript when the incident started. Finished the paragraph first. Then went to help.

---

**Fen Cobbleskip**
*Stout Halfling | Monk — Way of the Open Hand | Acolyte*

Monk who left his order to travel and study the intersection of physical discipline and academic learning. Given a library pass to research ki manipulation texts. Came to take notes. Took a lot of notes. Still taking notes. This counts as field research.

---

**Rook Nightfall**
*Human | Rogue — Assassin | Spy*

Intelligence work: surveillance, information extraction, the occasional delivery of unpleasant news in person. In the college on a job he cannot discuss — something involving the academic correspondence records. The lockdown is an unforeseen complication. He has not decided yet whether it's connected.

---

**Lady Isolde Ferwyn**
*Half-Elf | Paladin — Oath of the Ancients | Noble*

Paladin sworn to an ancient tradition concerned with preserving light, beauty, and living things. In Korranberg on a diplomatic mission for a Brelish noble house. Meeting was postponed. Came to the college library to spend the time usefully. The mimic incident has become part of her definition of useful.
