## Who You Are

Mossy is a naturalist visiting from a rural commune outside Korranberg. She studies the intersection of natural and constructed environments — how living things find their way into places they're not supposed to be. She communicates better with plants than with most people, has strong opinions about what constitutes a healthy ecosystem, and finds the College of Drystone's basement deeply concerning from a biological standpoint.

**Why you're here:** You were examining a specimen in the Schematics collection when the situation escalated. You stayed because the mimics are interesting.

## In a Fight

**Produce Flame** *(Cantrip — unlimited)*
Create a flame in your hand (harmless, or hurl it). Hurl it: roll d20 + **4**, range 30 ft. On a hit: **1d8 fire** damage.

**Shillelagh** *(Cantrip)*
Touch your staff or club. For 1 minute, it counts as magical, attacks use your WIS modifier: d20 + **4**, damage **1d6 + 2** bludgeoning. Better than hitting with STR.

**Entangle** *(1st-level spell)*
Plants erupt in a 20 ft. square within 90 ft. All creatures in the area make a STR save vs. DC **12** or are **Restrained** (can't move, attacks against them have advantage). Concentration, 1 minute.

**Thunderwave** *(1st-level spell)*
Everything within 15 ft. makes a CON save vs. DC **12**. On fail: **2d8** thunder damage and pushed 10 ft. On success: half damage.

---

**Wild Shape** *(twice per rest)*
Transform into a beast you've seen. At this level: CR 1/4 or lower (Wolf, Giant Spider, etc.). You keep your WIS, INT, and CHA, but use the beast's physical stats and HP. You revert when reduced to 0 HP in beast form, dropping to 1 HP.
*A Wolf has AC 13, 11 HP, speed 40 ft., and can knock enemies prone on a hit.*

**Natural Recovery** *(once per long rest)*
During a short rest, recover up to **2 levels worth** of spell slots. A 1st-level and a 1st-level, or a 2nd-level.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Wisdom increases from 15 to **17 (+3)**.

- Spell Save DC: 12 → **13**
- Spell Attack Bonus: +4 → **+5**
- HP increases by **6** (new total: **27**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Endurance:** CON 14 → **16 (+3)**
- HP increases by **8** instead (new total: **29**)
- CON saves improve — critical for maintaining the concentration spells that are your main combat tools

**Option C — Feat: War Caster**
You are trained to cast spells while being hit.
- **Advantage on concentration saves** when you take damage — protects Entangle, Spike Growth, and other concentration spells
- You can cast spells as opportunity attacks when enemies move past you
