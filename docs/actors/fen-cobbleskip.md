## Who You Are

Fen is a monk who left his order to travel and learn — particularly interested in the intersection of physical discipline and academic study. He is thoughtful, methodical, and has a habit of thinking out loud in ways that are either insightful or extremely irritating depending on your patience. He is also significantly harder to hurt than he looks, which surprises people routinely.

**Why you're here:** You were given a brief library pass to study texts on ki manipulation and early monastic orders. You came to take notes. You took a lot of notes. You are still taking notes. This counts as field research.

## In a Fight

**Unarmed Strike:** Roll d20 + **4**. On a hit: **1d4 + 2** bludgeoning damage.

**Shortsword:** Roll d20 + **4**. On a hit: **1d6 + 2** piercing damage.

**Martial Arts:** When you attack with your monk weapons or an unarmed strike, you can make one additional unarmed strike as a bonus action (no ki required).

---

**Ki Points — 3 per short or long rest**
A pool of inner power. Spend ki to use the following:

**Flurry of Blows** *(1 ki — Bonus Action, after you attack)*
Make **two additional unarmed strikes**. Three total attacks in a turn.

**Patient Defense** *(1 ki — Bonus Action)*
Take the **Dodge** action as a bonus action. Attacks against you have disadvantage; you have advantage on DEX saves.

**Step of the Wind** *(1 ki — Bonus Action)*
Take the **Disengage or Dash** action as a bonus action. Jump distance doubles.

**Stunning Strike** *(1 ki — when you hit)*
Force the target to make a CON save vs. DC **13**. On fail: **Stunned** until end of your next turn — they can't move, lose reactions, attacks against them have advantage, and they fail STR and DEX saves.

---

**Open Hand Technique** *(on Flurry of Blows hits)*
When hitting with Flurry of Blows, apply one of these effects (no save):

- Target is knocked **prone** (lying down)
- Target is **pushed 15 ft.** away
- Target **cannot take reactions** until end of your next turn

## Outside a Fight

**Acrobatics +4:** Tumble, balance, move through tight spaces, escape grapples.
**Insight +5:** You read people well. Body language, intention, hesitation.
**Stealth +4:** Halflings move quietly. Monks even more so.

**Halfling Nimbleness:** Move through the space of creatures larger than you.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Dexterity increases from 15 to **17 (+3)**.

- Attack bonus: +4 → **+5**
- Damage bonus: +2 → **+3**
- AC: 15 → **16** (Unarmored Defense: 10 + DEX 3 + WIS 3)
- HP increases by **7** (new total: **31**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Insight:** WIS 16 → **18 (+4)**
- Unarmored AC: 15 → **16** (10 + DEX 2 + WIS 4)
- Stunning Strike DC: 13 → **14** — even harder to resist
- HP same as the recommended upgrade

**Option C — Feat: Lucky**
Fortune bends toward you — and halfling luck means you already ignore some bad rolls, so this stacks nicely.
- Gain **3 luck points** per long rest (separate from your racial Lucky feature)
- Spend a point to **reroll any attack roll, ability check, or saving throw** (yours or targeting you) and choose either result
