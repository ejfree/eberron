## Who You Are

Pip is a freelance acquisitions specialist — meaning they steal things for money, professionally, for clients who prefer to ask no questions. Pip is quick, adaptable, and has a cheerful relationship with morally ambiguous situations. They have been in worse spots than this. They will tell you this, with some frequency, until it becomes true.

**Why you're here:** You were hired to retrieve a specific document from the college's restricted archives. You found it. Then the lockdown began. You are now technically in possession of property belonging to the college, which is a secondary concern given the mimics, but still worth keeping in mind.

## In a Fight

**Shortsword:** Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** piercing damage.

**Hand Crossbow** *(range 30/120 ft.)*
Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** piercing damage.

**Sneak Attack** *(+2d6 damage — once per turn)*
Whenever you have **advantage** on an attack OR when an ally is within 5 ft. of your target (and you're not at disadvantage), add **2d6** to your damage. This applies once per turn, on your turn.

---

**Cunning Action** *(Bonus Action — every turn)*
Choose one free action: **Dash** (double movement), **Disengage** (no opportunity attacks when leaving), or **Hide** (attempt to vanish from sight).

**Fast Hands** *(Thief feature)*
Your bonus action can also be used to: use thieves' tools, palm or produce an item, use a consumable, or make a Help action.

## Outside a Fight

**Stealth +7:** Moving unseen is your most reliable skill.
**Sleight of Hand +7:** Pick pockets, palming objects, anything requiring quick or subtle hands.
**Deception +4:** Lying is a professional competency.
**Thieves' Tools (proficient):** Open locks, disable basic traps.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Dexterity increases from 17 to **19 (+4)**.

- Attack bonus: +5 → **+6**
- Damage bonus: +3 → **+4**
- AC: 15 → **16**
- HP increases by **6** (new total: **27**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Silver Tongue:** CHA 15 → **17 (+3)**
- Deception: +4 → **+5**
- Better at talking your way out once you've been caught
- HP same as the recommended upgrade

**Option C — Feat: Lucky**
Fortune bends toward you — and you already have halfling luck, so you get even more.
- Gain **3 luck points** per long rest (different from your racial Lucky feature)
- Spend a point to **reroll any attack roll, ability check, or saving throw** (yours or targeting you) and choose either result
