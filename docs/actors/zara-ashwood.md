## Who You Are

Zara is an independent contractor — she does jobs that are unusual, sensitive, or require someone who is comfortable with both the arcane and the disreputable. She made a pact with a fiendish entity at a point in her life she describes as "complicated," and she has spent the years since turning that arrangement into a practical toolkit. She is focused, professionally calm, and has a dry sense of humor about situations that would unsettle most people.

**Why you're here:** You have a contact at the college you're waiting to meet. They are significantly late. You were passing time in the library when the commotion started.

## In a Fight

**Eldritch Blast** *(Cantrip — unlimited, range 120 ft.)*
Roll d20 + **5** to hit. On a hit: **1d10 + 3** force damage.
Your primary attack. Reliable, long range, no resources spent.

**Hex** *(1st-level spell, Bonus Action — cast at 2nd level)*
Mark a target within 90 ft. You deal **+1d6 necrotic** every time Eldritch Blast hits it. Choose one ability — the target has **disadvantage** on checks with that ability. Concentration, up to 3 hours at 2nd level.

**Hunger of Hadar** *(2nd-level spell)*
30 ft. sphere of cold blackness centered on a point within 150 ft. Creatures inside: blinded; **2d6 cold** at start of each turn inside; DEX save vs. DC **13** or **2d6 acid** at end of each turn. You cannot see into it. Concentration, 1 minute.

---

**Pact Magic — Short Rest Recovery**
2 spell slots, both cast at 2nd level. Return on a **short rest**, not just long rest.

**Devil's Sight** *(Invocation)*
You can see normally in magical darkness (including Hunger of Hadar). You can use this to be the only one who can see.

**Dark One's Blessing**
When you reduce a creature to 0 HP, gain **temporary HP** equal to CHA mod + warlock level = **3 + 3 = 6**.

**Tiefling Magic:**
- *Thaumaturgy* (cantrip): Minor supernatural effects
- *Hellish Rebuke* (1/day): When damaged, reaction to deal **2d10 fire** back at attacker (DEX save vs. DC **13** for half)
- *Darkness* (1/day): Magical darkness in a 15 ft. sphere — you can see through it, most others can't

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Charisma increases from 17 to **19 (+4)**.

- Spell Save DC: 13 → **14**
- Eldritch Blast attack: +5 → **+6**, damage bonus: +3 → **+4**
- HP increases by **6** (new total: **27**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Endurance:** CON 13 → **15 (+2)**
- HP increases by **7** instead (new total: **28**)
- More durable when enemies try to break your Hex concentration

**Option C — Feat: War Caster**
You are trained to cast spells under fire.
- **Advantage on concentration saves** when you take damage — protects Hex, which doubles your damage output
- You can cast spells (including Eldritch Blast) as opportunity attacks when enemies move past you
