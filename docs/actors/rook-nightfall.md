## Who You Are

Rook does intelligence work. Surveillance, information extraction, the occasional delivery of unpleasant news in person. He works through intermediaries and is careful about what he puts in writing. He is quiet, methodical, and has a gift for being in places without anyone remembering they saw him. He is very good at his job. He would prefer you didn't know what his job is.

**Why you're here:** You are here on a job you cannot discuss. The assignment involved the college's academic correspondence records — access, not removal. The lockdown is an unforeseen complication. You have not decided yet whether it is connected to your assignment.

## In a Fight

**Shortsword:** Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** piercing damage.

**Hand Crossbow** *(range 30/120 ft.)*
Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** piercing damage.

**Sneak Attack** *(+2d6 damage — once per turn)*
Whenever you have **advantage** on an attack OR when an ally is within 5 ft. of your target (and you're not at disadvantage), add **2d6** to damage. Once per turn.

---

**Cunning Action** *(Bonus Action — every turn)*
**Dash** (double speed), **Disengage** (no opportunity attacks), or **Hide** (attempt to vanish).

**Assassinate** *(Assassin feature)*
Two conditions, both powerful:

1. **Surprise:** If your target hasn't acted yet in combat (you surprised them), your attack roll is made with **advantage**.
2. **Critical Hit on surprised targets:** Any hit against a surprised creature is automatically a critical hit — roll all damage dice twice.

*Getting the drop on something means you deal double damage dice plus 2d6 Sneak Attack, also doubled. Plan accordingly.*

**Infiltration Expertise** *(Assassin feature)*
You can create false identities — forged documents, assumed personas — that hold up to scrutiny for extended periods.

## Outside a Fight

**Stealth +7:** You are exceptionally good at not being noticed. This is your primary professional tool.
**Deception +5:** You maintain cover, tell convincing stories, manage what people believe.
**Investigation +4:** You find things people are hiding. Records, patterns, inconsistencies.
**Insight +3:** You know when people are lying. Hazard of the profession.
**Sleight of Hand +5:** Palming items, picking pockets, unseen manipulation.
**Thieves' Tools (proficient):** Locks, basic traps — standard tradecraft.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Dexterity increases from 16 to **18 (+4)**.

- Attack bonus: +5 → **+6**
- Damage bonus: +3 → **+4**
- AC: 14 → **15**
- HP increases by **6** (new total: **28**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Analysis:** INT 14 → **16 (+3)**
- Investigation: +4 → **+5**
- Better at finding what people are hiding — correspondence, hidden compartments, forged documents
- HP same as the recommended upgrade

**Option C — Feat: Alert**
You are never caught off guard — and going first enables your Assassinate ability.
- Permanent **+5 to Initiative** — you almost always act in the first round, before targets have acted
- You **cannot be surprised** — which matters for Assassinate
- Hidden enemies do not gain advantage on attacks against you
