## Who You Are

Aelindra is a visiting scholar from a prestigious elven academy, here on a research exchange. She studies applied elemental magic and has a particular interest in how Zil gnomes have integrated arcane theory with practical engineering. She is precise, a little formal, and takes notes on everything. She is also an exceptional battle mage when the situation demands it.

**Why you're here:** You were in the restricted stacks on a research visa when the incident started. You came to observe. You are now part of the observation.

## In a Fight

**Fire Bolt** *(Cantrip — unlimited, range 120 ft.)*
Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **2d10** fire damage. Your main ranged attack.

**Magic Missile** *(1st-level spell)*
Three darts automatically hit one or more targets — no roll needed. Each: **1d4 + 1** force damage.

**Scorching Ray** *(2nd-level spell)*
Three separate rays, each: roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **2d6** fire damage per ray. Spread them or focus them.

**Thunderwave** *(1st-level spell)*
Everything within 15 ft. makes CON save vs. DC **13**. Fail: **2d8** thunder damage + pushed 10 ft. Success: half. Emergency melee escape.

---

**Sculpt Spells**
Protect up to **2 allies** in any evocation spell's area — they automatically succeed on saves and take no damage.

**Shield** *(1st-level spell — Reaction)*
When hit, cast this to add **+5 AC** until your next turn. The hit might miss.

**Misty Step** *(2nd-level spell — Bonus Action)*
Teleport up to 30 ft. to a spot you can see. Escape, reposition, close distance.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Intelligence increases from 16 to **18 (+4)**.

- Spell Save DC: 13 → **14**
- Spell Attack Bonus: +5 → **+6**
- HP increases by **5** (new total: **22**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Resilience:** CON 12 → **14 (+2)**
- HP increases by **6** instead (new total: **23**)
- CON saves improve — important for maintaining concentration on battlefield control spells

**Option C — Feat: War Caster**
You are trained to cast spells under fire.
- **Advantage on concentration saves** when you take damage — protects Misty Step and any concentration spells you pick up
- You can cast spells as opportunity attacks when enemies move past you
- You can use your arcane focus even with full hands
