## Who You Are

Zibble works security for the College of Drystone. He has seen a lot of strange things pass through this building and has developed a philosophy: ask questions later, stop the thing first. He is stocky, efficient, and deeply unimpressed by most things. He has been unimpressed by a lot of mimics.

**Why you're here:** You were on shift when the complaint came in. Roondar grabbed you because you were the nearest warm body with a weapon.

## In a Fight

**Longsword:** Roll d20 + **4**. On a hit: roll **1d8 + 2** slashing damage.
*On a natural 19 or 20 — a critical hit — roll the damage dice twice.*

**Handaxe (throw up to 20 ft.):** Roll d20 + **4**. On a hit: **1d6 + 2** slashing damage.

---

**Second Wind** *(Bonus Action — once per short or long rest)*
Roll 1d10 and add 3. You regain that many HP. Use this when you're hurting.

**Action Surge** *(once per short or long rest)*
Take a full extra action on your turn. Use this for two attacks in one round when it matters.

**Improved Critical**
Your attacks score a critical hit on a 19 or 20, not just a 20. Roll damage dice twice on a crit.

## Outside a Fight

You are a professional. You know how institutions work and how to talk to guards, soldiers, and administrators. You carry a badge.

**Intimidation +1:** Useful when you need someone to back down fast.
**Perception +2:** You notice things that don't fit.
**Athletics +4:** Climbing, jumping, shoving, grappling — you're good at all of it.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Strength increases from 15 to **17 (+3)**.

- Longsword attack bonus: +4 → **+5**
- Longsword damage bonus: +2 → **+3**
- HP increases by **8** (new total: **36**)

*Write the new numbers on your sheet when Roondar sends you to the basement.*

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Endurance:** CON 14 → **16 (+3)**
- HP increases by **9** instead (new total: **35**)
- You stay standing longer in the front line

**Option C — Feat: Alert**
You are never caught off guard.
- Permanent **+5 to Initiative** — you almost always go first
- You **cannot be surprised**
- Hidden enemies do not gain advantage on attacks against you
