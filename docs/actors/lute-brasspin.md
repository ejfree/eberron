## Who You Are

Lute is the College of Drystone's unofficial social hub — the gnome who knows everyone, has a story about everything, and is somehow always present when something interesting happens. He plays a small lute (obviously), composes satirical ballads about college politics, and has an absolutely encyclopedic knowledge of who is sleeping with whom in the administration building. The Trust has considered recruiting him. They decided he is more useful not knowing that.

**Why you're here:** You heard there was a mop incident. You came immediately. This is going in the next song.

## In a Fight

**Vicious Mockery** *(Cantrip — unlimited, range 60 ft.)*
Shout creative insults at one creature. They make a WIS save vs. DC **12**. On a fail: **1d4 psychic** damage AND they have **disadvantage** on their next attack roll. No attack roll needed from you.

**Dissonant Whispers** *(1st-level spell, range 60 ft.)*
One creature makes a WIS save vs. DC **12**. On a fail: **3d6 psychic** damage and they must use their reaction to flee. On success: half damage, no flee.

**Thunderwave** *(1st-level spell)*
Everything within 15 ft. must make a CON save vs. DC **12**. On fail: **2d8 thunder** and pushed 10 ft. On success: half damage. Great for clearing space.

---

**Bardic Inspiration** *(Bonus Action — 2 uses per long rest)*
Give a **d6** to a teammate. They can add it to any d20 roll within the next 10 minutes — an attack, a saving throw, or an ability check. Hand these out early.

**Cutting Words** *(Reaction)*
When an enemy makes an attack roll, ability check, or damage roll, roll your **d6** and subtract it from their result. Can turn a hit into a miss.

**Healing Word** *(1st-level spell, Bonus Action)*
An ally within 60 ft. regains **1d4 + 2** HP. Use your bonus action so you can attack or do something else in the same turn.
