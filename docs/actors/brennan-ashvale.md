## Who You Are

Brennan is a professional soldier-for-hire who has been doing this long enough that almost nothing surprises him. He is methodical, competent, and quietly observant. He fought in a few of the smaller conflicts left over from the Last War, decided that working for causes was less reliable than working for contracts, and has been doing private security work since. He ended up in Korranberg on a job he can't discuss. The college visit was not part of the brief.

**Why you're here:** You were hired to escort someone to the college for a meeting. The meeting is running very late. You've been waiting in the hallway for two hours when Roondar's situation became apparent.

## In a Fight

**Longsword:** Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d8 + 3** slashing damage.

---

**Second Wind** *(Bonus Action — once per short or long rest)*
Regain **1d10 + 3** HP.

**Action Surge** *(once per short or long rest)*
Take a full extra action this turn. Use this for two attacks in a critical moment.

**Superiority Dice — 4d8 per long rest**
You have 4 dice (each a d8) that fuel your Battle Master maneuvers. Track them below:
*[ ] [ ] [ ] [ ]*

**Maneuver: Trip Attack**
When you hit with an attack, spend 1 die. Add **1d8** to the damage. The target makes a STR save vs. DC **13** or falls **Prone** (lying down — melee attacks against it have advantage).

**Maneuver: Disarming Attack**
When you hit, spend 1 die. Add **1d8** damage. Target makes STR save vs. DC **13** or drops one held item.

**Maneuver: Commander's Strike**
Spend 1 die and use your bonus action. Give one ally a free attack. They add the **d8** to the damage roll.

**Maneuver: Menacing Attack**
When you hit, spend 1 die. Add **1d8** damage. Target makes WIS save vs. DC **13** or is **Frightened** of you (disadvantage on attacks against you) until end of next turn.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Strength increases from 16 to **18 (+4)**.

- Longsword attack: +5 → **+6**
- Damage bonus: +3 → **+4**
- HP increases by **8** (new total: **36**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Resilience:** CON 14 → **16 (+3)**
- HP increases by **9** instead (new total: **37**)
- Better for absorbing punishment in extended engagements

**Option C — Feat: Sentinel**
You lock down the battlefield.
- When you hit with an opportunity attack, the target's speed drops to **0** — they are stopped cold
- Enemies provoke opportunity attacks from you **even when they use Disengage**
- When an enemy attacks someone other than you within 5 ft. of you, you can use your reaction to attack them
