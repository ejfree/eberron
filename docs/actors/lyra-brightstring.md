## Who You Are

Lyra is a traveling journalist and performer — technically a bard, practically a very musical investigative reporter. She is working on a feature about the Library of Korranberg for a periodical based in Sharn. She has credentials, a letter of introduction, and absolutely no intention of writing only about what she was sent to cover. The mop incident is a better story.

**Why you're here:** You were touring the facilities with a college administrator when the screaming started. You took notes.

## In a Fight

**Vicious Mockery** *(Cantrip — unlimited, range 60 ft.)*
Deliver withering criticism. Target makes WIS save vs. DC **13**. On fail: **1d4 psychic** damage and **disadvantage** on their next attack. No attack roll needed.

**Dissonant Whispers** *(1st-level spell, range 60 ft.)*
Target makes WIS save vs. DC **13**. On fail: **3d6 psychic** damage and must flee on their reaction. On success: half damage.

**Hypnotic Pattern** *(3rd-level spell — your best combat spell)*
Create a swirling pattern in a 30 ft. cube within 120 ft. Every creature in the area makes a WIS save vs. DC **13**. Those who fail are **Charmed and Incapacitated** — they do nothing until hit or shaken. Concentration, 1 minute.

---

**Bardic Inspiration** *(Bonus Action — 3 uses per long rest)*
Give a teammate a **d6**. They add it to any one roll in the next 10 minutes.

**Cutting Words** *(Reaction)*
When an enemy rolls for an attack, save, or skill check: roll a d6 and subtract it from their result. Can turn a hit into a miss.

**Healing Word** *(Bonus Action, 1st-level spell)*
Ally within 60 ft. regains **1d4 + 3** HP.
