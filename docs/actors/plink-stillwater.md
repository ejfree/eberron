## Who You Are

Plink is visiting from a small monastery outside Korranberg to use the college's meditation facilities — specifically a resonance chamber on the 4th floor that vibrates at a frequency useful for certain contemplative practices. She is serene to an extent that slightly unnerves people, deeply curious about everything she encounters, and capable of hitting a mimic six times in a single round if the circumstances demand it. The circumstances have demanded it.

**Why you're here:** You were meditating nearby when the commotion started. You investigated. You found the mop situation philosophically interesting. You stayed.

## In a Fight

**Unarmed Strike:** Roll d20 + **4**. On a hit: **1d4 + 2** bludgeoning damage.

**Shortsword:** Roll d20 + **4**. On a hit: **1d6 + 2** piercing damage.

---

**Martial Arts — Bonus Attack**
After you make an attack with a shortsword or unarmed strike, you get a **free bonus unarmed strike** as your bonus action. Always take this.

**Flurry of Blows** *(Bonus Action — costs 1 Ki)*
Instead of your free bonus strike, make **2 unarmed strikes** as your bonus action. That's 3 attacks in one turn.

**Patient Defense** *(Bonus Action — costs 1 Ki)*
Take the **Dodge** action as a bonus action. All attacks against you have disadvantage until your next turn.

**Step of the Wind** *(Bonus Action — costs 1 Ki)*
Take the **Dash or Disengage** action as a bonus action. Your jump distance doubles.

**Open Hand Technique**
When you land a hit with Flurry of Blows, you can:
- Force a DEX save (DC **12**) or knock the target **Prone** (lying down — attacks on it from melee have advantage)
- Force a STR save (DC **12**) or push the target **15 ft. away**
- Forbid the target from using reactions until its next turn

**Stunning Strike** *(costs 1 Ki)*
After hitting with a melee attack, the target makes a CON save vs. DC **12** or is **Stunned** until the end of your next turn. Stunned: can't act, attacks against it have advantage. Very powerful.

**Deflect Missiles** *(Reaction)*
When hit by a ranged attack, reduce the damage by **1d10 + 4**. If reduced to 0, you catch the projectile and can throw it back (d20 + **4**, 1d4 + 2 damage).

## Outside a Fight

**Insight +4:** You read people well.
**Stealth +4:** You move quietly.
**Stillness of Mind:** As an action, you can end one charmed or frightened condition on yourself.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Dexterity increases from 14 to **16 (+3)**.

- Attack bonus: +4 → **+5**
- Damage bonus: +2 → **+3**
- AC: 14 → **15** (10 + DEX 3 + WIS 2)
- HP increases by **7** (new total: **31**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Intuition:** WIS 14 → **16 (+3)**
- Unarmored AC: 14 → **15** (10 + DEX 2 + WIS 3)
- Stunning Strike DC: 12 → **13** — harder for enemies to shake off
- HP same as the recommended upgrade

**Option C — Feat: Alert**
You are never caught off guard.
- Permanent **+5 to Initiative** — act before nearly every enemy
- You **cannot be surprised**
- Hidden enemies do not gain advantage on attacks against you
