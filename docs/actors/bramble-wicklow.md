## Who You Are

Bramble is the college's senior archivist for the restricted collection. He knows where everything is filed, what it means, and — more importantly — what it implies. He is methodical, patient, and has opinions about document storage that he will share at length. He is also the only person in the building who has successfully mapped the 4th floor.

**Why you're here:** Roondar borrowed you from the archives to take notes. You are now taking notes on a mimic situation, which is more interesting than the previous project.

## In a Fight

**Sacred Flame** *(Cantrip — unlimited, range 60 ft.)*
Target must make a DEX save vs. DC **12** or take **1d8 radiant** damage. Ignores cover.

**Guiding Bolt** *(1st-level spell)*
Roll d20 + **4** to hit, range 120 ft. On a hit: **4d6 radiant** damage AND the next attack against that target has advantage. Big opener.

**Spiritual Weapon** *(2nd-level spell, Bonus Action)*
Summon a floating magical weapon. Each turn you can use a bonus action to attack with it: d20 + **4**, **1d8 + 2** force damage. Lasts 1 minute and doesn't require concentration.

---

**Healing Word** *(1st-level spell, Bonus Action)*
Touch range. Target regains **1d4 + 2** HP. Use your bonus action so you can also do something else.

**Cure Wounds** *(1st-level spell, Action)*
Touch range. Target regains **1d8 + 2** HP.

**Channel Divinity — Knowledge of the Ages** *(once per rest)*
Name any skill or tool. For the next 10 minutes, you are proficient in it (+4 bonus). Useful for cracking any problem.

**Channel Divinity — Read Thoughts** *(once per rest)*
One creature within 60 ft. makes a WIS save vs. DC **12**. On a fail, you read their surface thoughts for 1 minute and can ask questions. Very useful in social situations.

## Outside a Fight

You are the smartest person in almost any room, and you can prove it.

**Arcana +6 / History +6:** These are your superpowers. You know things. You recognize things.
**Insight +4:** You can tell when someone is lying or hiding something.
**Investigation +4** *(via Knowledge of the Ages)*: With your Channel Divinity, you become instantly proficient in anything you're not already good at.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Wisdom increases from 15 to **17 (+3)**.

- Spell Save DC: 12 → **13**
- Healing spells: +2 → **+3** to healing rolls
- HP increases by **7** (new total: **31**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Scholarship:** INT 15 → **17 (+3)**
- Arcana and History expertise: +6 → **+7** each
- Better at recalling magical lore — very useful in a library full of secrets
- HP same as the recommended upgrade

**Option C — Feat: War Caster**
You are trained to cast spells under combat conditions.
- **Advantage on concentration saves** when you take damage
- You can cast spells as opportunity attacks when enemies move past you
- You can use your holy symbol even while holding weapons and shield
