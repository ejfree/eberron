## Who You Are

Tick patrols the college's outer grounds and the service corridors that most staff pretend don't exist. She grew up outside Korranberg and came to the city late, which means she notices things that lifelong city-gnomes have learned to ignore. She tracks things. She is very quiet when she wants to be.

**Why you're here:** You were doing a sweep of the 4th floor service corridor when Roondar's runner found you. A creature loose in a closet is your jurisdiction.

## In a Fight

**Shortbow** *(range 80 ft. / 320 ft.)*: Roll d20 + **7**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** piercing damage.
*The +2 Archery fighting style is already included in this bonus.*

**Shortsword** *(melee)*: Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** piercing damage.

---

**Colossus Slayer** *(once per turn)*
When you hit a creature that is below its maximum HP, you deal an extra **1d8** damage. Aim at the wounded target.

**Hunter's Mark** *(1st-level spell, Bonus Action)*
Mark one enemy as your quarry. You deal **+1d6 damage** whenever you hit it. If it dies, you can move the mark to another creature as a bonus action. Concentration, up to 1 hour.

**Favored Enemy — Monstrosities**
You have advantage on Survival and Perception checks to track monstrosities (mimics are monstrosities). You also know a bit about them.

**Speak with Small Beasts** *(Forest Gnome)*
You can communicate simple ideas to small animals. Useful for information.

## Outside a Fight

You are the party's eyes and ears in open terrain — and in unfamiliar buildings.

**Perception +6:** Very hard to surprise you.
**Survival +6:** Track creatures, navigate, find what's out of place.
**Stealth +5:** Move silently when you need to.
**Natural Explorer — Urban:** You can't get lost in a city. You find twice as much food and water when foraging (rarely relevant, but still). You also move at full speed while tracking.
