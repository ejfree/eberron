## Who You Are

Ser Aldric is a knight sworn to a minor noble house in Breland. He is disciplined, courteous, and takes his responsibilities seriously. He carries himself with quiet authority — not from arrogance, but from years of knowing that someone has to be the one who makes the call. He believes in doing things properly. He is also aware that "properly" sometimes means improvising in a library basement.

**Why you're here:** You escorted a member of your house's trading partners to a meeting at the college. The meeting never started. You've been waiting in an antechamber for two hours when the commotion from the lower levels reached you.

## In a Fight

**Longsword:** Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d8 + 3** slashing damage.

**Divine Smite** *(when you hit — your most powerful move)*
After you see a hit land, spend a spell slot to add radiant damage:
- 1st-level slot: **+2d8** radiant damage
- Even more effective against undead or fiends: **+3d8** instead.
You decide after the hit lands — no gambling needed.

**Sacred Weapon** *(Channel Divinity — 1 use per rest)*
Bonus action. For 1 minute, add **+2** to all attack rolls with your longsword. The weapon glows with holy light.

**Turn the Unholy** *(Channel Divinity — 1 use per rest)*
Hold up your holy symbol. Each undead and fiend within 30 ft. must make a WIS save vs. DC **12** or be turned — they flee and cannot approach you for 1 minute.

---

**Lay on Hands** *(healing pool — 15 HP total per long rest)*
Touch a creature to restore HP from your pool (1 point = 1 HP). Or spend 5 points to cure one disease or one poison. Track the pool with a number.

**Bless** *(1st-level spell — Concentration)*
Up to 3 creatures within 30 ft. add **+1d4** to every attack roll and saving throw. Lasts up to 1 minute.

**Cure Wounds** *(1st-level spell)*
Touch a creature. They regain **1d8 + 2** HP.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Strength increases from 16 to **18 (+4)**.

- Longsword attack: +5 → **+6**
- Damage bonus: +3 → **+4**
- HP increases by **8** (new total: **34**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Presence:** CHA 15 → **17 (+3)**
- Spell Save DC: 12 → **13**
- Sacred Weapon attack bonus: +2 → **+3**
- Better in negotiations and social situations
- HP same as the recommended upgrade

**Option C — Feat: Tough**
You are significantly harder to kill.
- Gain **+2 HP per level**, applied retroactively — **+8 HP** on top of your normal level-up gain (new total: **42**)
- Paladins in the front line appreciate the extra buffer
