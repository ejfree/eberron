## Who You Are

Thordak is a freight hauler who made a delivery to the College of Drystone this morning — large crates, heavy, no questions asked, standard contract. He signed the paperwork, handed off the goods, and then couldn't find the exit. He has now been in this building for four hours. He is not angry. He is simply committed to resolving this.

**Why you're here:** You found Roondar's problem because you were trying to find the stairs. You stayed because someone needs to deal with this and you might as well be useful.

## In a Fight

**Greataxe (2-handed):** Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d12 + 3** slashing damage.
*(Drop the shield when you Rage — you want the big axe.)*

**Handaxe (throw up to 20 ft.):** Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** slashing damage.

---

**Rage** *(Bonus Action — 3 uses per long rest)*
Enter a furious combat state for 1 minute:
- All your melee damage rolls get **+2**
- You have **resistance** to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage (take half)
- Advantage on STR checks and saves
- You cannot cast spells or concentrate on spells (doesn't matter — you have none)

*Rage ends if you don't attack or take damage by end of your turn.*

**Reckless Attack**
On your turn, before rolling your first attack: declare Reckless Attack. Your attack has **advantage** (roll twice, keep higher). BUT attacks against you also have advantage until your next turn. Worth it when you need to hit.

**Frenzy** *(during Rage only)*
When you Rage, you can choose to frenzy. You get an extra attack as a bonus action each turn. After the Rage ends, you gain 1 level of Exhaustion (disadvantage on ability checks until you rest). Use for big fights only.

**Danger Sense**
Advantage on DEX saves against effects you can see (traps, spells with visible effects).

## Outside a Fight

**Athletics +5:** You are the strongest person in most rooms. Climbing, lifting, shoving, grappling.
**Intimidation +4:** Your size and manner do most of the work.
**Dwarven Stonecunning:** Whenever you make a History check related to stonework, add double proficiency (+4).

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Constitution increases from 15 to **17 (+3)**.

- Unarmored AC: 13 → **14** (with shield: 16)
- HP increases by **9** (new total: **41**)
- Rage damage bonus remains +2 (increases at level 9)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Fury:** STR 17 → **19 (+4)**
- Greataxe attack: +5 → **+6**
- Damage bonus: +3 → **+4** (rage still adds +2 on top)
- HP same as the recommended upgrade (**41**)

**Option C — Feat: Tough**
You are even harder to kill than you already are.
- Gain **+2 HP per level**, retroactive — **+8 HP** on top of your normal level-up gain (new total: **49**)
- Barbarians with Tough are extraordinarily durable
