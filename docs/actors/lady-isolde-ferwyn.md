## Who You Are

Lady Isolde is a paladin sworn to an ancient elven tradition — an oath that predates most kingdoms, concerned not with law or conquest but with the preservation of light, beauty, and living things. She is gracious, composed, and radiates a quiet authority that has nothing to do with her title. She takes the title seriously. She takes the oath more seriously. She is also, in a practical sense, very hard to kill.

**Why you're here:** You are in Korranberg on a diplomatic mission for a minor Brelish noble family with trade interests in Zilargo. The meeting was postponed this morning. You came to the college library to spend the time usefully. The mimic incident has become part of your definition of "useful."

## In a Fight

**Longsword:** Roll d20 + **4**. On a hit: **1d8 + 2** slashing damage.

**Divine Smite** *(when you hit — your most powerful option)*
After seeing a hit land, expend a spell slot:
- 1st-level slot: **+2d8 radiant** damage
- Smiting fey or fiends: **+3d8** (Oath of the Ancients bonus)

**Nature's Wrath** *(Channel Divinity — 1 use per rest)*
Spectral vines erupt from the ground. One creature within 10 ft. makes a STR or DEX save (their choice) vs. DC **13** or is **restrained** until they succeed on the save (retry each turn).

**Turn the Faithless** *(Channel Divinity — 1 use per rest)*
Fey and fiends within 30 ft. make a WIS save vs. DC **13** or are turned for 1 minute. Turned creatures flee and cannot approach you.

---

**Lay on Hands** *(healing pool — 15 HP total per long rest)*
Touch a creature to restore HP from your pool (1 point = 1 HP). Spend 5 points to cure one disease or one poison.

**Bless** *(1st-level spell — Concentration)*
Up to 3 creatures within 30 ft. add **+1d4** to every attack roll and saving throw. Up to 1 minute.

**Cure Wounds** *(1st-level spell)*
Touch a creature. They regain **1d8 + 3** HP.

**Ensnaring Strike** *(1st-level Oath spell — always prepared)*
Before an attack: on a hit, STR save vs. DC **13** or target is restrained.

**Speak with Animals** *(1st-level Oath spell — always prepared)*
Communicate with beasts. Ritual, no slot needed.
