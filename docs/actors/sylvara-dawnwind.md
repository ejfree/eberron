## Who You Are

Sylvara is a tracker and scout who hires out to merchant caravans, survey teams, and the occasional private investigation. She grew up in the Eldeen Reaches and made her way east following her work. She is quiet, observant, and has a strong academic interest in unusual creatures — catalogued, sketched, analyzed, not befriended. She noticed something was wrong with the college's morning freight delivery before anyone inside the building did.

**Why you're here:** You noticed the freight delivery this morning had the wrong signs — sounds, smells, subtle movement in the crates that didn't match what was on the manifest. Your professional instincts said: follow up. You entered the college to investigate and were inside when the lockdown started. You are, once again, annoyingly correct about your concerns.

## In a Fight

**Shortbow** *(range 80/320 ft. — Archery fighting style included)*
Roll d20 + **7**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** piercing damage.
*The +7 includes your Archery style (+2 bonus to all ranged attacks). Past 80 ft., attacks have disadvantage.*

**Shortsword** *(melee backup)*
Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** piercing damage.

**Colossus Slayer** *(once per turn)*
When you hit a creature that is **below its maximum HP**, add **+1d8** to the damage. Most creatures are below max HP by their second hit.

---

**Hunter's Mark** *(1st-level spell — Bonus Action, Concentration)*
Mark one creature within 90 ft. Every time you hit it: **+1d6** extra damage. Lasts 1 hour (concentration). If the target drops, move the mark to a new target as a bonus action.

**Ensnaring Strike** *(1st-level spell — Concentration)*
Declare before making an attack. On a hit, the target makes a STR save vs. DC **12** or is **restrained** — stuck in place, speed reduced to 0, attacks against it have advantage. The creature can retry the save each turn.

**Cure Wounds** *(1st-level spell)*
Touch a creature. They regain **1d8 + 2** HP.

## Outside a Fight

**Perception +4:** You notice things before others do. This is a habit, not a talent.
**Survival +4:** Track creatures, navigate terrain, read signs others overlook.
**Stealth +5:** Move quietly, use cover, disappear into shadow.

**Favored Enemy (Monstrosities):** Advantage on WIS (Survival) and INT checks to track or recall information about monstrous creatures. Mimics qualify.
