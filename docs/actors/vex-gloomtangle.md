## Who You Are

Vex is a graduate student in theoretical arcane cognition — the study of minds that work differently than gnomish minds do. At some point during his research, something noticed him noticing it. He made a pact. He doesn't fully understand the terms. The entity communicates in impressions and imagery rather than words, which he finds academically fascinating and personally unsettling. He is pale, intense, and sleeps poorly.

**Why you're here:** You were in the stacks when the mimics incident started. You recognized immediately that mimics are creatures of dual nature — simultaneously object and mind — and you want to understand what made these ones break free.

## In a Fight

**Eldritch Blast** *(Cantrip — unlimited, range 120 ft.)*
Roll d20 + **4** to hit. On a hit: **1d10 + 2** force damage.
This is your bread and butter. You can be far away and still be effective.

**Hex** *(1st-level spell — cast at 2nd level, Bonus Action)*
Curse one creature within 90 ft. Deals **+1d6 necrotic** every time you hit it with Eldritch Blast. Choose one of its abilities — it has disadvantage on checks using that ability. Concentration, 1 hour.

**Hunger of Hadar** *(2nd-level spell)*
Create a 20 ft. sphere of cold darkness at a point within 150 ft. Creatures inside are blinded. At the start of each turn inside: **2d6 cold** damage. At the end: make a DEX save vs. DC **12** or take **2d6 acid**. Concentration, 1 minute. You cannot see into it either.

---

**Pact Magic — Short Rest Recovery**
You have **2 spell slots**, but they come back on a **short rest**, not just a long rest. Every slot is cast at 2nd level.

**Armor of Shadows** *(Eldritch Invocation)*
Cast Mage Armor on yourself at will, for free, no slot needed. Your AC is already calculated with this active (14).

**Awakened Mind**
You can communicate telepathically with any creature within 30 ft. that has a language. One-way: they hear you in their mind. They can respond verbally. No one else hears it.
