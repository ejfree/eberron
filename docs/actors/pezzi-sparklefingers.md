## Who You Are

Pezzi is a junior researcher in the Schematics & Prototypes division, two doors down from Roondar's office. She studies the intersection of mechanical and magical engineering. She is enthusiastic, slightly reckless, and smells faintly of scorched paper. She keeps her spellbook in a fireproof case.

**Why you're here:** You were in the hallway when the mop handle started moving. You are professionally curious about this.

## In a Fight

**Fire Bolt** *(Cantrip — unlimited uses, range 120 ft.)*
Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **2d10** fire damage. This is your main attack.

**Magic Missile** *(1st-level spell — no roll needed)*
Spend a 1st-level slot. Three glowing darts automatically hit one or more targets. Each dart does **1d4 + 1** force damage. Always works — no attack roll, no saving throw.

**Thunderwave** *(1st-level spell)*
Everything within 15 ft. of you must make a CON save vs. DC **13**. On a fail: **2d8** thunder damage and pushed 10 ft. away. On a success: half damage. Loud. Very loud.

**Scorching Ray** *(2nd-level spell)*
Three rays, each rolled separately: d20 + **5** to hit. Each ray that hits does **2d6** fire damage.

---

**Sculpt Spells**
When you cast an evocation spell, you can protect up to **2 allies** inside the spell's area — they automatically succeed on their saving throw and take no damage even on a hit.

**Shield** *(1st-level spell — Reaction)*
When something hits you, cast this as a reaction (after seeing the roll). Add **+5 to your AC** until the start of your next turn. The hit might miss.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Intelligence increases from 17 to **19 (+4)**.

- Spell Save DC: 13 → **14**
- Spell Attack Bonus: +5 → **+6**
- HP increases by **6** (new total: **26**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Resilience:** CON 14 → **16 (+3)**
- HP increases by **7** instead (new total: **27**)
- CON saves improve — important for holding concentration spells when you get hit

**Option C — Feat: War Caster**
You are trained to cast spells under fire.
- **Advantage on concentration saves** when you take damage — your spells are much harder to break
- You can cast spells as opportunity attacks when enemies move past you
- You can use your arcane focus even with full hands
