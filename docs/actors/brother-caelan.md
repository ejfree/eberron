## Who You Are

Brother Caelan is a traveling cleric of the Traveler — the Sovereign Host deity of change, deception, and clever solutions. He is cheerful about this in a way that makes people slightly nervous. He genuinely believes that deception in service of a greater good is sacred work. He is also a skilled healer who happens to know a lot of useful spells that other clerics consider disreputable.

**Why you're here:** You arrived in Korranberg as part of a pilgrim group visiting various temples and places of learning. The others went to the Temple of Aureon. You slipped away to the library, which seemed more interesting. You have been here ever since, and you do not regret this.

## In a Fight

**Sacred Flame** *(Cantrip — unlimited, range 60 ft.)*
Target makes a DEX save vs. DC **13**. On fail: **1d8 radiant** damage. Ignores cover. No attack roll needed — they have to save.

**Inflict Wounds** *(1st-level spell — melee)*
Roll d20 + **5** to hit. On a hit: **3d10 necrotic** damage. Brutal up close.

**Spiritual Weapon** *(2nd-level spell — Bonus Action)*
Summon a floating spectral mace (it looks like a mask) within 60 ft. As a bonus action each turn: it makes a melee attack, +5 to hit, **1d8 + 3** force damage. Lasts 1 minute, no concentration.

---

**Channel Divinity — Invoke Duplicity** *(1 use per rest)*
Create an illusory duplicate of yourself within 30 ft. as an action. For 1 minute: you can cast spells as if standing where the duplicate is, and allies have advantage on attacks against creatures within 5 ft. of it. Move it 30 ft. per turn as a bonus action.

**Channel Divinity — Cloak of Shadows** *(1 use per rest)*
As an action, become **invisible** until the end of your next turn.

**Healing Word** *(Bonus Action, 1st-level spell)*
Ally within 60 ft. regains **1d4 + 3** HP. Bonus action means you can heal and still do something else.
