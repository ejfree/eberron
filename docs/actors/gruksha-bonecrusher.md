## Who You Are

Gruksha runs a bounty recovery operation — when someone skips bail, breaks a contract, or disappears with money that isn't theirs, people hire Gruksha to bring them back. She is businesslike about this. Her targets come back intact. She has a code: the job is the job, nothing personal. She has been following a fugitive for three days. The trail led here.

**Why you're here:** You tracked a bail jumper to the college this morning. You were inside making quiet inquiries when the lockdown started. Your target is still in the building somewhere. So is whatever is making that noise. Both problems, one location.

## In a Fight

**Greataxe (2-handed):** Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d12 + 3** slashing damage.

**Handaxe (throw up to 20 ft.):** Roll d20 + **5**. On a hit: **1d6 + 3** slashing damage.

---

**Rage** *(Bonus Action — 3 uses per long rest)*
Enter a battle fury for 1 minute:

- All melee damage rolls get **+2**
- Resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage (take half)
- Advantage on STR checks and saves
- Cannot cast spells or concentrate on spells (doesn't matter — you have none)

*Rage ends if you don't attack or take damage by end of your turn.*

**Bear Totem** *(active while Raging)*
The Bear Totem Spirit makes you exceptionally hard to kill. While raging, you have resistance to **all damage types** except psychic — fire, cold, lightning, acid, necrotic — everything hits for half.

**Reckless Attack**
Declare before your first attack. Your attack has **advantage** (roll twice, take higher). But attacks against you also have advantage until your next turn. Use when you need to land the hit.

---

**Relentless Endurance** *(Half-Orc — once per long rest)*
When you drop to 0 HP (not killed outright), you drop to **1 HP** instead. You keep fighting.

**Savage Attacks** *(Half-Orc)*
When you score a critical hit with a melee weapon, roll **one extra damage die** and add it to the damage.

**Danger Sense**
Advantage on DEX saves against effects you can see — traps, spells with visible effects, and similar dangers.

## Outside a Fight

**Athletics +5:** You physically impose yourself on situations. Climbing, grappling, lifting, shoving.
**Intimidation +4:** You don't raise your voice. Most people reconsider before you need to.
**Survival +3:** Tracking, navigation, reading terrain.
**Darkvision:** 60 ft. — useful in unlit corridors and storage wings.

## Level 4 Upgrade — Use at Mid-Session

Your Strength increases from 17 to **19 (+4)**.

- Greataxe attack: +5 → **+6**
- Damage bonus: +3 → **+4**
- HP increases by **9** (new total: **41**)

**Other Level 4 Options**

A *feat* is a special ability you take instead of a stat increase. Pick any one option — recommended is above, but any of these is valid.

**Option B — Endurance:** CON 15 → **17 (+3)**
- HP increases by **10** instead (new total: **42**)
- More durable — you hit less hard but can take a lot more damage

**Option C — Feat: Tough**
You are extraordinarily hard to kill.
- Gain **+2 HP per level**, applied retroactively — **+8 HP** on top of your normal level-up gain (new total: **49**)
- Barbarians with Tough and Bear Totem are some of the most durable characters in the game
