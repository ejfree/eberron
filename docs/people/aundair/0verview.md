# Aundair — Peoples and Culture

The Aundairians are an arcane-minded, chivalrous people who prize wit, learning and tradition.

## Peoples

Aundair is mostly human, with a significant population of elves and Khoravar who are folded into its noble families, and a few noble families of gnomes.

## Society and Government

Aundairians hold closely to the old traditions of Galifar and have a romantic view of their nobility. Noblesse oblige and chivalry are common values, and most believe their rulers will do what is best for the country. The common folk value self-reliance and ambition. Nobles are expected to have wit, knowledge and some arcane talent; a noble who cannot cast even a cantrip will be teased. Aundairians tend to believe that they have a dignity and decency beyond their neighbors, an idealism that does not extend to foreign nobles. Dueling has a long history, and the west and east of the country grew apart over the last two centuries, which ended in the secession of the Eldeen Reaches.

## Religion and Beliefs

The Sovereign Host is the main faith. The west embraced the Pure Flame sect before it seceded. Thelanis, the fey realm, has a strong influence: many noble estates sit near manifest zones tied to it, and many old families claim ancient agreements with fey.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

Aundairians favor grace, mobility and skill over heavy armor and brute strength. They often use glamerweave or transmutation to add flair to clothing and armor, so a knight might have twinkling stars or storm clouds worked into their armor. The lightly armored, fashionably dressed wandslinger with a wand and rapier is celebrated over the heavy knight.

## Language and Names

Common is spoken throughout. Aundairian nobles use the standard noble ranks of Galifar; arcane spellcasters add a title for their school of magic and the highest circle of spell they can cast, such as "Diviner of the Fourth Circle".

## How Others See Them

Aundairians tend to believe their people, nobles and commoners alike, have a dignity and decency beyond their neighbors.
