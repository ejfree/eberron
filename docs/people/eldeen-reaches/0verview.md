# Eldeen Reaches — Peoples and Culture

The people of the Eldeen Reaches live close to nature under the care of druidic sects.

## Peoples

Humans and other ancestries live in the forests and villages of the Reaches, alongside the fey and other creatures of the wild.

## Society and Government

The Reaches are governed by druidic sects, chiefly the Wardens of the Wood and the militant Ashbound. The people of the Reaches use natural materials, including the hide and bones of supernatural creatures, which make their gear stronger than one would expect; an Eldeen equivalent of plate armor could be made from the hide and bones of a demon-spawned bear.

## Religion and Beliefs

Eldeen faith is druidic and centered on nature, including the Three Faces of the Wild. Many in the Reaches have faith in the Sovereign Host as well.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

The people of the Eldeen Reaches use natural materials taken from supernatural creatures. The Ashbound oppose arcane magic and industry, while the Wardens of the Wood take a more moderate path.

## Language and Names

Common is spoken, with Druidic known to the sects.

## How Others See Them

Aundairian nobles who lost their lands in the secession are known as the Lost Lords.
