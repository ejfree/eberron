# Breland — Peoples and Culture

The Brelish are a pragmatic, egalitarian and diverse people, proud of their parliament and their cities.

## Peoples

Breland is mostly human, but its cities, especially Sharn, hold every ancestry of Khorvaire. Gnomes, dwarves, halflings, elves, shifters, changelings, warforged and kalashtar all live here, and the city of Sharn is the most cosmopolitan place on the continent.

## Society and Government

Brelish commoners accepted that the nobles had the power but never bought into the romantic ideals of divine bloodlines. It is always said that a Brelish farmer sees themselves as the equal of any king. Communities choose their own reeves and lesser officials, and simple matters of justice are often resolved by the people instead of the courts. Nobles have to earn the respect of their subjects. The Brelish are known for pride and loyalty when they respect their leaders, but they have seen more minor uprisings than the other Five Nations. A strong movement thinks the Brelish monarchy should end with King Boranel's reign.

## Religion and Beliefs

The Sovereign Host and the Church of the Silver Flame are the main faiths among the Brelish.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

The Brelish are pragmatic and have no great desire to stand out. Thanks to their industrial capacity, they fielded more soldiers in medium armor than their rivals, and mass production gave Brelish soldiers nearly identical equipment, with personal touches that serve comfort and function more than flair. See [Breland](../../locations/breland/breland.md) for the Brelish phrases and the five things every Brelish knows.

## Language and Names

Common is spoken throughout. Brelish speech and slang are described in the existing Breland document.

## How Others See Them

Breland is the only one of the Five Nations that lets nobles sell their titles.
