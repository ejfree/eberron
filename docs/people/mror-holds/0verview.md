# Mror Holds — Peoples and Culture

The Mror are dwarves of the Ironroot Mountains, shaped by clan, family and the long War Below.

## Peoples

The Mror Holds are the home of dwarves who were born on the surface of the Ironroot Mountains, with a feudal system of twelve active holds. There are also dwarves of the Realm Below.

## Society and Government

The Holds are a feudal society made of holds, spires and families. Each hold is ruled by a clan that gives its name to the hold, and each hold is broken into spires, each ruled by a clan. Land is held by clans or families, and most businesses are family businesses. Clan plays a major role in Mror culture. The people are shaped by Dol Udar, the War Below, a simmering conflict against aberrations; civilians drill for assault and all are expected to contribute to the war effort.

## Religion and Beliefs

Faith is varied; many Mror follow the Sovereign Host and clan traditions. The War Below has shaped belief and resolve.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

The Mror appreciate sunlight and color, and their buildings typically have windows, but dwarves do not need light. Day and night have little meaning, so Mror communities are active at all hours and major businesses are continuously open. "Nightlife" is not a concept. The Mror Holds have a strong tradition of chainmail; Doldarun double-chain uses the statistics of plate armor.

## Language and Names

Dwarvish and Common are spoken.
