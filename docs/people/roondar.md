# Roondar Timbers

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Title** | Senior Librarian, Head of Schematics & Prototypes |
| **Affiliation** | College of Drystone |
| **Base** | Korranberg, Zilargo |

> "I need this resolved. Not by me. By you."

## Overview

Roondar Timbers is the director of the Schematics & Prototypes division on the 4th floor of the College of Drystone, and he would like someone else to handle whatever problem has just arrived at his door. He wears a large pastel jacket and sports empty pouches on his belt — the pouches suggest a person who once carried things and has since decided that carrying things is for other people. He has a patchy chestnut beard and wild chestnut hair. His brown eyes are alert and acquisitive. He is not easily forgotten, which is something he both cultivates and resents.

Roondar is envious of other people's things in a way that has become completely normalized to him — he does not experience it as a flaw, simply as an accurate assessment of the situation. He hoards: treasure, art, food, wine, anything that catches his attention. He is also lazy in the specific way of intelligent people who have learned that most problems can be made to go away if you delegate them correctly and position yourself at a plausible distance from the outcome.

## Abilities

Despite the affect of disengagement, Roondar has built and maintained one of the most comprehensive schematic archives in Zilargo. The filing system on the 4th floor is organized in a way that only he fully understands — which is not an accident. He is a competent administrator when the matter interests him, a gifted acquisitor always, and a capable political survivor for more years than most people expect.

## Activities

He directs the day-to-day operation of Schematics & Prototypes, which primarily means managing researchers, arbitrating disputes over shelf space, and deflecting requests from other floors. His office — buried in the middle of the 4th floor with no windows — is dense with accumulated things: rolled schematics he never reads, framed mechanical drawings, three clocks showing different times, a bowl of fruit he picks from but does not replenish.

## Secrets

> *DM Eyes Only*

### Not Trust, But Useful

Roondar has no formal Trust affiliation. He is, however, exactly the kind of person the Trust finds useful: acquisitive, discreet when it benefits him, and capable of making administrative decisions that leave no obvious trail. Whether he knows the full scope of what moves through his floor — or through the college more broadly — is an open question he has never been asked directly.

### The Bottle

There is a bottle of AngryCat 30/30 Reserve Vintage in his locked cabinet. He bought it before the collapse. He has not opened it. He will not explain why he still has it, and he becomes visibly irritated if anyone notices it.
