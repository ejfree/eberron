Roondar Timbers

Senior Librarian
Head of Schematics & Prototypes
College of Drystone

Description: This man tends to wear a large pastel jacket, and sports empty pouches on his belt. With a patchy chestnut beard and wild chestnut hair. This brown eyed westerner is not easily forgotten.

Personality: He is envious of other's things. He hoards treasure, art, food, wine, and anything that he so desires. He is lazy and tries to find solutions quickly.

History: Born in the west his family was never financially stable. He was an apprentice working in his father's forge. He hated it and apprenticed as a librarian.
