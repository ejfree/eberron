# Thrane — Peoples and Culture

The Thrane are a devout, disciplined people whose daily lives are tied to the Church of the Silver Flame.

## Peoples

Thrane is predominantly human, with other ancestries from across the Five Nations.

## Society and Government

Thrane is a theocratic kingdom governed in the name of the Church of the Silver Flame. The Church shapes law, daily life and the military, and its templars are a serious force. Thrane's citizens are known for devotion, discipline and a strong sense of duty.

## Religion and Beliefs

The Silver Flame is the dominant faith, born in Flamekeep. Followers usually display a symbol of their faith as pendants, brooches or painted designs. Other faiths, such as the Sovereign Host, are practiced by some.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

Thrane is the most practical and least pretentious of the Five Nations in armor and fashion. Templars may wear heavy armor, but peasant militias rely on light armor and bows. Light armor is common in everyday life but unostentatious; cosmetic transmutation and glamerweave are rare, and the militias have no standard uniform.

## Language and Names

Common is spoken throughout.
