# Demon Wastes — Peoples and Culture

The Demon Wastes are held by barbarian tribes and fiends, with little culture of settled people.

## Peoples

The Wastes are inhabited by barbarian tribes, fiends and other creatures.

## Society and Government

The wastes are a desolate, blighted land. The tribes live by raiding and survival, and fiends and the Lords of Dust are said to find sanctuary here.

## Religion and Beliefs

The demon city of Ashtakala lies hidden in the wastes.
