# Shadow Marshes — Peoples and Culture

The people of the Shadow Marshes are hardy hunters and trackers, the home of House Tharashk.

## Peoples

Orcs, half-orcs and humans live in the marshes, along with other peoples.

## Society and Government

House Tharashk has its home here and uses its dragonmark of Finding to run businesses of finders and bounty hunters, with the Finders Guild. The house is led by a Triumvirate. See [House Tharashk](../../groups/house-tharashk/background.md).
