# Lhazaar Principalities — Peoples and Culture

The people of the Lhazaar Principalities are sailors, raiders and explorers.

## Peoples

Humans make up most of the population, with dwarves and others in mixed towns along the coast.

## Society and Government

Each principality is ruled by a prince of a seafaring family, and each has its own distinctive style, customs and rivalries. They tend to wear light armor that will not impede a swimmer.

## Religion and Beliefs

Faiths vary by principality.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

As befits a people who spend much of their time on the sea, the people of the Lhazaar Principalities favor light armor and practical clothing. Each principality has its own style.

## Language and Names

Common is spoken throughout.
