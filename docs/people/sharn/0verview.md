# Sharn — Peoples and Culture

Sharn's people are as varied as its towers, divided by altitude as much as by wealth.

## Peoples

Sharn is the most cosmopolitan city of Khorvaire, with humans, dwarves, elves, halflings, gnomes, changelings, shifters, warforged, kalashtar and more. Passengers arriving by rail are greeted by "scheming warforged and nimble halflings to mystic kalashtar and curious tourists".

## Society and Government

The city is layered: the upper towers belong to the affluent and influential, mid-level towers hold merchants and crafters, and the Lower Wards are home to grit, grime and mystery. Twenty-five noble families are represented in Sharn; together with thirty-five other powerful families they form the Sixty, the city's social nobility. The Lord Mayor is a viscount appointed by the elected city council.

## Religion and Beliefs

Faiths of every kind are found here.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

Sharn is a place of street performers, hawkers, open-air markets and the smell of spiced food and forges. Skycoaches glide between towers and bridges link districts.

## Language and Names

Common is spoken, along with every language of Khorvaire.

## How Others See Them

Sharn sits in the Duchy of the Hilt.
