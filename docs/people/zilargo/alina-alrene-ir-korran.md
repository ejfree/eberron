### Alina Alrene ir'Korran

Alina Alrene ir'Korran is the appointed Korran-family chair of the council of college heads that governs the Library of Korranberg.

### Appearance

A tall-for-a-gnome woman with silver-streaked braids and a ceremonial chain of office she wears slightly crooked.

### Personality

Diplomatic, patient, and quietly exhausted by eight rival heads. Hates losing a vote, loves a compromise she can claim credit for.
