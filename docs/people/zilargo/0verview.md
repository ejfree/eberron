# Zilargo — Peoples and Culture

The Zil are a sociable, curious and scheming people, held together by family, clan and the Trust.

## Peoples

Zilargo is predominantly gnomish, with humans and dwarves forming significant minorities and kobolds in the mountains ringing it, who consider themselves outside the Zil state and are isolationist. Sources differ on the proportion of gnomes: the existing [Zilargo](../../locations/zilargo/zilargo.md) document gives about 90%, while Politics of Zilargo says about three-fifths.

## Society and Government

Gnomes value the bonds of kinship highly. A gnome's "last name" is their clan and the "middle name" their family; the clan succeeds or fails together, and family units demand intense loyalty. The Triumvirate rules, with a representative from each of Zilargo's three major cities, each governed by its own Council of Nine. Both are formally democratic but in practice are decided by the plots and schemes of the wealthy, and the Councils of Nine are populated only by gnomes. Rivalries are intense, but all agree that the needs of the nation come first. The Trust enforces two rules: the national interest comes first, and no crime. Zil society is a web of debts, deals and favors, where the letter of an obligation always comes before its spirit.

## Religion and Beliefs

Illusion is part of everyday life, and many gnomes can speak with small burrowing creatures. The gnomes' connection to Thelanis may shape their attitudes to debts and obligations.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

Zilargo is built to accommodate medium-sized visitors, even in small villages. The people prefer not to wear armor in everyday life, since the Trust means they have no fear of violence, though light armor can be part of casual fashion.

## Language and Names

Common is spoken, and Gnomish is the language of the people.

## How Others See Them

Visitors are advised to watch the shadows for rodents that might be spying on them. Others see the Zil as clever, indirect and hard to read.
