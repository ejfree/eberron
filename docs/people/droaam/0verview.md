# Droaam — Peoples and Culture

The people of Droaam are a diverse nation of creatures many call monsters, united under the Daughters of Sora Kell.

## Peoples

Droaam's population is staggeringly diverse: gnolls (including the Znir Pact), goblins, ogres, trolls, hill giants, ettins, minotaurs, harpies, medusas, gargoyles, changelings from Lost, and many more. Some enclaves such as Cazhaak Draal and the Venomous Demesne are mostly of one species, but the great cities hold all kinds.

## Society and Government

All citizens serve the Daughters. Each region is governed by a warlord in their name, and chibs rule local communities. Citizens are expected to serve when called and to do whatever is asked. In return they receive sustenance, shelter and a sense of pride in defying the eastern nations. Droaam is only about a decade old, still a frontier, and many citizens are sincerely committed to it. Those who appear idle or cause trouble are swept up by press gangs.

## Religion and Beliefs

Droaam is one of the places where the Dark Six are revered, and the Cazhaak faith is part of its culture. Many of its citizens are in awe of the Daughters, with a balance of fear and wonder.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

Many Droaamites live in hall housing in cities that are still being built; work ranges from fixed jobs, such as miners, masons and soldiers, to a general labor pool. Gargoyles serve as couriers and scouts, and changelings from Lost serve the Daughters as entertainers, mediators and spies. Many Droaamish mercenaries find work with House Tharashk.

## Language and Names

Several exotic languages are used, such as the chattering of the Znir Pact gnolls and the hissing and weaving of medusas. Goblin is the common language of trade, from the Dhakaani era, and almost every Droaamite understands it. The Daughters are encouraging the spread of Common.

## How Others See Them

Droaam has a bad reputation in the eastern nations, which many of its people take pride in defying.
