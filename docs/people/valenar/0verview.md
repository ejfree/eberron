# Valenar — Peoples and Culture

The Valenar are the Tairnadal elves, devoted to their ancestors and to the honor of war.

## Peoples

The Tairnadal elves are the people of Valenar, with humans and others who lived in the land when they arrived.

## Society and Government

The Tairnadal live by ancestor veneration, believing in warriors of legend who live on in descendants. Their society is martial, with clans and war-bands. Valenar was formed from eastern Cyre almost four decades before 998 YK.

## Religion and Beliefs

The Tairnadal faith centers on the Ancestors of Legend; warriors bind the spirits of ancestors to their weapons, and the Paladin Oath of Veneration comes from this faith.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

Tairnadal society is martial, built on clans, war-bands and the honor of the ancestors.

## Language and Names

Elvish and Common are spoken.
