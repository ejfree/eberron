# Umpeen Bafflestone

Greeter
Administration
College of Drystone

Description: He wears very tight pants and sleeveless undershirts, though occasionally he sports a dark overcoat over the top of this ensemble with no apparent awareness of how this looks. He has a moderate pudge and is not particularly fit. His head is shaved completely bald and polished. He wears large industrial goggles on his forehead at all times — pushed up, never down, which raises the question of what they are for. His hands are rough and calloused in the specific way of someone who spent years doing physical labor before transitioning to desk work.

Personality: Eccentric and domineering, Umpeen drives every conversation he is part of whether or not he was invited into it. He is skeptical of what others tell him and slow to extend trust, preferring to verify independently before accepting any claim at face value. He compensates for a deep uncertainty about who he actually is by performing a version of himself so consistently that the performance has become indistinguishable from the person. He is not unkind. He is thorough. These are not always the same thing.

History: Umpeen was born in a poor docking district to a working family. His father was a mechanic — the kind who could fix anything with what was available — and Umpeen spent his early years doing general labor alongside him, learning the same approach: assess what you have, figure out what it can do, make it work. He was good at it. He was also quietly miserable at it. He left when an opportunity presented itself, though he has never been entirely specific about what that opportunity was or who offered it. He has worked at the College of Drystone for eleven years. Before that, his employment history is not in any file the college has access to.

Above Table: Umpeen is a Level 3 operative in the Trust. He is the supervisor of all Trust assets stationed on the lower floors of the college — including Glitter in Shipping & Receiving. His role as Greeter is ideal for intelligence work: everyone who enters the basement passes through him, and he remembers every face, every name, every stated purpose. He reports to a Level 4 Sub-Director based on the 10th floor (Innovation & Transformation) whose name does not appear in any college directory.

The performance Umpeen puts on — the eccentricity, the domineering manner, the goggles — is not entirely a performance. But it is also not entirely not one. He has been doing this long enough that he has stopped keeping track of where one ends and the other begins.
