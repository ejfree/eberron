# Mournland — Peoples and Culture

The Mournland has no living culture of its own; its people are the refugees of Cyre and the ghosts of the Mourning.

## Peoples

The Mournland is uninhabited, aside from the things that moved in after the Mourning. Cyrans survive elsewhere in Khorvaire, in New Cyre and as refugees.

## Society and Government

Cyre was a land of dreamers devoted to arts, sciences and philosophy, whose people believed "what our dreams imagine, our hands create." The Treaty of Thronehold established Cyre as a fallen nation, and displaced Cyran nobles have little but their pride.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

Cyran armor and clothing combined Brelish practicality with some of the flair of Aundair. Cyran heavy armor mirrored Karrnathi light plate without the gothic styling, and cloaks and capes were an important part of Cyran fashion. Cyran soldiers were sometimes called "Greencloaks". Cyre had long been known for "the Cyran Appreciation".

## Language and Names

Common is spoken by Cyran refugees.
