# Darguun — Peoples and Culture

The people of Darguun are goblinoids, bound in clans, who value strength and revenge.

## Peoples

Darguun is a land of goblins, hobgoblins and bugbears, with humans and others living among them. Goblinoids are the ruling people.

## Society and Government

Darguun is organized around clans and warlords under the rule of a Lhesh. Honor, strength and a code of revenge and justice matter deeply: Chronicles notes that in Darguun revenge and justice are seen as one and the same. The nation was built on the ruins of the Dhakaani Empire, and its people draw on that heritage.

## Religion and Beliefs

The Dark Six have a following, and the Mockery is revered in Darguun; the Shadow is seen as a patron of those who live by deception and survival. Many follow the clan's ancestral traditions.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

It is common for the people of Darguun to wear armor in everyday life. Each clan has its own style, and medium armor, especially scale mail, is common. Like Karrnath, Darguun arms and armor are often designed to intimidate.

## Language and Names

Goblin is the common language, with Common used for trade.
