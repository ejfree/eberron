# Karrnath — Peoples and Culture

The Karrns are a hardy, martial people shaped by a long war, harsh winters and mandatory service.

## Peoples

Humans make up a little more than half of the population and live throughout the nation. Dwarves are the next largest group at nearly a fifth, found mostly in the east. Halflings live mainly in the south, elves in the urban west, and there are also Khoravar, warforged, goblinoids, goliaths, orcs, planetouched, changelings and kalashtar.

## Society and Government

Karrnath is the least industrialized of the nations that survived the war. It is governed by the Code of Kaius, a harsher and more authoritarian version of the Code of Galifar, with martial law placing near-absolute power in the hands of local commanders. Accused citizens have no jury or independent judiciary. Most curfews have lifted since the war, but not every count has fully let go of wartime law. All citizens must serve at least two years in the military, a tradition started by Karrn the Conqueror, and dodging service is a deep shame. Many Karrns make service a career or join the Blademarks Guild of House Deneith. The royal corpse collectors of the Ministry of the Dead recover the remains of elite soldiers for the Odakyr Rites.

## Religion and Beliefs

Of twenty random Karrns, about eleven would profess to be Vassals of the Sovereign Host (with an emphasis on its martial deities), six Seekers of the Divinity Within (the Blood of Vol), two no faith, and one something else such as the Church of the Silver Flame or Talentan spiritualism. The Blood of Vol was the state religion for most of the war; the Regent stripped that status in 976, and the king has since banned its open worship in Korth. It remains strong in Atur.

## Customs, Fashion and Daily Life

Rural Karrns wear thick wool; city dwellers copy military dress uniforms with sharp lines and dark fabrics. Black and red are the national colors, and everyone appreciates a solid cloak. The Karrns have always been the finest armorers of the Five Nations, and armor is more common here than elsewhere, with a gothic style and designs meant to intimidate. The Code of Kaius limits civilian access to weapons, so a civilian's sword is usually peace-bound. A chess-like war game called conqueror is beloved across the nation, and Atur is the home of the True Shapers, House Thuranni's artists. Nightwood Ale is a famous export.

## Language and Names

Common is spoken throughout.

## How Others See Them

Foreign press sensationalizes Karrnath's undead soldiers and the Blood of Vol, though the nation is more varied than that. Since the war ended, the king has made a show of supporting the Sovereign Host.
