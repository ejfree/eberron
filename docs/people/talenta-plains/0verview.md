# Talenta Plains — Peoples and Culture

The Talenta are nomadic halflings who ride dinosaurs and keep their own traditions.

## Peoples

Halflings of the Talenta tribes make up most of the population.

## Society and Government

Nomadic halfling tribes live on the plains and gather at seasonal sites. They ride dinosaurs and hold to tribal traditions and a holistic relationship with the land. Talentan druids take a holistic approach to the land.

## Religion and Beliefs

Talentan spiritualism is the main faith.
