# Glitter

Real name: Binderly Bimpnottin

Shipping & Receiving Supervisor
Logistics & Administration
College of Drystone

Description: She has a missing left arm, lost below the shoulder, which she makes no effort to conceal or explain. She prefers fine silks in deep colors — burgundy, forest green, black — though she keeps several sets of combat armor maintained and accessible for what she calls her hunting needs. Her silver hair is enormous: matted, messy, and big in a way that suggests it has its own agenda. She has several tiny ruby earrings on each ear, enough that they catch the light when she turns her head. She carries herself like someone who has assessed every room she has ever walked into and found most of them manageable.

Personality: Glitter cares significantly about what others think of her — not in a fragile way, but in the way of someone for whom reputation is a professional tool as much as a personal concern. When someone's opinion aligns with hers, she is generous and warm. When opinions clash, she becomes argumentative with a speed and intensity that surprises people who only know her from the silks. She is also, with some embarrassment, terrible with names. She will try to learn them, fail two or three times, and then quietly assign descriptors to people until the name finally sticks. She is aware this is a problem and finds it mortifying. She powers through anyway.

History: Bimpnottin was raised in a hard-working, conservative household where career was identity and the family trade was bounty hunting. Her mother hunted. Her older sister hunted. There was never a serious conversation about alternatives. She was good at the work — methodical, patient, harder to rattle than she looked — and built a reputation over several years operating across Zilargo and into the surrounding regions. The left arm was lost on a contract she does not discuss. Shortly after, she transitioned out of active hunting into logistics, which she describes as a lateral move. She has run Shipping & Receiving at the College of Drystone for four years. The warehouse is more organized than it appears, and appears significantly more organized than it did when she arrived.

The name Glitter was given to her by a target who thought he was being cruel. She kept it.

Above Table: Glitter is a Level 2 operative in the Trust, working under Umpeen Bafflestone in the college's intelligence structure. Shipping & Receiving is an ideal Trust posting: every physical object that enters or leaves the college passes through her department. She handles special acquisitions, materials of interest, and the occasional object that needs to arrive without appearing in any official inventory. The bounty hunting background is not incidental to the Trust's interest in her. She is still, underneath the silks and the logistics role, very good at finding people.
