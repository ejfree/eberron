Glitter
Real name: Binderly Bimpnottin

Shipping & Receiving Supervisor
Logistics & Administration
College of Drystone

Description: She has a missing left arm. She prefers to wear fine silks, though has several suits of combat armor for her hunting needs. Her silver hair is matted, messy, and big. She has several tiny ruby earrings on each ear.

Personality: She cares much about the opinion of others, both in good and in bad: when keeping in line with her opinion, she thinks highly of the other person, but when opinions clash, she can get violently argumentative. She is terrible with names forgetting people's names several times before they finally stick; this causes her no end of embarrassment.

History: Bimpnottin was raised in a hard working conservative environment. Like her mother and her sister before her, after school she went straight into learning to become a Bounty Hunter. She has worked as a Bounty Hunter like her mother before her.

Above Table: She is a L2 Spy in the Trust organization in addition to being in charge of Shipping & Receiving. She works directly for Umpeen on special operations for the college.
