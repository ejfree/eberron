## Overview

Wobbles Cobblepot is a middle-aged gnome who has recently become a smaller version of himself. His jacket is good quality — it was bought when things were better — but it no longer fits quite right across the shoulders. His fingers are ink-stained. His eyes have learned not to linger on anything too long. He keeps a small framed picture of a vineyard on his desk, turned slightly away from him, as if he is not ready to look at it directly but also cannot bring himself to put it away.

He was, before the collapse, a cheerful and optimistic administrator with a genuine passion for Zil wine culture. That version of him is still in there, buried under several layers of exhaustion and low-grade dread. He is not a bad person. He made one bad decision after a series of bad decisions made by market forces beyond his control. He is deeply, quietly afraid — not of violence, but of the specific Zil variety of consequences: being noticed, being known, being recorded.

## Abilities

Wobbles is a competent purchasing administrator with twenty years of unremarkable, reliable service. He understands procurement routing, intake documentation, and the informal shortcuts that experienced clerks use to move paperwork through a slow system. These same skills made him useful to someone who needed a crate to disappear into an inventory without inspection.

## Activities

He processes purchase orders, manages vendor correspondence, and maintains intake records for the Purchasing Department. His desk, his paperwork, and his attendance record are all perfectly normal. He has been waiting for something bad to happen ever since he signed for the crate. He does not know what is in the crate. He told himself this did not matter.

## Secrets

> *DM Eyes Only*

### The Collapse

Wobbles lost everything in the AngryCat 30/30 winery bubble. The winery launched its fortified reserve vintage as an investment vehicle. Wobbles put in everything he had, including savings he had no business touching. The winery collapsed within fourteen months. AngryCat 30/30 turned out to be mostly the concept of wine rather than wine. He has told no one at the college.

### The Crate

He was approached through an intermediary — a note slipped under his office door with an amount written on it and instructions to expect a delivery from a supplier named Yardley. He never met the person who hired him. He filed the crate under "Janitorial Supplies, Miscellaneous" because that is what the label said, and because the money was already in his account by the time it arrived and he had spent most of it on back rent.

He will confess everything under any reasonable social pressure. He has been waiting for someone to ask. The relief of telling someone is almost worth the consequences.

### The Note

The original contact note is in the lining of his jacket. He has not decided whether to mention it. The note is unsigned but carries an IT department acquisition code in the header — standard internal procurement formatting. Anyone familiar with the college's administrative structure can identify it as a 10th floor Innovation & Transformation requisition number. Wobbles does not know what IT is, exactly. He knows that when he saw that code he felt both relieved and more frightened.
