### Quandry Lyrriman d'Sivis

Quandry, "Q" to most, is an elderly, eccentric gnome tinkerer who runs his whole life on a strict schedule and relies on his assistant "someone" for everything else.

### Appearance

An elderly gnome in the style of a fantasy steampunk tinkerer. He wears an elaborate jeweled toolbelt made of green leather, with holsters of still more tools.

### Personality

Weird, eccentric, odd, a little loveable, and quite mad.

His entire life is scheduled in 94-minute blocks he calls **Primary Indices of Time**, or **PINTs**. Groups of 7 and 14 PINTs are called **Hogs** and **Tuns**. You must be on time, or he will move on to another evolution and you may lose your audience.

**Q's Cycles** (he rotates through these in order):

1. Sleep
2. Maintenance
3. Exercise Red
4. Supply Yellow
5. Relax
6. Research
7. Eat

When a meeting is over he points to the door and says that "someone" will help you. That someone is always [Harriet](harriet-lycaris-d-sivis.md).

#### The Dragon

Quandry is a super ancient emerald dragon, older than the Doyenne ([Lysse Lyrriman d'Sivis](lysse-lyrriman-d-sivis.md)). He loves to research and build. That is all he cares about, and he listens to whatever "someone" says.

#### The Q Levels

He lives on the Q levels (0a and 0b) of the Trust Headquarters, which also hold the secret rail spur and the five gnome-built war trains. He is always in gnome form.
