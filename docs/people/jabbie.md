Jabbie Boondiggles

Executive Assistant to the Shipping & Receiving Supervisor

Jabbie is essentially the executive assistant for Glitter. However, over time he becomes the cook for glitter and to some extent to group. He is a very complex & odd character. Appears at first glance to be a simpleton.

Jabbie sits at a desk in a cubicle outside an elevator. In an area next adjacent to his desk is a small kitchenette, with a hot plate like aparatus and a tiny sink. The elevator goes to the top of the management complex overseeing this huge warehouse like underground cavern.
