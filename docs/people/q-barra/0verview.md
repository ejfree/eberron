# Q'barra — Peoples and Culture

The people of Q'barra are colonists and settlers living on a dangerous jungle frontier.

## Peoples

Q'barra is settled by humans, Galifarian loyalists and others, and shares its jungle with lizardfolk and other native peoples.

## Society and Government

Q'barra was founded by Galifarian loyalists led by the Cyran Duke Ven ir'Kesslan, who sailed during the Last War. They built fortified trading towns such as Adderport, which supports Newthrone and other new settlements. Adderport is a busy town with little vacancy, split into an inner and outer city; the settlers farm, herd livestock and harvest shards.

## Religion and Beliefs

The Sovereign Host is the main faith of Adderport, with a large temple at the crossroads and a statue of Boldrei brought on the original voyage.
