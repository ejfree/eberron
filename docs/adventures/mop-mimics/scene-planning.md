# Mop Mimics — Scene Planning

**System:** Savage Worlds Adventure Edition (SWADE)
**Setting:** College of Drystone, Korranberg, Zilargo
**Tone:** Gnome bureaucracy comedy with genuine stakes. Think office farce that turns into a real investigation.
**Runtime target:** 3–4 hours

---

## Above the Table — Full Story

The party has stumbled into a Trust operation without knowing it. Every institution they touch in this adventure — Supply, Administration, Facilities — is staffed by Trust operatives at varying levels. Glitter and Umpeen are both Trust. Margery quietly does enchantment work for the Trust. Even Jabbie, seemingly just an odd little cook, is something far stranger.

**The actual chain of events:**
Wobbles Cobblepot, a purchasing clerk in the basement, was financially ruined by the AngryCat 30/30 winery collapse. Desperate, he was approached by a **third party** (see: The Loose Thread, below) and paid to smuggle a crate of mimics into the college through Purchasing. He contracted Margery Yardley — a legitimate mimic enchanter who does Trust work on the side — to provide the mimics in "mop" form. Margery delivered them. Wobbles filed them under "Janitorial Supplies, Miscellaneous." The mimics, being mimics, eventually woke up in the closet.

Margery is not the villain. The mimics slipping their enchantment was not her fault — she delivered a stable product. Someone put them somewhere warm and adjacent to cleaning product fumes for six weeks, which broke the binding.

**The Loose Thread — Resolved:** The 10th floor ordered them.

Innovation & Transformation (IT) placed a legitimate procurement order through Margery Yardley for a larger shipment of enchanted mimics — research applications, nature unspecified. The mop-form subset of that order was routed through Wobbles in Purchasing as a routine janitorial intake. Someone in the receiving chain put the crate in the wrong location. The mimics sat in a warm closet next to solvent fumes for six weeks until the binding degraded.

This was not espionage. It was a filing error.

When the party (or Glitter) traces the purchase order back to IT, every door that was open closes. Glitter stops asking questions. Umpeen, if informed, nods once and changes the subject. No one at the college will pursue this further. IT's procurement activity is not subject to review.

The party is left with the knowledge that something on the 10th floor wanted mimics badly enough to order them through unofficial channels — and that no one in this building will tell them why.

## Load-In — Roondar's Office (4th Floor, Schematics & Prototypes)

**Visual:** Show image of Roondar and his office. This is the first thing the players see. Use it to establish tone before anyone speaks.

**The Room:** Roondar's office is buried in the middle of the 4th floor, which means it has no windows. He has compensated by filling every surface with things he has acquired: rolled schematics he never reads, a locked cabinet of wines, framed mechanical drawings, three clocks showing different times, a bowl of fruit he picks from but does not replenish. There are empty pouches on every hook.

**Roondar's opening:**
> "Good. Sit. There is a situation in the hallway outside the bathrooms on this floor. A researcher filed a complaint — she said she was 'whipped by a thousand lashed demon.' She was not. She was attacked by a mop. I need you to go find out what is in that closet, remove it, and not tell anyone. Especially not Facilities."

If the party asks why not Facilities:
> "Because Facilities will file a report, the report will go to Administration, Administration will ask questions, and questions will reach my office. I want the questions to not reach my office. Is that a difficult concept?"

If the party asks who they are / why them:
> "You were available. Go."

**SWADE note:** No roll needed here. This is pure scene-setting. Give each player a moment to introduce their character to the others if they haven't.

## Scene 1 — The Janitorial Closet

**Location:** Hallway outside the bathrooms, 4th floor. The closet door is slightly ajar. Something wet and grey is visible through the gap.

**Boxed text:**
> The hallway smells of pine cleaner and something older underneath it — damp stone, old wood. The closet door is a standard institutional affair: dark stained wood, brass handle, a small placard reading *FACILITIES — AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL*. The placard has been partially obscured by a second placard reading *AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY*. Through the gap in the door you can see a mop handle. It is slowly rotating.

### The Fight — Mop Mimics (Extras)

Four mops have awakened. They are using the closet's tight quarters to their advantage. Standard mimic stat block reskinned as cleaning implements:

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Extra (x4) |
| Agility | d6 |
| Strength | d8 |
| Fighting | d6 |
| Toughness | 6 |
| Special | Adhesive — on a raise, target is Entangled until a Strength (d8) test as an action |

**Tactics:** Two mops immediately try to grapple (Entangle) the nearest character. Two attack freely. The tight closet counts as Difficult Ground for movement. Anyone knocked Shaken in the closet risks falling into the shelving — GM's call on a free Agility roll to avoid knocking cleaning products everywhere.

**The destruction:** The closet does not survive this encounter intact. Shelves collapse. Bottles break. Someone is going to end this fight smelling like ammonia. This matters for the next scene.

**After the fight:** The mimics revert to mop shape when incapacitated. They look like perfectly normal mops again. The party now has to explain what happened to a closet full of broken shelving, spilled product, and four suspiciously limp mops.

## Return to Roondar

Roondar takes one look at the party (and the state of them) and immediately becomes more upset about the closet than the mimics.

> "The closet. What happened to the closet."

If the party explains mimics:
> "I don't care what they were. That closet is a Facilities asset. You've destroyed a Facilities asset. Do you understand the amount of paperwork that generates? This goes to Supply. All janitorial assets route through Supply. You are going to go down to Administration, find whoever handles Supply intake, and you are going to make this right. You are also going to make sure my name is not on anything."

He gives them a handwritten note to present to Administration. The note is addressed to no one, signed only with an initial, and says: *"Re: Closet incident, 4th floor. Please advise on replacement protocol. — R."*

**SWADE note:** If a player tries to Notice something in Roondar's office (and makes their roll), they spot a half-empty bottle of wine in the locked cabinet labeled *AngryCat 30/30 — Reserve Vintage.* He gets visibly annoyed if anyone points it out.

---

## Transition — Down to the Basement

The party must take the stairs or the long ramp. The central lift does not service the basement.

**Corridor description (vary on the fly):**

> The stairs down feel longer than they should. The basement of Drystone is technically still part of the college, but the architecture changes — lower ceilings, harder stone underfoot, the faint hum of something mechanical in the walls. The signage becomes more bureaucratic the further you descend. By the time you reach the bottom, there are four signs telling you to have identification ready and two more explaining the procedure for obtaining identification.

---

## Scene 2A — The Bureau (Basement Administration)

**The room:** Exactly as described in the college file. A raised reception desk, three unmarked doors, six windows with alphanumeric "Now Serving" signs, uncomfortable seats. The ambiance is designed to make you feel like you've done something wrong.

**Umpeen Bafflestone** is at the desk. He is wearing an extremely tight pair of trousers, a sleeveless undershirt, and goggles pushed up onto his bald head. He is reading something, and does not look up immediately.

> "State your business and your names. In that order."

He processes the note from Roondar with the energy of someone who finds the note deeply suspicious but cannot technically reject it.

> "Supply. That's Door Two. You'll want Jabbie. Don't touch anything in the hallway. Don't open any doors that aren't Door Two. Don't talk to the window staff."

He watches them cross the room. He is still watching when they open Door Two.

**Above the Table:** Umpeen clocks every member of the party the moment they walk in. He already knows there was an incident on the 4th floor — he has a contact up there. He lets the party through because Glitter needs to know what happened, and the party is a convenient delivery mechanism. He will be informed of everything Glitter learns within the hour.

---

## Scene 2B — The Warehouse Corridor

**Transition:** Door Two opens into a long corridor that eventually opens into something much larger. The walls shift from office construction to older stone. The lighting changes. The hum in the walls gets louder.

> The corridor goes further than the building's footprint should allow. You pass a break room, three locked doors, a bulletin board covered in notices, and what appears to be a decommissioned warforged torso being used as a coat rack. Eventually the corridor opens into the warehouse.

---

## Scene 2C — The Warehouse (Shipping & Receiving)

**The room:** A large underground cavern repurposed into a functioning logistics hub. Shelving units, crates, a complex system of pneumatic tubes running along the ceiling. There is an elevator at the far end. Next to the elevator is a small desk, a kitchenette, and Jabbie Boondiggles.

**Jabbie** is at his hot plate doing something with a small pot. He looks up with the specific expression of someone deeply engaged in a task who is mildly resentful of the interruption, but is too fundamentally hospitable to show it fully.

> "You want Glitter. Elevator's behind me. But she's finishing something, so — have you eaten? I have a reduced stock that really needs to go."

He will offer food regardless of the answer. The food is exceptional. Jabbie uses this to assess the party — he's deeply perceptive and genuinely curious about people, and food is his method of gathering information.

**Above the Table:** Jabbie is a Vortex Dragon. This session he is purely a warm, curious, slightly eccentric NPC. He knows more than he lets on about Supply's actual function. He will not intervene in anything tonight. The Doyenne is aware of all of it.

If a player rolls Notice (TN 6) while talking to Jabbie, they catch something: his tattoos shift slightly in the light, almost as if they're moving under the skin. He notices them noticing, smiles pleasantly, and offers more food.

---

## Scene 3 — Glitter's Office (Top of Elevator, Overlooking the Warehouse)

**The elevator** is a small cage-style lift that rises to a glass-windowed office overlooking the warehouse floor. The view from up here makes the warehouse look even larger.

**Glitter (Binderly Bimpnottin)** is in fine silks today. Her silver hair is enormous. She has one arm. She's looking at a ledger when the party arrives and closes it with the manner of someone who has already assessed the situation.

> "You're from upstairs. The closet."

She does not remember any of the party's names. She will try twice and then give up and use descriptions (The Short One, Goggles, The One Who Smells Like Pine).

Her actual concern is the inventory chain:
> "Mops don't become mimics. Mimics come from somewhere and someone puts them somewhere. I need to know where mine went."

She wants the party to find out how mimics ended up in the college's janitorial supply. She has a theory — something in Purchasing is off — but she needs external people to do the looking. Her own staff are too visible.

**SWADE note:** A Persuasion roll (TN 4) gets her to share what she already suspects. A raise gets her to tell the party about Wobbles Cobblepot specifically.

> "There's a gnome in Purchasing. Wobbles Cobblepot. He's been sloppy with intake paperwork for about two months. Late submissions, strange line items. I can't touch it directly — that's above my lane. You can."

She gives them temporary floor credentials. They can access Purchasing, which is also in the basement, through Door Three.

---

## Scene 4 — Wobbles Cobblepot (Purchasing)

**Wobbles** (see [people/wobbles.md](../../people/wobbles.md)) should look and act like a gnome who is deeply, exhaustedly afraid. He is doing paperwork with the focus of someone trying to disappear into it.

**Physical description:** Middle-aged gnome, rumpled jacket that used to fit better, ink-stained fingers, a small framed picture of a vineyard on his desk turned slightly away from himself.

Getting information from Wobbles is a social encounter, not a combat one. He is not malicious — he is ruined, scared, and in over his head. He will fold under any combination of:

- Intimidation (TN 6) — he is already scared
- Persuasion with evidence of the mimics (TN 4) — he genuinely feels bad
- Notice (TN 6) — spots the AngryCat 30/30 label on something in his desk

**What Wobbles confesses:**

- He lost everything in the AngryCat 30/30 winery bubble. Everything.
- Someone approached him — he doesn't know who, intermediary only — and paid him to accept a crate marked "Janitorial Supplies, Miscellaneous" and route it into Facilities inventory without inspection
- The crate came from Margery Yardley, an enchanter with a shop somewhere in Korranberg proper
- He doesn't know what was in it. He told himself it didn't matter.

He asks if he's going to lose his job. The party cannot answer this honestly.

**The note:** Wobbles still has the original contact note in the lining of his jacket. He has not decided whether to mention it. If a player asks directly about proof, or rolls Notice (TN 8) while he's talking, they spot him unconsciously touching his jacket. The note is unsigned but has an IT department acquisition code printed in the header — standard internal procurement formatting. A Knowledge (Academics) or Streetwise roll (TN 6) identifies the code as a 10th floor IT requisition number.

At that point, Wobbles goes very quiet and says: *"I think you should probably stop asking about this."*

---

## Scene 5 — Margery Yardley's Workshop

**Getting there:** Wobbles has an address, or close enough to one. Margery works out of a building in Korranberg — not affiliated with the college.

**The approach:** Margery is aware of the party before they knock. She has eyes (mimics are excellent at observation). She will not open the door voluntarily.

**Boxed text:**
> The building is unremarkable from the street. A narrow three-story structure, older stonework, no signage. The curtains on the second floor move slightly, then stop. When you knock, a voice from behind the door says: "I'm not receiving visitors."

The party can try Persuasion to get her to talk through the door (TN 6). She will not be convinced to open it by words alone.

If they try to force entry: a Mimic (Wild Card) is waiting inside the front room.

### The Mimic Fight (Wild Card)

Margery's workshop mimic is substantially larger than a mop — it has taken the form of an armoire. This is a proper fight.

| Attribute | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Type | Wild Card |
| Agility | d6 |
| Strength | d10 |
| Fighting | d8 |
| Toughness | 9 (2 armor, natural hide) |
| Special | Adhesive — raise on attack Entangles target (Strength d10 to break, action) |
| Special | Engulf — on Wild Card Benny spend, can attempt to swallow a Shaken target (opposed Agility vs Strength d10) |
| Wild Die | d6 |

The room has environmental features: shelving with enchanting components, a worktable with active materials, narrow space. The mimic uses the furniture and clutter to limit movement.

**Margery interrupts before the mimic is incapacitated** — or immediately upon its incapacitation if the fight goes long.

---

## Scene 6 — Margery's Interruption

**Above the Table:** Margery is not a villain. She does Trust work quietly and doesn't want scrutiny. The party being here at all is a problem for her.

She appears from somewhere in the building — upstairs, a side room — and the fight stops the moment she does. She is calm, which is more alarming than anger would be.

> "You're from the college. You're going to tell me the mops woke up."

She did not do this wrong. She provided stable product. She can prove it — she has the commission paperwork, the binding notes, the delivery receipt. Someone received her product and stored it improperly for six weeks adjacent to solvent fumes. Binding degradation under those conditions is documented and foreseeable.

She is, however, done with this conversation.

> "I'm calling the ZPD. You broke into my workshop and attacked my inventory. Whatever problem you have with my client, you can explain it to them."

**The ZPD moment:** This can be played for comedy — the Zilargo Police Department is methodical, slightly officious, and very interested in paperwork. The party's temporary credentials from Glitter are technically valid but clearly not issued for anything like this. The ZPD officer files a report, notes the college credentials, and releases the party with a citation for Unauthorized Disruption of a Private Enchantment Workshop. Margery does not press further charges — too much scrutiny.

---

## Resolution — Back to Glitter

The party returns to Supply with:

- Confirmation of the mimic source (Margery Yardley)
- The name of the client who commissioned them (unknown — intermediary only)
- Wobbles Cobblepot's confession
- Possibly a ZPD citation

**Glitter's response:**

She is satisfied. The supply chain irregularity has a source. Wobbles's future employment is not the party's concern. The unknown client is her problem to pursue through her own channels.

Then she tells them the other thing.

> "You handled this without making it worse than it was. That's rarer than it sounds around here. I have work that occasionally requires people who are not on any official roster. You've just become those people. Welcome to Supply."

She forgets their names again immediately after saying this.

**Jabbie, downstairs, has made something for the trip home.** He presses it into the hands of whichever party member makes eye contact with him on the way out. It's wrapped in paper and smells extraordinary.

## Pacing Notes (Corporate Event)

| Scene | Expected Time | Cuttable? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Load-in / Roondar | 15 min | No — establishes stakes |
| Mop Mimic fight | 20–30 min | No — this is the hook |
| Return to Roondar | 10 min | Trim if running long |
| Umpeen / Bureau | 10–15 min | Yes — can fast-track to Door Two |
| Jabbie / Warehouse | 15 min | Trim food scene if needed |
| Glitter's office | 15–20 min | No — gives direction |
| Wobbles | 15 min | No — core mystery beat |
| Margery / fight | 30 min | No — second combat |
| ZPD / resolution | 15 min | Trim ZPD for time |

**If running short on time:** Cut the ZPD scene entirely. Margery simply throws them out after subduing the mimic. The resolution still works.

**If players are engaged and running long:** Let the Umpeen and Jabbie scenes breathe. Both characters reward player curiosity.
