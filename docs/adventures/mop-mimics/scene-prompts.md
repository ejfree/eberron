## Style Notes for Scene Art

Scene wallpapers are **environment illustrations, not portraits.** Key differences from character art:

- **No characters or people** — tokens are placed on top in Foundry
- **Slight overhead angle** (30–45°) so the space reads as navigable, not a flat wall
- **Every room reads as gnome-scale** — furniture is compact, ceilings lower, proportions tight
- **Consistent lighting** — warm amber lanterns and magewrought fixtures throughout the college; Margery's workshop is warmer and more personal; the warehouse is colder

### Global Style Base (paste into every prompt):
```
tabletop RPG scene background, art deco fantasy interior, Eberron D&D setting gnome college, warm amber lantern light, detailed furniture and architecture, slight overhead perspective angle, empty of characters, painterly digital illustration, rich color palette, no people
```

### Foundry VTT Export Settings:
- **Resolution:** 3840 × 2160 px (4K) or 1920 × 1080 (1080p for web)
- **Format:** JPG at 85% quality for backgrounds (PNG is unnecessarily large)
- **Foundry scene grid:** If using a grid, set scene dimensions to match your chosen pixels-per-grid-unit
