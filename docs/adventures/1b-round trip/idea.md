## Overview

Glitter Has a Job

There & Back Again..
Glitter has a mission for you. This will allow you to streatch your legs a little. It will require you to travel to Thurimbar then onward to  Tarandro. The penultimate leg of your expedition will take you to Zolanberg. Finally, you will return to the city here via lightning rail. Here is 150gp. This will cover your lightning rail tickets from Zolanberg as well as cover any incidentals. You will be receive a bonus on your return if al has gone well. Any questions? Here are your orders. 

1) Go to Hazelhorn. Pick up some Finny 22 from Finner's Distillery
2) Go to Thurimbar. Pick up some Rooster Heads from Thoms by the docks.
3) Take order packet B to the Notaries Guild in Thurimbar for further instructions. 

Order packet B is encrypted. 

Day 1 - Leave

Leaving - 70 miles to Hazelhorm. 
In reality there are inns every 35 miles or at cool locations like rivers. About halfway is an inn by the river. Need to have some inns already setup. 

Village of Hazelhorn - Pick up some Congac. There is a famous distillery here called Finner's Finest. The Finner's family has been here for years. As the PCs approach they see a very small shop attached to an larger operations. They enter, the bell rings and Abner looks up. He & Al are working in the shop moving boxes. There is no Finny 22 ready at this time. But they can have the order ready tomorrow. Upon entering, tomorrow,  a bell rings, a teenage gnome girl, Abigail Finner, appears from the back exclaiming "daddy"? She is in a bit of distress as the family patriarch, along with his son Al. (Save this for later. name is short for Algernon. his life ended to short, but at least he had that summer in Korranberg. This could be the lead in for a got get a missing component. Al worked for that summer and did AMAZING work at one of the universities, which ever one they are located. ) has been missing for the past day. She said that the last time she heard was that he was checking on the grand reserve. 

This leads them to the animal den. If they scope it out, they find a giant spider or 2, who have captured the father, Abner Finner. He is alive and wrapped in webs. The area is filled with barrels. When freed, he rewards the PCs.

Thurimbar - Pick up some Rooster Heads from Thom's Tubers, Truffles & Shrooms. Local specialty farmer who sells his wares down near the piers. When the PCs start their transaction the following event fires. 

	Saughins Attack
  10X Sahuagin + 1x Leader but responded to by the ZPD also. They attack people and are attempting to capture them and bring them below the water.


Order packet B Says

1) Go to Tarandro - get 5lb salt cured bacon from Paulie's Pork Products
2) Go to Zolanberg - get 2 bottles 98 Zolly Nouveau 
3) Take the train home as the bacon wont last the long way back.

On the road. 100 Miles to Tarandro- go regular random encounters. This is a well travelled road. 

The ladies lake is on the way to Tarandro. It is a likely location for a short encounter with the dryads and "satyr" 


Tarandro - At Paulies, there are hams galore as well as tons of sausage. But no bacon. In the space is simple a sign saying, None Today. When asked, the butcher, who is not Paulie, says they cant get to the bacon to sell it. There are "bats" in the Hanging house. Bacon sales paused until it is dealt with. ZDP has been called and promised to get to it in a week and a half. The bats are stirges, who attack anyone that enters. Reward is free bacon and they toss in a ham or they can wait for ZDP, which happens in 1 week. 


On the road. 75 miles to Zolanberg. This is a very well travelled route - go regular random encounters. 

Zolanberg - Pick up some wine. This is specific to Zolanberg. 2 bottles of the 98 Zolly Nouveau. rats in the cellar. 
  
7 hour transit down to Korranberg (Morning train departs at 8:00). Beautiful ride through the grape fields. 100gp for 1st class. 40gp for business class. 4gp for economy. This is reembursable

Jabbie is going to make Coq Au Vin for Glitter, who has not had it since her grandmother cooked it. 

Train arrives at 3:00pm. Party is invite to join Jabbie & Glitter in dinner. Roll for quality. Reward is 50gp plus a bonus 10gp for every day earlier than 21 days.
