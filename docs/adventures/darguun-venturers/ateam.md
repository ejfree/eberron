The A Team

a crack squad of commandos, whom were freed from prision after they robbed a bank.

Comprised of a Hobgoblin (Leader), 2 bugbear twin brothers, Bonk and Axe, just call them BA. A human girl (actually a goblin wearing a hat of disguise) who serves as diversions and other tricks. Finally, Morduk the gnoll engineer.

They escaped from a secret Darguun military base. Base them on the A team. In this adventure, this group should be some sort of opposing force.
