Finners

The Finner's family has been in @[Hazelhorn](settlement:56c4b337-821d-4d30-8156-9968ef517152) for years. As you enter, the bell rings and Abner or Abigail look up. They sell 

Finner's Finest - (standard label) $20gp bottle. 10 year old cognac
Finner's 22 - $50gp bottle. 22 year old cognac
Finny V - 250gp bottle. 50 year old congac
25% discount for Library employees

Rumors of even older vintages that are stored around the continent. 

The rarer vintages are made on premisis however, it is a "dis-used" underground storage in a gnarly part of the woods. This leads to an animal den area where the casks are stored.
