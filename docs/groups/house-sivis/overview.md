House Sivis is the gnomish dragonmarked house that controls communication across Khorvaire. Bearing the Mark of Scribing, Sivis heirs facilitate the transmission and authentication of information — from personal letters to diplomatic communiqués to letters of credit. House Sivis trains and licenses scribes, notaries, interpreters, cartographers, barristers, heralds, bookbinders, and others who work with words.

## The Mark of Scribing

The Mark of Scribing allows its bearers to create, authenticate, and transmit written communication with magical reliability. Key abilities include:

- **Arcane mark** — A Sivis mark on a document confirms its authenticity beyond dispute
- **Speaking stones** — Magic items allowing a Sivis heir to send short messages to a paired stone; the backbone of the long-distance Sivis communication network
- **Illusory script** — Encoded magical writing visible only to intended recipients
- **Comprehend languages / Tongues** — Many Sivis heirs carry linguistic magic as a matter of course

### Sivis-Stop

A new guild concept moving House Sivis out of traditional guild halls and into retail-friendly storefronts. Standard messaging services combined with an automat-style offering of beverages and packaged foods. Look for one on your nearest corner.

## Guilds & Divisions

### The Notaries Guild

Handles all matters of official writing. Creates legal documents and authenticates House Kundarak letters of credit. Licenses accountants, bookbinders, cartographers, scribes, translators, and related professions.

Sub-divisions:

- **Aureon's Press** — Primary producer of arcane ritual scrolls in the Five Nations
- **Hidden Word** — Cryptography and code-breaking services

### The Speakers Guild

Manages translators, interpreters, orators, and advocates. Their members serve in courts, embassies, and negotiations throughout Khorvaire.

## Neutrality & Reputation

House Sivis maintains a carefully cultivated position of **absolute neutrality** in all disputes — between houses, between nations. This neutrality is the foundation of their commercial value. If Sivis authentication cannot be trusted, the entire economy of Khorvaire shifts.

Sivis gnomes present as friendly, curious, and engaging. This kindly exterior often conceals a scheming mind. The Doyenne takes great pains to ensure that internal family intrigues never threaten the house's reputation. She is very good at this.

House Sivis has an especially close relationship with **House Kundarak**, as Kundarak letters of credit require Sivis arcane marks to be valid.

## Secrets

> *DM Eyes Only*

House Sivis's public position of neutrality is a carefully maintained fiction. In truth:

- The Trust reads **every message** that passes through the Notary Guild and Sivis-stop network. The method is concealed from front-line Sivis operators, who genuinely believe in their neutrality — making them credible witnesses to the lie.
- The head of House Sivis is always the head of the Trust. The Doyenne and the Protector are the same person.
- All senior House Sivis nobles are current or former Trust members and are read in on this fact.
- The Doyenne, Lysse Lyrriman d'Sivis, is an emerald dragon in disguise. *(See: Lysse Lyrriman d'Sivis)*
