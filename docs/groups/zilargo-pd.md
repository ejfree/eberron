# Zilargo Police Department

**AKA:** ZPD, Zilargo PD, the Zips

**Jurisdiction:** Domestic law enforcement across Zilargo (municipal branches in each city)

**Korranberg Headquarters:** Citadel district, adjacent to the Notaries Guild

## Overview

The ZPD is Zilargo's public-facing law enforcement body. They investigate property crimes, burglaries, and the occasional homicide — most of which involve wine, either business disputes or drunkenness. The ZPD is friendly, community-oriented, and genuinely focused on keeping the peace. They are the visible face of law enforcement so that the Trust doesn't have to be.

All information gathered by the ZPD eventually ends up in the Trust.

## Role & Jurisdiction

The ZPD handles:

- Property crimes and burglary
- Domestic disturbances
- Wine industry disputes (a disproportionate share of the caseload)
- Lightning rail platform traffic enforcement
- Licensing enforcement for market stalls and public events
- The occasional homicide — rare, and usually solved quickly; the informant network makes this nearly inevitable

The ZPD does not handle:

- National security matters (Trust jurisdiction)
- House affairs (internal house adjudication)
- Anything the Trust decides to handle first

## Organizational Structure

| Rank | Role |
| --- | --- |
| Commissioner | Head of the ZPD; appointed by the city's Council of Nine |
| Inspector | Leads a division or district |
| Sergeant | Supervises a squad of officers |
| Officer | Standard patrol and investigation |
| Auxiliary | Part-time civilian volunteers; frequently Trust informants |

## Culture & Methods

ZPD officers are known for being approachable — almost disarmingly so. They are trained in de-escalation and prefer talking to force. Most officers genuinely believe they are serving their communities and have no knowledge of the Trust's deeper operations or their inadvertent role as a data collection arm of the intelligence apparatus.

Officers are lightly armed. Standard kit includes a baton, a set of manacles, a whistle, and a personal warding charm. Serious criminal matters that escalate beyond the ZPD's capacity are quietly handed to "federal consultants" — which is how the Trust prefers to be described in those circumstances.

## Relationship with the Trust

The ZPD believes itself to be an independent law enforcement body. This is accurate in day-to-day operations.

What the ZPD does not know:

- Their Commissioner is reviewed and approved by the Trust before appointment
- Case files are copied to Trust Analysis Division (Guts) within 48 hours of filing
- Several ZPD officers in each city are Trust operatives (rank 1 Handlers) embedded within the force
- When ZPD investigations get close to sensitive Trust operations, cases develop a habit of going cold
