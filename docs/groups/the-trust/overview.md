# The Trust

## Classification: Restricted

Conservative estimates suggest one in three gnomes of Zilargo's population are informants, sending reports to the Trust on their neighbors on a daily basis. This is a precise number in actuality — roughly 1/3 of the population are members of the Trust in some form or fashion.

The Trust can open an investigation into any person residing in Zilargo. They will actively gather information on the individual's schedule, contacts, finances, and message traffic. If laws are being broken, punishment will be enforced. If the person is outside of Zilargo, similar information is usually collected, with the final dispensation of punishments at the direction of the Trust.

> "Zilargo survived the war by selling information. We will continue that practice. However it is imperative that we do so quietly and with no notice. Never be noisy."

---

## Classification: Secret

The true nature of Trust activities is known only to those inside it. We are everywhere. 1/3 of every gnome reports to the Trust. Plus we run thousands of spies. We rarely operate offensively, and then only to protect ourselves and gather information. We share everything — for a price.

The Trust is also involved in the backend operations of the Notary Guild. The Notary Guild is run by House Sivis. In general practice, the Notary Guild practices the highest level of secrecy — messages are known only to sender and receiver; the Guild never sees the contents of a letter, only validates the sender and receiver. This has been audited as true during the war.

Also true is that the Trust reads all of that traffic. Every letter. Every diplomatic communiqué. Via mechanical and magical methods, every message is copied. Because the method is purposefully withheld from the Notary Guild, everything appears legitimate to the outside world. This is a very closely held secret within the Trust.

Over the years, the organization has tuned Zilargo into a tight ship. There was little fallout from the civil war and the creation of elemental binding, which produced airships and the lightning rail. The informant payments infuse cash into a large portion of society. Essentially the economy is led by the espionage organization, which is also the Noble House.

Our latest innovation for the Notary Guild is the new Sivis-stop. It is simply a way to collect more information. The official motto of "We see nothing" is correct — the method of operation for the sending stone means the actual operators have no knowledge of the message. However, every stone also has a configurable module that allows the Trust to intercept any message.

---

## Classification: Top Secret

The head of the Trust — the Protector — is always the leader of House Sivis. All "actual" House members are current or past Trust members and are aware of this fact. They are read in. Very few actual outsiders are.

This does NOT include the true nature of the Noble Houses.

---

## Classification: Above Table

The most restricted information about the Trust — held only among the nobles — is that all nobility of Eberron, all the "dragonmarked" Houses, are dragons in disguise. These dragons come from Argonessen and use their disguises to control the world.

The head of House Sivis, The Doyenne, Lysse Lyrriman d'Sivis, is actually the Head of the Emerald Dragon Wyer, Alyssa Siviridion — who is also a Professor of the Prophecy at the Diddly, the most prestigious university in the universe. She is currently on sabbatical.

---

## Character

Imagine if the Mossad and the Stasi had a baby, which also had traditional gnomish tendencies for innovation and precision. 90% of the organization is gnomish, but many of the operational and more dangerous elements are the 10% non-gnome agents who are fiercely respected.

---

## Organizational Details

### Mission

The mission of the Trust is to protect the nation of Zilargo by gathering information. They have taken this to mean *all* information.

### Onboarding

Initial onboarding of Halfs (applicants/prospects) is a secretive process and dependent on the Trust Division being joined. Most PC groups fall within the Operations Division, as that allows for the most flexibility in mission assignment.

### Ranks & Levels

| Rank | Title | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nils | Informants | General populace who report to the Trust |
| Halfs | Prospects | Those looking to apply to become rank 1 or 2 |
| 1 | Trust Handler | Well-known in their neighborhood. Located near any House Sivis guild, police station, market, or bath house in Zilargo. |
| 2 | Trust Spy | Standard foot soldier |
| 3 | Trust Operative | Senior agents. Has a wisp familiar for easier communication. |
| 4 | Trust Director | Leaders inside a division |
| 5 | Trusted | Head of a division |

### Divisions

The first five are called the "I"s. The last five are the "D"s.

| Code | Division | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CI | Counter-Intelligence | Eliminate internal spies |
| XI | External Intelligence | Foreign political & economic information |
| I² | Internal Intelligence | Internal to Zilargo. Keep the peace; includes the grannies. |
| MI | Military Intelligence | Capabilities of other nations |
| Corp | Corporate Intelligence | Capabilities of other Noble Houses |
| Ears | Surveillance Division | Passive & active data collection |
| Guts | Analysis Division | Make sense of it all |
| Heart | Logistics Division | Support activities for other divisions |
| Brains | Technical Division | Researches new advances in spycraft & communications |
| Ass | Operations Division | We take the shit out |

### Honest Language

"Honesty" is the name of not merely one secret language used among the Trust, but ten independent languages. The Trust uses these ten separate versions of signs, countersigns, codes, and ciphers to keep reports and divisions separate, ensuring maximum operational security. Agents in the Divisions will almost always have more than one Honest variant — their own plus whatever Intelligence type they are analyzing.

## The Trusted Ten

The heads of the ten Trust divisions. Each is a noble of House d'Sivis. Together they form the leadership council of the Trust.

### 1. Counter-Intelligence (CI)

#### Maelis d'Sivis — "The Quiet Snap"

A stern, unblinking matriarch who rarely speaks, but whose orders crackle down the hierarchy with lethal clarity.

Departments:

- **Red Net** — Loyalty audits and "random" disappearances
- **Echo Silence** — Double-agent detection
- **Mirror Warden** — Disinformation and counter-rumors
- **Clearing Crew** — Makes traitors vanish, and cleans up the mess

### 2. External Intelligence (XI)

#### Tolbrin d'Sivis — "Whispers on the Wind"

A charming bon vivant with dozens of false identities, known for showing up to foreign courts where he "officially" doesn't exist.

Departments:

- **Sailsight** — Watches Zilargo's sea traffic and foreign ports
- **Farleaves** — Embeds in foreign universities, courts, and embassies
- **Ink Pulse** — Oversees news and propaganda abroad
- **Blinkmen** — Runners and teleportation-linked spies stationed in foreign capitals

### 3. Internal Intelligence (I²)

#### Harela d'Sivis — "Gran"

A kindly old gnome in public — behind closed doors, a surveillance mastermind maintaining peace through persistent paranoia.

Departments:

- **Thimble Club** — Oversees gossip rings, taverns, and domestic chitchat
- **Hearthwatch** — Monitors "suspicious" domestic travel and communication
- **Pagefold** — Observes teachers, professors, and authors
- **Granny's Needles** — Surveillance through elderly "volunteers" and hidden observers

### 4. Military Intelligence (MI)

#### Barrin d'Sivis — "The General Who Never Was"

A master tactician who never served in a public military, but who knows every commander's routines and weaknesses.

Departments:

- **Drillglass** — Monitors drills, war games, and army exercises
- **Forgeprint** — Tracks weapons production and magical armament
- **Skyroost** — Watches for aerial assets, dragon activity, airship movements
- **Bonechart** — Maintains secret dossiers on foreign generals and mercenaries

### 5. Corporate Intelligence (Corp)

#### Jorana d'Sivis — "Quillblade"

A ruthless socialite who smiles sweetly while gutting economic opponents with a pen.

Departments:

- **Scripspin** — Monitors House finances, audits, and contract terms
- **Inkdealers** — Embeds in negotiations and arbitrations
- **Goldwhistle** — Tracks luxury, banking, and unusual spending
- **Clocklaw** — Monitors magical patents, inventions, and industrial sabotage

### 6. Surveillance Division (Ears)

#### Fimbric d'Sivis — "The Listener"

Rarely seen, always watching. It's said that even the wind whispers in his voice sometimes.

Departments:

- **Bugshells** — Installs and maintains magical and mundane listening devices
- **Ghost Staves** — Scryers and arcane surveillance teams
- **Hollowed Eyes** — Remote vision spells, familiars, and illusion tap networks
- **Feathermen** — Carrier birds and elemental message siphoners

### 7. Analysis Division (Guts)

#### Pinala d'Sivis — "The Mind-Breaker"

Never forgets a single report. Her quarters are said to contain a living map of Zilargo's entire web of lies.

Departments:

- **Woven Strings** — Interconnects leads from various divisions
- **Namewall** — Profiles notable figures (both threats and assets)
- **Threadcutters** — Dismantles false narratives and misdirections
- **Crackstone** — Code-breakers, mentalists, and sense-making theorists

### 8. Logistics Division (Heart)

#### Yorrik d'Sivis — "Old Fingers"

If it needs to be moved, hidden, or replaced, Yorrik has ten ways to do it and twenty backups.

Departments:

- **Black Pantry** — Poisoners, food control, and illicit procurement
- **Silent Hoof** — Wagons, carts, teleportation circles, and routes
- **Clay House** — Forged documents, forged people
- **Patchcoat** — Tailors, disguisers, and prosthetic mimics

### 9. Technical Division (Brains)

#### Selbryn d'Sivis — "The Spark-Eyed"

Eyes replaced with magnifying lenses, obsessed with arcanotech and neuro-runes.

Departments:

- **Wiredream** — Magical communication, speaking stones, and anti-divination wards
- **Glasshands** — Arcane gadgeteers, focusing on tiny magical tools
- **Nullwork** — Disruption tech, counter-spell fields
- **Honest Tongue** — Oversees development and revision of Honest Languages

### 10. Operations Division (Ass)

#### Velra d'Sivis — "The Dagger's Smile"

Wears perfume that masks blood. Handles the dirtiest jobs with style and civility.

Departments:

- **Gray Teeth** — Assassins, kidnappers, and enforcers
- **Torchhands** — Sabotage experts and arsonists
- **Windowmen** — Break-and-enter artists
- **Ashroom** — Clean-up teams, acid barrels, flesh-melting goo, memory wipes
