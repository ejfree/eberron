### Trust Headquarters — Layout and Scale

*GM only. Approximate scale drawings and level details for the [Trust Headquarters Complex](headquarters.md). Every dimension not given in that file is an assumption, marked below. Respect the [Trust's secrecy tiers](overview.md): Level 0 is Above Table.*

### Scale and Conventions

- **Plans:** 1 character wide = **10 ft**; 1 row = **20 ft**. A typical monospace character is about twice as tall as it is wide, so plans look roughly true to scale.
- **Section:** same scale, 1 column = 10 ft, 1 row = 20 ft of elevation.
- **Elevations:** street level in Korranberg is **0 ft**. The promontory top and checkerboard plaza are **+100 ft**.
- Orientation: north is up, east is right. The river and airship tunnel are on the east.

#### Level 8 — The Paper House (+100 ft)

The above-ground face of the Trust, officially a "Message Relay and Historical Archives Bureau". A charming three-story stone manor (~35 ft high) with decorative gardens and House Sivis signage, on the plaza with the Ancestral Citadel, Codex Vault, Notaries Guild, Speakers Guild and Zilargo PD. A hidden elevator disguised as a records vault drops straight to Level 3.

#### Level 7 — Whisper Hall (+80 ft)

Housing, libraries, mess halls and mental health chambers, with fountains and storytelling alcoves. Most surface agents don't know it exists. The top balcony, open on its inner edge to the atrium, so residents can look down at the lobby. At ~127,000 sq ft it houses roughly 350 residents at 350 sq ft each.

#### Level 6 — The Invisible Forge (+60 ft)

An alchemical foundry along the balcony: forges, vats and scroll-presses, artifact crafting, ink, weapon repair and transmutation research. At ~139,000 sq ft it supports roughly 230 artisans at 600 sq ft each.

#### Level 5 — Intelligence Directorate (+40 ft)

The five intelligence divisions (CI, XI, I², MI and Corp), each in its own wing of the balcony. The most heavily warded level besides Level 1.

#### Level 4 — Operations & Support Directorate (+20 ft)

The five support and operations divisions (Ears, Guts, Heart, Brains and Ops), each in its own wing. Signals intelligence, communications monitoring and courier dispatch run from here, mostly under Ears. The widest balcony, closest to the lobby, docks and lifts.

Levels 4 and 5 together offer ~313,000 sq ft, which houses roughly 1,250 staff at 250 sq ft each, or about 125 per division.

#### Level 3 — The Veiled Bridge (0 ft)

The lobby floor, ringed by the balconies above and level with Korranberg's streets. Looks like a very nice hotel lobby, with a cafeteria and exclusive restaurants. Hidden passages cut through the 100-ft rock faces to the streets on the north, west and south. The eastern edge is the river, with secret Trust and Navy docks, and the eastern opening leads to the airship mooring. The open atrium above is visible from every ring level.

#### Level 2 — The Ghost Barracks (−10 to −60 ft, floors 2a–2f)

Dormitories and training for elite invisible agents, including the Gray Choir (internal assassination and counter-subversion). Illusion-maze rooms, conditioning suites, sparring pits and disguise labs.

#### Level 1 — The Silent Deep (−80 to −130 ft, floors 1a–1f)

The most restricted level. Vaults of blackmail, soul contracts and ancestral pacts, interrogation chambers, memory extraction labs and magical containment vaults. Triple-encrypted doors (psychic, magical and linguistic).

#### Level 0 — Q Level (−180 to −300 ft, floors 0a–0c) *(GM only — Above Table)*

Known only to the dragons. Floors 0a and 0b are the Q levels, the home of [Quandry Lyrriman d'Sivis](../../people/zilargo/quandry-lyrriman-d-sivis.md) and [Harriet Lycaris d'Sivis](../../people/zilargo/harriet-lycaris-d-sivis.md), always in gnome form. Floor 0c is a huge cavern, reserved for dragons only.

- **0a:** Quandry and Harriet's home and workshops.
- **0b:** A secret rail spur and depot holding the five war trains built by the gnomes. The floor is 20 ft tall, double the usual 10 ft, to fit the trains. It is not an official stop and has no rail staff.
- **0c:** A huge cavern with its floor 100 ft deeper than the rest of the level, at −300 ft. Dragons only. Teleportation to Argonessen occurs here.

**The rail spur:** The lightning rail enters from the west. As it leaves the main Korranberg station (outside the west wall) it passes through the city wall and disappears into the ground. Multiple gates, each sealed by multiple wards, close off the passages on the way down to 0b. The gates and wards are unlisted.

### Assumptions

- **Above-ground limit:** Levels 3–7 sit inside the 650 × 440 ft promontory, so the balconies cannot be wider than that allows. Only Levels 0–2 could extend beyond the promontory underground.
- **Rail route:** the spur runs from the main station through the west wall and under the city to the Citadel, about 3,000 ft, then down about 200 ft to 0b, a grade of around 6%. That is steep for a rail line, so magic helps. The grade stays as is, and the rail stays on 0b.
- **Gates and wards:** deliberately unlisted. Their number, positions and wards are not specified.
- **Sublevels:** the floor counts, the gaps between sublevels and the per-floor contents are my assumptions, sized to match the capacity estimates (Level 2 about 150,000 sq ft, Level 1 about 105,000, Level 0 about 30,000).
- **Staffing:** the capacity figures above are rough and assumed. At 2,000 or so people in total, Level 4 and 5 hold about 1,250, the forge about 230 and the dormitory about 350.
- **Level 8 described twice:** It is first "all the visible buildings of the Citadel" and later "a three-story stone manor". I treated the manor as the Trust's own front among the other buildings.
- **Rank names:** "Trusted" is in the overview's rank table; "High-Trusted" (Level 1 access) is not.
