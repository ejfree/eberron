# Trust Headquarters Complex

The operational and administrative heart of the Trust is built in two parts: a vast underground cavern beneath the Korranberg Citadel, and a small above-ground front office that gives the whole complex a plausible public face. Together they house every division of the Trust, elite training facilities, archives, and rapid-extraction infrastructure.

---

## The Citadel (Above Ground)

Built within a 100-foot promontory rising above the surrounding Citadel district of Korranberg, this seemingly ordinary mountain-citadel serves as the visible face of Zilargo's bureaucratic order.

- **Total footprint:** ~20 million sq ft, including inner halls, noble estates, diplomatic receiving areas, and concealed access points
- **Access:** Two wide stairways (north and south) lead up the promontory, with smaller public lifts discreetly placed at the sides
- **Perimeter openings:** Each side of the promontory features natural and carved openings leading to noble houses, observation decks, and interior chambers — none of which are dead ends; all subtly feed into the undercavern network below

---

## The Cavern Below

A massive subterranean chamber rivaling the scale of Sharn's district caverns — over 300 feet tall at its peak, expansive enough to contain airship bays, docks, and multiple facility levels stacked vertically along its walls.

- **Eastern opening:** Leads to a hidden waterway and compact airship mooring zone; used for rapid insertion and extraction, secret travel, and cargo operations
- **Perimeter levels:** The north, west, and south sides of the cavern are ringed by five ascending levels, each set back and designed like industrial balconies, government complexes, and intelligence quarters stacked vertically
- **Vertical movement:** Multiple shafts and lifts allow secure movement between levels
- **Lighting:** Radiant crystals grown to resemble stalactites create a permanent twilight effect throughout the cavern
- **Air circulation:** Magically enchanted ventilation, subtly powered by the eastern airship tunnel, keeps air fresh throughout
- **Sound damping:** Each level features noise-dampening enchantments to maintain operational secrecy and reduce echo

## Level Breakdown

### Level 1 — Subbasement II: "The Silent Deep"

The most restricted level in the complex. Ultra-secure archives housing vaults of blackmail material, soul contracts, and ancestral pacts. Home to high-level interrogation chambers and magical containment vaults.

Notable features:

- Memory extraction labs
- Triple-encrypted doors requiring psychic, magical, and linguistic keys simultaneously
- Access restricted to Trusted rank and select High-Trusted personnel; all others require supervision

### Level 2 — Subbasement I: "The Ghost Barracks"

Training grounds and dormitories for elite invisible agents, including the Gray Choir — the Trust's internal assassination and counter-subversion squad.

Features:

- Illusion-maze training rooms
- Psychological conditioning suites
- Sparring pits
- Disguise labs

### Level 3 — Street Level: "The Veiled Bridge"

The interface layer connecting the Paper House, House Sivis family compounds, and surface infrastructure. The actual start of Trust territory underground.

Features:

- Administrative zone for public documentation, fake identities, and civilian case handling
- Message routing hub
- Wide stone causeways spanning parts of the cavern, giving a bridge-like urban layout

### Level 4 — Operations Level: "The Listening Spires"

Signal intelligence (SIGINT), communications monitoring, and illusion-encoded courier dispatch.

Features:

- Stacked tower-nodes and crystal lattice arrays running like antennae up to the cavern ceiling and down to the subbasements
- Entirely staffed by mid-tier operatives specializing in magical linguistics, Honesty dialects, and divinatory surveillance

### Level 5 — Command Level: "The House of Shadows"

Division headquarters for all ten Trust branches. The most heavily warded level besides Subbasement II.

Features:

- One semi-circular ring with ten equidistant blackstone towers, each inscribed with a Truth Rune — each housing one division
- The Table of Tiers at the center: a circular chamber where the Trusted Ten meet

### Level 6 — Logistics & Craft Level: "The Invisible Forge"

Magical artifact crafting, ink manufacturing, weapon repair, gear construction, and transmutation research.

Features:

- Networks of forges, vats, scroll-presses, and suspended platforms
- Resembles an alchemical foundry
- Staffed by artificers, illusionists, smiths, and the construction arm of the Trust

### Level 7 — Dormitory & Cultural Level: "Whisper Hall"

Housing for operatives, recreation, libraries, mess halls, and mental health chambers. Most surface agents are unaware this level exists.

Features:

- Soft lighting, calming fountains, and storytelling alcoves designed to reduce agent burnout
- Understated decor throughout
- Off-limits to outsiders

### Level 8 — Above Ground: "The Paper House"

The Trust's public-facing front. Officially registered as a "Message Relay and Historical Archives Bureau." Appears to be a charming three-story stone manor surrounded by decorative gardens and House Sivis signage.

A hidden elevator disguised as a records vault leads directly into Level 3 of the Underground Cavern.
