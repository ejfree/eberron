### Function and Purpose

#### Background

Origin (Port)
During the Last War, a group of Galifarian loyalists led by the Cyran Duke Ven ir'Kesslan decided to find a new home away from the fighting. He petitioned for, and was granted, the land to the east of the Endworld Mountains, known as Q'barra. Duke ir'Kesslan organized a flotilla of ships, and the settlers embarked for a new life.
After a grueling journey, two-thirds of the ships reached Adder Bay. The settlers founded a new town, called Adderport, and began taming the Q'barran wilderness. The settlements grew, even in the face of various lizardfolk tribes. Adderport continued to grow, and the humans continued to build more settlements, as more humans joined Duke ir'Kesslan's dream of a New Galifar.
Adderport is now a fortified trading town, supporting the city of Newthrone and other New Galifar settlements along the Adder River. Unknown to the people of Adderport, the corrupted black dragon Rhashaak has sent his lizardfolk troops in disguise into Adderport to spy on the humans in Q'barra.
Priority (Production and export)
Adderport supports the shard rush and what was once a refuge is now brimming with those trying to become rich mining and exporting Eberron Dragonshards. House Tharashk main operations and export business are in Adderport.
Specialty (Resource gathering)
While it's been less than a decade Adderport has changed considerably. The port used to be a place where one went to escape the Last War and live as it was back in the days of the Kingdom of Galifar. Many of those people have recently moved to other villages, towns or cities as Adderport has become the hotspot for the dragonshard rush. The port has switched it's focus to support crews, prospectors and House Tharashk.
Age (Mature)
Adderport was the first human settlement in Q'barra and has had time to build up a good infrastructure though the landscape is changing as new money flows into the town.
Size (Large 3700 people)
Adderport has pushed as far out as it can and as more people arrive is beginning to build upward.
Condition (Robust)
While some areas differ the main parts of the town are well built and maintained in the style of Galifar and Cyre. A lot of new construction can be seen in town as buildings are added onto or torn down and rebuilt.
Environment (Costal)
Adderport is built on the coast of Q'barra at the mouth of Adder bay. The bay provides fishing opportunities and connects to the Adder river to head further into the jungles of Q'barra.
Prosperity (Very successful)
After a long period of relative stagnation Adderport has suddenly exploded in activity due to the discovery of dragonshards.
Market Square (New, Lease, Spacious)
The new market in the outer city replaces the smaller one by the port. Spots are had by leasing your spot in advance.
Fish Market (Old, first come, tight)
The old market is now home to the city's fish market.
Fortification (Fortified, wood and towers.)
The city is split into an inner and outer. The inner is protected by the old wood wall. The city expanded and Adderport made a larger and thicker wall. Towers line both walls but only the outer walls are manned.

#### Community

Population density (Dense)
Adderport is a busy town and has little to no vacancy. In high traffic areas you all but elbow room.
Farm and resources (Farming, Livestock and shards)
Adderport is supported by farmers inside and outside the walls. The land is fertile to grow vegetables and fruit. Livestock was first brought from other lands but they manage to sustain the popular now. The influx of new people has put a strain on both resources and importing has begun. Plenty of people have come to Adderport to strike it rich out in the surrounding area trying to find shard deposits but quickly find out it's not that easy. Those that do strike a rich deposit soon get bought out by House Tharashk.
Visitor Traffic (Growing)
Steady for a long time but is now on the rise as more and more people venture to Q'barra for shards and adventure.
Night Activity (Active)
Nightlife in Adderport is lively. Between sailors, pirates, dragonshard prospectors and workers the town buzzes at night.
Demographic (Normal distribution)
65% Human, 15% half-orc, 10% halfling , 6% half-elf, 4% other
Disposition (Neutral to Friendly)
Adderport is a working class type of town. If you are a working class person you will be treated in a friendly manner. If it looks like you don't belong people are not hostile but wont go out their way for you either.
Leadership (Hereditary, ir'Kesslan)
Adderport has always been ruled by the ir'Kesslan family. Currently King Sebastes ir’Kesslan eldest son, Prince Ezen ir'Kesslan, rules the city training to one day rule all of Q'barra.
Ezen is a smart man and wise to Adderport's current potential of financial growth. He welcomes the houses and prospectors. Most move out to the fields but the gold flows through the city. The Prince has secret relationships with House Tharashk and lets things pass that his father would not allow.
Law Enforcement (Town watch and growing, The Steel Kraken's)
The Adderport watch is well run and experienced. It is filled with mostly ex-Cyran soldiers who avoided the mourning. The watch call themselves the Steel Kraken's after the rumored steel krakens House Cannith made for Cyre and are still roaming the seas. They are loyal to the crown.
Population Wealth (Average and growing)
The general population is of average wealth. Not many people are poor as there are plenty of jobs, especially now with the dragonshard rush. In fact there are more jobs then people.
Crime (Average)
During the day Adderport is very safe as there is no major poor areas. Violent crime happens mostly at night and is mostly alcohol or drug fueled, crimes of opportunity etc…

### Interior and Layout

#### Points of interest

The Sovereign's Abbey (Temple) - 1
The main religion of Adderport is the Sovereign Host and a large temple sits at the crossroads in the center of the city. The largest portion of Sovereign Host followers in Adderport follow Boldrei; the god of Hall and Hearth. This has been the way since the founding of Adderport as a large portion of the Cyran's who followed Duke Ven ir'Kesslan to Q'barra were followers of Boldrei and wanted to build a new community and family in the way of Galifar. None were better than hard work community focused followers of Boldrei.
During the initial voyage one of the ships was carrying a large statue of Boldrei to be the center piece of the town as well as their spiritual leader Bishop Kallistus. With the death of Bishop Kallistus the duty fell to Bonfire, a converted Cyran Warforged who refused to fight, ran away from the frontlines and found sanctuary in the church of the Sovereign Host.
The Kesslan Hall (Townhall) - 2
The Kesslan Hall is the seat of rule in Adderport and is currently held by Prince Ezen ir'Kesslan. The Hall is used for official town business, audiences, and meetings.
The prince comes across as jovial and friendly but is greedy and selfish. He pretends to go along with his father on putting regulations on House Tharashk but behind closed doors has made secret pacts with the House and encourages violence against the scales. He is also contemplating eliminating his only real competition, his father, the King.
The Academy of Galifar (School house) - 3
This educational institution of learning specializes in general education and the way and history of the Kingdom of Galifar. If the characters show any interest in the Kingdom of Galifar history they can have an audience with Professor Taldem Surgeeves.
House Tharashk (Dragonshard mining and export) - 4
House Tharashk has built a large enclave and dock for exporting dragonshards. It is run by Lord Targeth Velderan d'Tharashk, a half-orc male. He is a no nonsense business man. He reports to Lord Khalar Velderan d'Tharashk.
House Kundarak (Bank) - 5
House Kundarak has been in Adderport and Q'barra providing banking needs for all the displaced Cyran's. It's a one man shop that uses teleporting magic to move coin. Olver d'Kundarak opened the facility seeing the potential of Q'barra.
House Jarasco (Doctor/Apothecary) - 6
Healing potions, elixirs.
House Sivis (Scribes, Clerks and Message Station) - 7
Message station.
House Ghallanda: Golden Dragon Inn - 8
Is a typical inn and tavern. Simple and old fashion.
Rise and Shine (Baker) - 9
Mia Miah is the owner and operator and she is loved by the community. They call her Mama Mia and every morning you can smell this baker's food permeate the market. Her only goal is to bring joy through food. This makes her happy. Anyone who eats any of her food has advantage on constitution saves for the day.
Threading the Needle (Tailor) - 10
The local tailor. Specializing in older Galifar wardrobe. Iric loves old Galifar history and creates the dress of the time as best he can. Very popular in Newthrone, Adderport and even as far as New Cyre.
Good for the Sole (Cobbler) - 11
Shoe and boot store. Makes and repairs. Has a few magical boots for sale. The shop is owned and operated by Lorber Stormfury. The Stormfury family is a military family and all died in the mourning except Lorber who shunned violence and escaped the war 12 years ago moving to Adderport. the military.
The Bowing Shelves General Store (General Store) - 12
General store with plenty of stock. Runo is a retired Brelish soldier and came to Q'barra interested in living a life away from the frontlines. His wife, Siena, works at the school as a teacher. His 2 kids that live in Sharn: Briley and Timo. Runo is one of Gwent's friends!!
The Swinery (Butcher) - 13
This butcher shop is owned and operated by a creepy dwarf simply named Gwent and has a dark secret. On the surface it's a simple butcher shop but in the basement it's dark secret is revealed: Gwent, his family and some friends are cannibals' and worship a Gibbering Mouther housed beneath the shop. Gwent has a relationship with one of the guards at the Gauntlet and receives some of the dead bodies and the escapees. During the time of the Dino race the Gauntlet is used to house the racers and no bodies are given. They resort to capturing easy prey, mostly elders.
A Cut Above (Barber) - 14
Servicing men and women of all races that have hair and beards. Run by a gnome name Whiskers. Lie: Moved here to avoid the last war. Truth: Was kicked out of Zilargo after a run in with the Trust. He was spreading rumors and got caught.
Mr. Shiny's (Jeweler) - 15
Mr. Shinny loves all things shiny and is a jeweler in Adderport. He is here to learn and work with dragonshards. He gets his shards from independent dealers as House Tharashk don't sell to individuals. He sells mostly jewelry but has a few rings, pendants and necklaces that are magical.
The Rusty Anvil (Smithy) - 16
The goblin Kleet runs the place and he is a Dhakaani who is posing as a normal goblin. He will recognize that Rissk'it is Dhakaani. He can make Dhakaani weapons from Byeshk. He is a master craftsman supplying weapons to the Dhakaani on the surface who are in Q'barra gathering information on their old enemies, the dragonborn.
The Caldron (Alchemist/Poison/Rare Botanicals/Herbalist) - 17
A place to get potions, special ingredients, oils etc… It is run by Kostis. He is always experimenting on ingredients found in the jungle. He does not remember where he is from or how he got this shop. He has experimented so much that his memory is foggy. Talks like a stoner, man. If asked he is more than happy to help Rissk'it learn about poisons…but wants him to find a rare ingredient.
Hide and Seek (Tanner) - 18
Specializes in making quiet and high quality leather armor and is well known in Q'barra. Can make a personal set of leather armor that gives advantage on stealth checks and suggests to have it enchanted.
Adderport Equipment Co-op (Rare Trade Goods) - 19
The co-op sells and equips adventures and prospectors heading out into the wilds of Q'barra. It's a good place for information and a place to hire guides.
The Black Barnacle (Inn/Tavern) - 20
This tavern has been here since the founding of Adderport and is located in the dock area. The bar is filled with sailors, criminals, shard prospectors and pirates. The Inn is run by a Halfling named Vasto Valdamere, a retired pirate who won the bar from the original owner in a card game. Can provide info and rumors.
The Emerald Wyrms Inn (Inn/Tavern) - 21
This is a small inn and tavern which houses agents of the Emerald Claw. The Emerald Claw use up most of the inn but rooms are available. The Inn is run by a human female named Lieutenant Nia Reine and she has a number of agents in Q'barra. She has been informed that the Rainbow company is arriving in town and her commander has asked that the group be followed.
Adderport Brewery Inn (Rare Libations & Fare) - 22
The brewery makes various liquors and ale with hints of local fruit flavors. Pineapple, Coconut, Banana etc..
Clearwater Bathhouse (Bathhouse) -23
A place to bathe. A secret place for the "boys"
Steel Kraken Barrack - 24
This building contains the barracks and offices of the Steel Kraken's. They are led by the male human, Captain Tilleman Ophay.
The Gauntlet of Fortuity (Entertainment) - 25
Due to the limited space of Adderport instead of having prisons, criminals are sent through the Gauntlet. The level of difficulty is based on the offence. You either die or are set free outside the walls of Adderport. People also chose to enter the gauntlet willingly and has become a form of entertainment in Adderport
