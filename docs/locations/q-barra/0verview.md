### Q'barra

Q'barra is a southeastern jungle land, settled by Galifar loyalists during the Last War.

### Function and Purpose

Q'barra is a jungle land in the southeast of Khorvaire, a frontier of colonists, dangerous wildlife and old ruins. The settlers who founded it were Galifarian loyalists, led by the Cyran Duke Ven ir'Kesslan, who sailed during the Last War and reached Adder Bay. They built a fortified trading town, Adderport, which supports the city of Newthrone and other new settlements.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: The jungles of southeastern Khorvaire.
- District/adjacency: Lies south-east of the Talenta Plains and Valenar, facing the Dragonreach sea.
- Exterior/architecture: Dense jungle, river ports and fortified settlements.
