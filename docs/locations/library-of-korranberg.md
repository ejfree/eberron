# The Library of Korranberg

The Library of Korranberg is the largest repository of knowledge in Khorvaire. Its buildings occupy a full quarter of the city of Korranberg in Zilargo, and the library is a source of immense gnomish pride. In addition to being a research library, it houses eight colleges, each focused on a distinct field of study, attracting scholars from across the continent. The library continuously funds expeditions aimed at expanding its collection.

## History

The library was founded by Loremaster Dorius Alyre ir'Korran long before the founding of the Kingdom of Galifar. Dorius was an adherent to the belief that the deities of the Sovereign Host were dragons who had ascended to godhood, and he created the library so that he himself might ascend to take Aureon's place. His family and the city-states of Zilargo supported his efforts, intrigued by the prospect of housing the greatest library in the world. After Dorius's death, they continued to grow and expand it.

Zilargo's police force, the Trust, originated as an oversight body for the library — a fact that illuminates how deeply entwined knowledge and power have always been in Zil society.

## Organization

The Library of Korranberg is governed by a central council made up of the **Doyens** of its eight colleges. The head of the council is always an appointed member of the Korran family; the current chair is **Alina Alrene ir'Korran**.

The eight colleges have a fierce rivalry with each other in academic, athletic, and arcane fields. This competition is considered healthy — and occasionally dangerous.

## The Eight Colleges

### The Tabernacle — Religion and Philosophy

Scholars here study theology, metaphysics, the nature of the gods (particularly the Sovereign Host), and the relationship between faith, law, and ethics. The Tabernacle maintains the most extensive collection of religious texts in Khorvaire. Rumored to hold documents that make certain churches deeply uncomfortable.

### Morridan — Mathematics

Pure and applied mathematics, arcane geometry, numerical divination, and the theoretical underpinnings of magical systems. Morridan scholars are valued by artificers and military planners alike. Said to be the most competitive college for admissions.

### Aureon's Holt — Law and Oratory

Training ground for Zilargo's barristers, advocates, diplomats, and orators. Their graduates staff courtrooms and embassies across Khorvaire. Aureon's Holt students are famous for their ability to argue any side of any position convincingly, and for the fact that they always, always read the fine print.

### Blackdragon — Alchemy

Chemistry, transmutation theory, poisons, medicines, explosives, and material science. Blackdragon is the most heavily regulated college due to the hazardous nature of its work. Its lower labs are warded against fire — which has not prevented several notable incidents.

### Drystone — Engineering

Structural, civil, mechanical, thermodynamic, and arcane engineering. Drystone's tower is notable for its intentionally disorienting architecture — floors interpenetrate in ways that defy easy mapping, and the upper levels require restricted access credentials. The college also maintains a prototype museum and ballistics research division. *(See: College of Drystone for full details.)*

### Balinor's Horn — Natural Sciences

Biology, natural history, ecology, zoology, and the study of magical creatures. Balinor's Horn maintains the most extensive bestiary in Khorvaire and regularly funds expeditions to Xen'drik, Argonessen's coastal regions, and the Mournland. Field researchers from Balinor's Horn are among the hardiest in Korranberg.

### Soladas — History

Archives, historiography, archaeology, and oral tradition. Soladas scholars reconstruct the past through artifacts, texts, and firsthand accounts. Their work occasionally overlaps with Blackdragon (analyzing ancient materials) and The Tabernacle (interpreting sacred history). The deepest vaults of Soladas are said to hold accounts that predate human memory of Khorvaire.

### Lyrris — Art and Literature

Poetry, prose, theater, visual art, music theory, and the study of bardic magic. Lyrris is considered the most approachable college and throws the best parties. Do not mistake approachability for softness — Lyrris graduates staff the *Korranberg Chronicle*, foreign embassies, and more than a few Trust cells.

## The Ellipse

The central courtyard connecting all eight colleges. A large open oval of manicured gardens, fountains, and covered walkways. College competitions — athletic, arcane, and academic — often begin or conclude here. The Ellipse is technically neutral ground where inter-college rivalries are supposed to be set aside. They are not.

## Location & Access

The library's main building is a massive structure with archives and vaults extending deep underground. Information is protected by guards and magical wards. Citizens of Korranberg are expected to dedicate at least **four years of service** to the library; in return, they may attend its colleges free of charge.

The library maintains outposts across Khorvaire and in Stormreach to support its research teams.
