# Korranberg

Korranberg is the cultural, academic, and intelligence capital of Zilargo — and arguably of all Khorvaire. It sits at the terminus of the eastern lightning rail and has deep river access to the south, making it a hub for both land and sea trade. The city is famous for its vineyards, its wines, and above all, the Library of Korranberg, which occupies a full quarter of the city.

## History

The city, like most major urban centers of Zilargo, was built on the foundations of a former hobgoblin city.

The Library of Korranberg was founded approximately 3,000 years ago, as the first dragonmarks were manifesting — including the Mark of Scribing of House Sivis. Loremaster Dorius Alyre ir'Korran is credited by gnomish historians as the library's founder, wanting to erect a "fortress of knowledge" to unlock the mysteries of the world.

## Politics

Korranberg is one of the three cities represented on the Triumvirate, Zilargo's ruling council. Its representative is selected from the city's **Council of Nine**, itself drawn from wealthy gnomish families. Several members of this council are also members of the Aurum.

The city is administered by the Council of Nine for local civic matters, deferring to the Triumvirate on national affairs. House Sivis, whose most ancient enclave is in Korranberg, wields considerable political influence. House Cannith (Cannith South) and House Kundarak also maintain important presences in the city.

## Culture

Korranberg's identity is inseparable from the Library and its eight rival colleges. Academic competition permeates the city — in scholarly debate, athletics, arcane exhibitions, and political maneuvering. The city's vineyards on the northern Grape Side produce wines exported throughout Khorvaire. The *Korranberg Chronicle* is printed here for distribution across the continent.

## Dragonmarked Houses

All of the Houses maintain a presence in Korranberg. House Sivis's most ancient enclave is here. House Cannith (represented by Cannith South) and House Kundarak also have significant operations in the city.
