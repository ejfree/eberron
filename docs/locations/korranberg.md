Korranberg is the cultural, academic, and intelligence capital of Zilargo — and arguably of all Khorvaire. It sits at the terminus of the eastern lightning rail and has deep river access to the south, making it a hub for both land and sea trade. The city is famous for its vineyards, its wines, and above all, the Library of Korranberg, which occupies a full quarter of the city. It is the oldest city in Zilargo and the second largest of the three gnomish cities.

## History

The city, like most major urban centers of Zilargo, was built on the foundations of a former hobgoblin city.

The Library of Korranberg was founded approximately 3,000 years ago, as the first dragonmarks were manifesting — including the Mark of Scribing of House Sivis. Loremaster Dorius Alyre ir'Korran is credited by gnomish historians as the library's founder, wanting to erect a "fortress of knowledge" to unlock the mysteries of the world.

## Politics

Korranberg is one of the three cities represented on the Triumvirate, Zilargo's ruling council. Its representative is selected from the city's **Council of Nine**, itself drawn from wealthy gnomish families. Several members of this council are also members of the Aurum.

The city is administered by the Council of Nine for local civic matters, deferring to the Triumvirate on national affairs. House Sivis, whose most ancient enclave is in Korranberg, wields considerable political influence. House Cannith (Cannith South) and House Kundarak also maintain important presences in the city.

## Culture

Korranberg's identity is inseparable from the Library and its eight rival colleges. Academic competition permeates the city — in scholarly debate, athletics, arcane exhibitions, and political maneuvering. The city's vineyards on the northern Grape Side produce wines exported throughout Khorvaire. The *Korranberg Chronicle* is printed here for distribution across the continent.

## Dragonmarked Houses

All of the Houses maintain a presence in Korranberg. House Sivis's most ancient enclave is here. House Cannith (represented by Cannith South) and House Kundarak also have significant operations in the city.

## City Layout

Korranberg sits within a thick octagonal wall. The river forms the natural eastern boundary, curving south toward the Pierside docks. The wall is pierced by several gates — the main western gate opening toward the lightning rail station and the slums, a southern gate leading down to the piers, and smaller gates on the northern and eastern faces.

The city's interior is divided into roughly two halves by the central Citadel promontory. The northern half contains the Library Zone and Estates Zone. The southern half contains the Artisan, Bottling, and Holding zones. The Racking Zone occupies the broad western corridor running north–south between the western wall and the Citadel.

## City Divisions & Known Locations

### Outside Wall Areas

#### Grape Side *(northwest, outside walls)*

Fertile vineyard land stretching northwest of the city. The orderly rows of vines are visible from the Library Zone towers. Grapes are harvested and brought inside via the northern gate into the Racking Zone. This land is carefully maintained by gnomish vintner families.

#### Lightning Rail Station & Slums *(west, outside walls)*

The lightning rail terminus sits immediately west of the main gate, in a large compound. A sprawling, unplanned settlement — the "Outside" — has grown up around it, accommodating laborers, travelers, and those who cannot afford city rents.

- **#35** — Lightning Rail Station *(large walled compound; the line ends here)*

#### Sanctuary *(across the river, east — neutral ground not under Zilargo control)*

A strip of land on the eastern bank of the river, held as extraterritorial neutral ground by treaty. The dragonmarked houses whose activities require independence from gnomish oversight maintain their Korranberg compounds here. The Neutral Theater and a hostel serve travelers who prefer not to enter the city proper.

- **#30** — House Tharashk Compound
  - Main Estate
  - Finders Guild
- **#31** — House Lyrandar Compound
  - Main Estate
  - Raincaller Guild
  - Shipwrights Guild
  - Skyship Tower
- **#32** — House Thuranni
- **#33** — The Neutral Theater
- **#34** — Goodfella's Inn *(Hostel of House Tallendra)*

#### Riverside *(east bank, outside walls, south of Sanctuary)*

The stretch of river-facing land south of the Sanctuary compounds, outside the city walls. Smaller docks, fishermen, and river trade that doesn't warrant a full berth at Pierside.

#### Pierside *(south, outside/at the southern wall)*

The southern port district where the river widens and deepens enough to accommodate ocean-going vessels. Warehouses, chandlers, and shipping factors line the docks. The southern gate connects Pierside directly to the Holding Zone for bonded goods.

### Inner Areas

The interior of Korranberg is organized around the Citadel at its center. Moving from north to south and west to east:

#### Library Zone *(north-central, inside walls)*

The northern interior of the city, immediately inside the northern wall. The eight colleges of the Library of Korranberg occupy large, architecturally distinct structures arranged around the Ellipse. This quarter has its own internal roads and is perpetually busy with students, scholars, and visitors. It is the most densely studied and watched neighborhood in all of Zilargo.

- **#1** — The Tabernacle *(religion and philosophy)*
- **#2** — Morridan *(mathematics)*
- **#3** — Aureon's Holt *(law and oratory)*
- **#4** — Blackdragon *(alchemy)*
- **#5** — Drystone *(engineering)*
- **#6** — Balinor's Horn *(natural sciences)*
- **#7** — Soladas *(history)*
- **#8** — Lyrris *(art and literature)*
- **#9** — The Ellipse *(central courtyard and gathering space connecting all eight colleges)*

#### Estates Zone *(northeast, inside walls, along the river)*

The wealthy residential quarter occupying the northeastern arc of the city between the Library Zone and the river wall. River breezes and views make this the most desirable real estate in Korranberg. The dragonmarked houses with operations inside city jurisdiction maintain their in-walls enclave here.

- **#10** — House Jorasco
- **#11** — House Ghallanda
- **#12** — House Vadalis
- **#13** — House Medani
- **#14** — House Deneith
- **#15** — House Orien
- **#16** — House Phiarlan
- **#17** — House Kundarak & Bank

#### Racking Zone *(west-central, inside walls)*

The broad western interior corridor running north–south. Wine from the Grape Side vineyards enters through the northern gate and proceeds here for processing — fermenting, aging in barrels, and racking. The smell of wine and oak is ever-present. Cooperages and cellars dominate.

#### The Citadel *(center, elevated)*

The Citadel sits atop a 100-foot stone promontory at the geographic center of the city. Its most distinctive feature is a large open checkerboard plaza — alternating light and dark stone — visible from every part of the city and from across the river. Stairs cut into the promontory on the north and south sides provide the only access; the sheer faces of the rock serve as natural defense. House Sivis's ancestral compound, the Codex Vault, and the city's civic and law-enforcement institutions all occupy this elevated ground.

- **#23** — House Sivis — Ancestral Citadel
  - Audience Hall & Reception
  - Personal Quarters of Head of House
  - Sivis Family Housing
- **#24** — Codex Vault *(the gnomish archive of contracts, oaths, and recorded intelligence)*
- **#25** — Notaries Guild
- **#26** — Speakers Guild
- **#27** — Zilargo PD *(the Trust's public-facing law enforcement)*

#### Artisan Zone *(center-south, inside walls)*

The commercial and craft heart of the city, spreading south and east of the Citadel base. Printers, instrument makers, alchemical suppliers, scribes-for-hire, and the offices of the *Korranberg Chronicle* occupy this dense district. The zone sits between the Bottling Zone to the southwest and the Holding Zone to the southeast.

- **#27** — Korranberg Chronicle *(daily broadsheet printed and distributed across Khorvaire)*
- **#28** — House Cannith *(Cannith South enclave; foundry and artificer workshops)*

#### Bottling Zone *(southwest inner, inside walls)*

Final wine production — bottling, labeling, sealing, and export staging. Goods flow from the Racking Zone north and then out the western gate or south to Pierside.

#### Holding Zone *(southeast inner, inside walls)*

Bonded warehousing for goods arriving at Pierside that have not yet cleared customs or been claimed by their buyers. Heavily watched by Zilargo PD and the Trust. The southern gate and river access make this the city's main import processing area.
