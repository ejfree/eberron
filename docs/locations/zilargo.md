# Zilargo

Zilargo is situated in the south of Khorvaire, bordered in the north and west by Breland, in the east by Darguun, and in the south by the Thunder Sea. Its borders are defined geographically by the Howling Peaks in the northwest and the Seawall Mountains in the east. The country is networked by several House Orien roadways, and the lightning rail passes along the eastern border with a stop in Zolanberg and terminus at Korranberg.

## Industries

Despite its relatively small size, Zilargo exports a large array of goods and services. Due to their renown for diplomacy, Zil translators, advocates, and mediators are highly sought after. House Sivis regulates these services and maintains a stranglehold on communication throughout Khorvaire.

Zilargo is well known for its poets, orators, and bards who weave word and magic together to produce shows of illusion and song. The *Korranberg Chronicle* is shipped throughout the Five Nations to its many readers. The Library of Korranberg is the largest collection of general knowledge in Khorvaire, and scholars from across the continent study there.

The gnomes of Trolanport are among the greatest shipwrights and cartographers of Khorvaire — even the Lhazaar princes travel there to commission Zil vessels. Tremendous wealth pours from the jewel mines of Zolanberg. The Zil also pioneered the method for binding elementals to vehicles, weapons, and armor, a discovery that transformed Khorvaire's transportation industry and made Zilargo indispensable to the post-war economy.

## Society

The Zil are a people that thirst for knowledge. Everything from the smallest bit of gossip to the biggest state secrets is ambrosia to a gnome of Zilargo. This eagerness for knowledge is at the foundation of a society embedded in a web of deceits, deals, manipulations, and outright blackmail. For the most part, even the more underhanded transactions are considered beneath the notice of the government, which rules with an extremely light hand.

Many visitors to Zilargo are astonished at the lack of visible policing — a city watch is generally nowhere in sight. They would be mistaken to think they may act with impunity. For the entire nation — and wherever gnomish social interests lie beyond its borders — the Trust is there.

The Trust was formed by the Triumvirate to police and protect gnomish interests. Its power lies in the fact that no one knows exactly who is a member. One's next-door neighbor could be a Trust informant. Gnomes therefore tend to abide by the laws that exist, which does not mean those laws cannot be manipulated.

For most of Zil society, the thirst for knowledge is not malevolent. Gnomes gravitate toward careers that sustain their curiosity: artificers, historians, librarians, engineers, and craftspeople dominate the workforce.

## Military

Zilargo maintains no significant standing army. The nation has survived by being too valuable to conquer and too well-informed to be caught off-guard. Its true defense is information. The Trust acts as the nation's intelligence and covert action apparatus; the ZPD handles domestic peacekeeping; and diplomatic relationships — particularly the formal alliance with Breland since 962 YK — provide external deterrence. In emergencies, Zilargo can call on hired mercenaries, House Deneith contracts, and allied Brelish forces.

## Government & Politics

Zilargo is ruled by the **Triumvirate**, a council of three representatives — each from one of the major cities of Korranberg, Trolanport, and Zolanberg. Originally the Triumvirate was an oversight committee created to monitor the behavior of students and patrons of the Library of Korranberg. Its powers eventually expanded to cover the entire nation.

Each of the three cities has its own **Council of Nine**, comprising gnomes from wealthy families charged with local civic concerns. In theory, each city's representative to the Triumvirate is democratically selected from the Council of Nine. In practice, the process is determined by secret wars of words and blackmail.

The Trust reports directly to the Triumvirate and maintains a coordinator in each city who works with the respective Council of Nine. It is a state secret that the Triumvirate possesses an intelligence vault rivaling the Library of Korranberg itself, thanks entirely to the work of the Trust.

## History

### The Dhakaan Empire

The gnomes of Khorvaire have inhabited the region now known as Zilargo for thousands of years, well established there during the height of the Dhakaani Empire and the Daelkyr War. The Dhakaani clans considered them little more than a nuisance — the Dhakaani word for gnomes translates roughly as "jungle rats." Some hobgoblin sages of the time believed gnomes were descended from fey rats from Thelanis, citing their size, burrowing instinct, and communicative ability with other small burrowing mammals.

During the Daelkyr invasion, the gnomes used the strain placed on the empire to their advantage — sowing discord and igniting civil wars among their goblinoid neighbors.

### Formation of the City-States

As the Dhakaani were forced to consolidate their holdings, gnomes took over cities abandoned due to war-time depopulation. Korranberg, Trolanport, and Zolanberg emerged from these ruins and remain the Zil people's greatest cities. The gnomes gathered the knowledge of magics and technologies left by the hobgoblins and daelkyr and began to accumulate and build upon it. The Library of Korranberg was created on the foundations of this accumulated knowledge.

### The Nation of Zilargo

When humans came to Khorvaire under Karrn the Conqueror, the gnomes were caught between his forces and the armies of warlord Malleon the Reaver. Rather than fight, they innovated. As individual city-states they were too weak to resist; so they broadened the powers of the Triumvirate — previously an oversight council for the Library — to encompass government of the entire nation. Enforcement of Zil law was handed to the Trust, a secret body whose members blend into the general population.

### Province of Galifar

When Galifar I sought to subjugate Zilargo, the gnomes met his forces in the valley north of Dragonroost — and immediately surrendered, negotiating the status of an independent province of the Kingdom of Galifar rather than a conquered territory. They achieved through diplomacy what they could not have achieved through war, and came away with favorable terms. They have been doing this ever since.

### The Last War and Modern Times

During the early Last War, gnomes maintained formal neutrality, selling information and goods to all sides and maximizing profit. In **962 YK**, Zilargo's political stance shifted — declaring formal alliance with the Brelish crown. No one outside Zilargo knows what prompted the change. The Treaty of Thronehold (996 YK) confirmed Zilargo as a fully recognized independent nation of Khorvaire.
