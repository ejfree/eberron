### Breland

Breland is a populous, practical nation of central Khorvaire, ruled by a crown that shares power with an elected parliament.

### Function and Purpose

Breland was formed from the frontier kingdom of Wroat, founded by reavers and bandits. Brelish commoners accepted the feudal structure of Galifar but never fully embraced its nobility; it is said a Brelish farmer sees themselves as the equal of any king. Communities choose their own reeves and lesser officials, and nobles have to earn and keep the respect of their subjects, which has also meant more minor uprisings than in the other Five Nations.

The Brelish Parliament was given greater powers by Princess Wroann ir'Wynarn in 895 YK. It makes the laws, and the crown enforces them and oversees foreign affairs and national security. King Boranel is exceptionally popular, though a strong movement wants the monarchy to end with his reign or become purely symbolic. Breland is the only one of the Five Nations that lets nobles sell their titles, with the sovereign's approval. Many western counties are ruled by shield lords, and the western border with Droaam is a frontier.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Central and western Khorvaire.
- District/adjacency: Borders Droaam to the west, the Eldeen Reaches and Aundair to the northwest, Thrane to the north, the Mournland to the east, and Zilargo and Darguun to the south and south-east.
- Exterior/architecture: Cities and farmland cut by the Dagger River, with the Graywall Mountains on the Droaam border and the great city of Sharn.
