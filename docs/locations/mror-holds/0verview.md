### Mror Holds

The Mror Holds are the dwarven mountain realm of eastern Khorvaire, ruled by clan lords in the Ironroot Mountains.

### Function and Purpose

The Mror Holds are a dwarven realm in the mountains and deep caverns of eastern Khorvaire, divided into twelve active holds, each governed by a ruling clan, with the Realm Below beneath. The dwarves are shaped by Dol Udar, the War Below, a simmering conflict against aberrations. The dwarves have a long martial tradition and a strong tradition of chainmail. The Holds lie beside Karrnath, and their dwarves have often fought Karrnath's armies.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: The mountains of eastern Khorvaire.
- District/adjacency: Lies near Karrnath and the Talenta Plains, facing the Lhazaar Sea to the east.
- Exterior/architecture: Mountain strongholds and cavern cities, with the Realm Below under them.
