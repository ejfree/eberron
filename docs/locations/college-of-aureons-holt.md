### Aureon's Holt

Aureon's Holt is the Library's school of law and oratory, a courthouse-amphitheater where Zilargo trains its barristers, advocates, and diplomats.

### Function and Purpose

Training ground for Zilargo's barristers, advocates, diplomats, and orators. Graduates staff courtrooms and embassies across Khorvaire. Students are famous for arguing any side convincingly, and for always reading the fine print. In the years since the Treaty of Thronehold, the Holt has become a favored venue for disputes that nations prefer not to settle by force.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Library Zone, Korranberg (#3 on the city map)
- District/adjacency: One of the eight schools arranged around the Ellipse; see [Library of Korranberg](library-of-korranberg.md)
- Exterior/architecture: A colonnaded amphitheater with a bronze bell on the roof, the **Gavel Bell**, rung when a verdict is entered. Statues of famous advocates line the steps, several of them unflattering.

### Interior and Layout

#### Forum — Moot Court & Public Debates

Open to the public. A full working courtroom used for student moots and, occasionally, for real arbitration. Public debates are scheduled weekly.

#### Hall of Precedent

The law library: Zil statutes, Brelish and Karrnathi codes, Galifar-era records, and the full record of the Treaty of Thronehold negotiations. Public access by registration.

#### Clerkship Wing

Student clerks draft briefs and contracts for paying clients. Prices are moderate; quality is startling.

#### Diplomatic Academy

Protocol, languages, and negotiation. Hosts embassy staff on exchange, which makes it a good place to hear gossip from every nation.

#### Restricted — The Fine Print Registry

A vault of sealed contracts, arbitration awards, and notarized agreements. Open only to litigants or by order of the Head.

### Operations

- Hours/open access: Forum open daily; arbitration by appointment.
- Procedures/queues/permits: Arbitration requires both parties' consent and a retainer.
- Dependencies (couriers, portals, guard): Notaries Guild for seals; Library security.
