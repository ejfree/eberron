### Karrnath

Karrnath is the cold, militarized kingdom of northeastern Khorvaire, a nation of soldiers and undead armies.

### Function and Purpose

Karrnath is the military heart of the Five Nations, known for hardy soldiers, harsh winters, and the Blood of Vol, whose temple in Atur draws a significant part of the population. Its armies were famously replenished with undead in the Last War. The great Karrn Falls spill out of the mountains near Atur into the Karrn River, and the Ashen Spires rise beside the city.

Karrnath is a nation of Khorvaire founded by Karrn the Conqueror, and its culture and rulers draw on a long martial tradition. See the existing Karrnath documents for the nation as a whole and for Atur, the City of Night.
