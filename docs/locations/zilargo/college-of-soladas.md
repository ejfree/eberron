### Soladas

Soladas is the Library's faculty of history, a stepped tower of archives whose lower levels grow progressively older.

### Function and Purpose

Archives, historiography, archaeology, and oral tradition. Soladas scholars reconstruct the past through artifacts, texts, and firsthand accounts. Their work overlaps with [Blackdragon](college-of-blackdragon.md) (analyzing ancient materials) and [the Tabernacle](college-of-tabernacle.md) (interpreting sacred history). The deepest vaults are said to hold accounts that predate human memory of Khorvaire.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Library Zone, Korranberg (#7 on the city map)
- District/adjacency: One of the eight houses of study arranged around the Ellipse; see [Library of Korranberg](library-of-korranberg.md)
- Exterior/architecture: A stepped tower of layered stone, each tier a different shade, like a cut through the strata of a hill.

### Interior and Layout

#### Hall of Ages

Open to the public. A timeline gallery from the Age of Monsters through the Last War.

#### Oral Tradition Studio

Recordings and transcriptions of firsthand accounts, from elders to veterans.

#### Archaeology & Artifact Analysis

Labs where finds from Xen'drik, Dhakaani ruins, and Mournland expeditions are cleaned and dated.

#### Last War Archive

A post-war documentation project: casualty rolls, refugee testimony, battlefield accounts, and Cyran survivor interviews. Politically sensitive and widely consulted.

#### Restricted — The Deep Vaults

Descending levels hold older and older material: Dhakaan, giants, and the Age of Monsters. The deeper one goes, the less the records resemble anything written by mortals.

### Operations

- Hours/open access: Hall of Ages open daily; archives by registration; Deep Vaults by Head's order.
- Procedures/queues/permits: Oral accounts may be sealed by their givers for a term of years.
- Dependencies (couriers, portals, guard): Expedition finds from [Balinor's Horn](college-of-balinors-horn.md); Library security.
