### The Tabernacle

The Tabernacle is the Library's seminary of theology and philosophy, a ring of chapels around a central archive of religious texts.

### Function and Purpose

Scholars here study theology, metaphysics, the nature of the gods (particularly the Sovereign Host), and the relationship between faith, law, and ethics. The Tabernacle holds the most extensive collection of religious texts in Khorvaire and is rumored to hold documents that make certain churches deeply uncomfortable. It attracts clergy, ethicists, and the quietly doubtful from every faith on the continent.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Library Zone, Korranberg (#1 on the city map)
- District/adjacency: One of the eight halls of learning arranged around the Ellipse; see [Library of Korranberg](library-of-korranberg.md)
- Exterior/architecture: A low, round cloister of pale stone with a copper dome. Nine small chapels, one per Sovereign, project from the outer wall like petals; a tenth door, always shut, faces north.

### Interior and Layout

#### Ground Floor — The Rotunda & Nine Chapels

Open to the public. Each chapel is maintained by a rotating student congregation and holds a modest shrine to one of the Sovereign Host. Nonbelievers, Silver Flame pilgrims, and Blood of Vol sympathizers are welcomed on equal terms, which has caused trouble before. The chapel of the Dark Six is unlabeled and kept locked; its key is held by the Rector.

#### 1st Floor — Comparative Religions Stacks

Open with registration. Texts on the Sovereign Host, Silver Flame, Blood of Vol, Path of Light, Cults of the Dragon Below, and the Spirit of the Past. The post-war refugee flood has swelled the collection with pamphlets, hymnals, and oral-history recordings from Cyran survivors.

#### 2nd Floor — Ethics & Jurisprudence Seminars

Seminar rooms and a debate cloister, run jointly with [Aureon's Holt](college-of-aureons-holt.md). Famous for the weekly *Hard Case* debates, which are open to visitors.

#### 3rd Floor — Office of Prophecy Studies

Public face of the Library's collaboration on **Tasker's Dream**, an academic program to decode draconic prophecy (see [Lysse Lyrriman d'Sivis](../../people/zilargo/lysse-lyrriman-d-sivis.md)). Cataloguers, linguists, and Draconic translators work in shifts. Visitors are shown a tidy exhibit and a reading room.

#### Restricted — The Archive of Contested Texts

Below ground. Access requires the Rector's seal. Holds heretical, suppressed, and disputed documents donated, bought, or confiscated from many faiths.

### Operations

- Hours/open access: Chapels and Rotunda open dawn to dusk; stacks by registration.
- Procedures/queues/permits: Archive of Contested Texts requires the Rector's seal; two-person rule for readers.
- Dependencies (couriers, portals, guard): Courier traffic from Thrane and Karrnath clergy; Library security.
