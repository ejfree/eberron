### Trolanport

Trolanport is the seat of Zilargo's government and a southern port famed for shipbuilding and cartography.

### Function and Purpose

- Government seat: the Triumvirate rules from here; its triumvir is chosen from the city's Council of Nine.
- Shipwrights and cartographers: gnomish ships and charts are sought even by the Lhazaar princes, who travel here to commission vessels.
- Maritime trade: its harbor on the Thunder Sea, via the Straits of Shargon, drives Zilargo's sea commerce.
