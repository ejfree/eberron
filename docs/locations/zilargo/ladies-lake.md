### Staff and Roles

#### Amabalis

Dryad who controls the southern side of the Ladies Lake.

She is friendly but very very sad. In general she tends to be a little reclusive but still loves to engage with those that visit her lake through the daily water play & nightly parties. There are trees in the water play area as well as the water park which are more focused in the southern part of the lake.

She is sad because she misses her love and she missed all the recent parties which spiraled her into depression. She is missing the current love of her life, an dwarf named Goins Hammerfast.

#### Laurenia

Dryad who controls the northern side of the Ladies Lake.

She is angry, a little mean, but very very sad. In general she tends to be a very reclusive and demand quiet on the northern end of the lake. She is sad because she misses her love and she missed all the wonderful music he played. She is missing the current love of her life, an halfling named  Kasster Sunbarrel. Kasster plays a whisper flute. Normally, Laurenia hates music because it is just too loud. That is why the spa is on the northern side of the lake. The quiet areas are all in Laurenias part of the forest.

Once she is happy again she will go back to reading her book, which is a harlequin romance novel.

#### Pintaros

This satyr is shabily dressed. Chews tobacco. He has been disguising himself as Goins Hammerfast, dwarven SF, and a halfling bard, named Kasster sunbarrel

He leaves goins medals for Amabalis and leaves his whisper flute for Laurenia to remember "him" by.
