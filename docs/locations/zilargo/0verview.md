### Zilargo

Zilargo is the gnomish nation of southern Khorvaire, ruled by a three-member Triumvirate and watched over by the Trust.

### Function and Purpose

Zilargo is a small but rich nation, about 90% gnomish with about 2.5 million people, that exports goods and services far beyond its size. Zil translators, advocates and mediators are sought after, House Sivis regulates communication across Khorvaire, and the Library of Korranberg holds the largest collection of general knowledge on the continent. Zilargo has no significant standing army; it survives by being too valuable to conquer and too well informed to be surprised. The Trust is the unseen police and intelligence force of the nation, and the Triumvirate rules from Trolanport, with the cultural and knowledge capital at Korranberg.

The Zil formally allied with Breland in 962 YK, and the Treaty of Thronehold confirmed Zilargo as an independent nation in 996 YK. For the full picture of Zilargo's geography, society, government and history, see [Zilargo](zilargo.md).

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Southern Khorvaire.
- District/adjacency: Bordered by Breland to the north and west, Darguun to the east and the Thunder Sea to the south.
- Exterior/architecture: The Howling Peaks in the north-west and the Seawall Mountains in the east, with vineyards on the southern coast.

### Interior and Layout

- Notable areas/rooms: [Korranberg](korranberg.md), the cultural and knowledge capital and the terminus of the lightning rail; Trolanport, the government seat and a center of shipbuilding and cartography; [Zolanberg](zolanberg.md), the jewel-mining hub; Dragonroost, the mountain gateway where Zilargo surrendered to Galifar; [Tarandro](tarandro.md); [Thurimbar](thurimbar.md); [Hazelhorn](hazlehorn.md); [the Ladies Lake](ladies-lake.md); [Paluur Draal](paluur-draal.md); [Pylas Pyrial](pylas-pyrial.md).
- Capacity/scale: About 2.5 million people.
- Accessibility (flight, ramps, portals): The lightning rail runs along the eastern border, stopping at Zolanberg and ending at Korranberg; Orien roads link the towns.
