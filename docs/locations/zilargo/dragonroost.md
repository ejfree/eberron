### Dragonroost

Dragonroost is the mountain gateway on Zilargo's border, where the gnomes met Galifar's army in the valley to the north and surrendered.

### Function and Purpose

- History: the 'surrender to Galifar' that won Zilargo favorable terms as an independent province. The gnomes still treat it as a diplomatic triumph.
- Role: a chokepoint on the approach from Breland through the Howling Peaks.
