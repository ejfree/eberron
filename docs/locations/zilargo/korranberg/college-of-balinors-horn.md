### Balinor's Horn

Balinor's Horn is the Library's natural sciences school, a ring of greenhouses, menageries, and spiral towers devoted to the living world.

### Function and Purpose

Biology, natural history, ecology, zoology, and the study of magical creatures. The school keeps the most extensive bestiary in Khorvaire and regularly funds expeditions to Xen'drik, Argonessen's coastal regions, and the Mournland. Its field researchers are among the hardiest in Korranberg.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Library Zone, Korranberg (#6 on the city map)
- District/adjacency: One of the eight institutions arranged around the Ellipse; see [Library of Korranberg](library-of-korranberg.md)
- Exterior/architecture: Curving horn-shaped towers rise above a ring of glass greenhouse domes. The smell of loam and animals carries across the Ellipse.

### Interior and Layout

#### The Bestiary Hall

Open to the public. Preserved specimens, detailed illustrations, and living display habitats.

#### Vivarium & Menagerie

Live creatures, from the mundane to the magical. Strict handling rules, and a quarantine ward.

#### Herbarium & Greenhouses

Rare plants, including Mournland flora of uncertain origin kept under glass.

#### Field Expedition Office

Plans and funds expeditions to Xen'drik, the Argonessen coast, and the Mournland. Returning teams file reports here.

#### Magical Creatures Ward

Study of constructs, oozes, and mimics. Occasionally consults outside experts, such as [Margery Yardley](../../../people/zilargo/margery-yardley.md).

#### Restricted — Specimen Vault

Dangerous and unidentified specimens. Access by Head's order.

### Operations

- Hours/open access: Bestiary Hall and greenhouses open daily; Menagerie by guided tour.
- Procedures/queues/permits: Handling permits for live specimens; quarantine on return from the field.
- Dependencies (couriers, portals, guard): Expedition shipping; Library security.
