### Lyrris

Lyrris is the Library's school of art and literature, a bright complex of theaters, galleries, and salons.

### Function and Purpose

Poetry, prose, theater, visual art, music theory, and the study of bardic magic. Lyrris is the most approachable of the eight and throws the best parties. Do not mistake approachability for softness: Lyrris graduates staff the *Korranberg Chronicle*, foreign embassies, and more than a few Trust cells.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Library Zone, Korranberg (#8 on the city map)
- District/adjacency: One of the eight schools arranged around the Ellipse; see [Library of Korranberg](library-of-korranberg.md)
- Exterior/architecture: A riot of colored stone and painted shutters around a bell tower and an open-air amphitheater. Music drifts from some window at every hour.

### Interior and Layout

#### The Amphitheater & Playhouse

Open to the public. Student and faculty productions, poetry readings, and concerts.

#### Galleries & Studios

Exhibitions of student and master work. Pieces may be purchased; some are commissioned for embassies.

#### Conservatory of Music & Bardic Studies

Music theory, composition, and bardic magic. Practice rooms ring all day.

#### Salon of the Thousand Parties

A suite of reception rooms hosting soirées where scholars, journalists, diplomats, and Chronicle staff mingle. Gossip is the currency.

#### Press & Correspondents Office

Working relationship with the *Korranberg Chronicle*; trains reporters and embassy correspondents.

#### Restricted — The Quiet Room

Faculty-only reading room with a well-stocked library and no recording of who comes or goes.

### Operations

- Hours/open access: Playhouse and galleries open daily; salons by invitation.
- Procedures/queues/permits: Salon invitations are issued by the Head's office.
- Dependencies (couriers, portals, guard): Chronicle printers; Library security.
