### Blackdragon

Blackdragon is the Library's alchemy institute, a low, soot-streaked complex whose labs are the most heavily regulated in Zilargo.

### Function and Purpose

Chemistry, transmutation theory, poisons, medicines, explosives, and material science. Blackdragon is the most heavily regulated of the eight because of the hazardous nature of its work. Its lower labs are warded against fire, which has not prevented several notable incidents.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Library Zone, Korranberg (#4 on the city map)
- District/adjacency: One of the eight institutions arranged around the Ellipse; see [Library of Korranberg](library-of-korranberg.md)
- Exterior/architecture: A long, low sprawl of black-glazed brick with a forest of chimneys and vents. The origin of the name is debated; the institute prefers not to say.

### Interior and Layout

#### Dispensary & Apothecary

Open to the public. Licensed medicines, tonics, and reagents sold under a Blackdragon seal. Purchase logs are kept.

#### Poisons & Toxicology Registry

Every known poison and antidote is catalogued here. Access by license; the Registry keeps a ledger of who asks about what.

#### The Pit — Explosives Range

Walled-in proving ground with blast shields and a standing fire brigade. Public viewing days are announced the morning of.

#### Transmutation & Material Science

Labs for metallurgy, glassmaking, and arcane transmutation. Collaborates with [Morridan](college-of-morridan.md) on modeling and [Drystone](college-of-drystone.md) on materials.

#### Compliance Office

Licensing, inspection, and the Incident Log. Every accident at Blackdragon is recorded; the log is thicker than most institutional histories.

#### Restricted — Containment Vaults

Warded cellars where unstable compounds and failed experiments are stored.

### Operations

- Hours/open access: Dispensary open daily; labs by enrollment or license.
- Procedures/queues/permits: Compliance Office licenses all purchases of controlled reagents.
- Dependencies (couriers, portals, guard): Reagent suppliers; Library security; fire brigade.
