### Lhazaar Principalities

The Lhazaar Principalities are the seafaring lands of the east, ruled by competing princes who trace their line to the explorer Lhazaar.

### Function and Purpose

The Principalities are a scatter of island and coastal holdings along the Lhazaar Sea, each ruled by a prince of a seafaring family. They are known for raiders, explorers and traders. The principalities compete as often as they cooperate.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: The Lhazaar Sea and the islands east of Khorvaire.
- District/adjacency: Lies off the coast beyond the Mror Holds and Karrnath.
- Exterior/architecture: Islands, harbors and ship-towns, with fleets and trading ports.
