# College of Drystone

> Part of the Library of Korranberg

The College of Drystone is Korranberg's premier engineering institution, housed in a distinctive octagonal tower whose architecture becomes progressively more disorienting as one ascends. Public research libraries occupy the lower floors; specialized and restricted research facilities dominate the upper floors. The college attracts engineers, artificers, and inventors from across Khorvaire.

The building itself is considered a feat of engineering as much as a home to engineering study. Whether the unusual interlocking floor layouts are intentional, emergent, or the result of expansion magic is debated even among the college's own faculty. Nobody asks Floor 13 about it.

---

## Basement — Administration: "The Bureau"

Administration is housed in the basement per a centuries-old agreement. As the college grew, the space somehow accommodated all growth required. No one asks too many questions about this.

There is no elevator access from the central lift. All visitors must descend the stairs or take a lengthy ramp. Upon exiting into the center of the basement, visitors find:

- A large enclosed **reception desk**, raised off the ground, manned at all times by **Umpen Bafflestone** — an administrative gnome of extraordinary patience and extraordinary obstruction
- Three walls, each with a single unmarked, unlabeled door
- One wall of **six glass windows**, behind which additional gnomes work. Each window displays a wooden "Now Serving" sign followed by an alphanumeric code (e.g., *G73*, *BD22*). Five windows are labeled **Burser**. One is labeled **Expenses**.
- Rows of **uncomfortable seats** for waiting

Departments housed here: HR, Purchasing, Shipping & Receiving, Facilities.

## Lower Floors — Public Access

### 1st & 2nd Floor — General Engineering Stacks

Open public access. General engineering texts, reference materials, periodicals, and introductory arcane engineering collections. Study rooms and group workspaces. The most navigable floors in the building.

### 3rd Floor — Advanced Engineering Stacks

Public access with registration. Advanced texts, specialized journals, and restricted reference materials available for approved researchers. A librarian reviews credentials at the main reading room entrance.

---

## Interlocking Zone I — Floors 4 & 5

The transition begins here. Floors 4 and 5 are built as **interlocking floors** — physically separate but spatially interpenetrating. Portions of the 4th floor expand into the 5th floor's vertical space; portions of the 5th expand downward into the 4th. Both floors extend beyond the octagonal footprint of floors 1–3. Researchers regularly lose track of which floor they are on.

### 4th Floor — Schematics & Prototypes

Director: Roondar Timbers

Working schematics, prototype storage, and model workshops. Blueprint archives going back centuries. Active design projects share space with decommissioned drawings and failed prototypes in a filing system that only Roondar fully understands.

### 5th Floor — Museum

Director: Zanna Ironhide

A curated display of historical engineering achievements and notable inventions produced by Drystone alumni. Some exhibits can only be reached by crossing what should be the wrong floor. The museum is open to the public by appointment.

---

## Interlocking Zone II — Floors 6 & 7

Follows the same interlocking structure as floors 4 and 5.

### 6th Floor — Structural & Civil Engineering

Focus on large-scale construction, bridge design, arcane infrastructure, and city planning. Scale models of Korranberg's underground systems occupy a significant portion of the floor space. Useful for those who need to know exactly what lies beneath the city.

### 7th Floor — Fluid & Thermodynamics

Research into elemental binding applications, hydraulics, airship propulsion theory, and heat-transfer in arcane systems. Several active experimental rigs operate continuously. Safety gear is available at the entrance and strongly recommended.

---

## Restricted Access

*Above the 7th floor, access requires a Restricted Access credential issued by the college's Dean of Research. Standard academic credentials are not sufficient.*

### 8th Floor — Ballistics & Siege Engineering

Weapon range calculations, siege engine design, and arcane projectile research. Works closely with House Cannith and, unofficially, with the Trust's Technical Division. Visitors are rare. Successful visitors are rarer.

---

## Interlocking Zone III — Floors 9–12

The three-way interlocking zone. Navigation without a guide is strongly discouraged. The exact connections between floors cannot be easily parsed without extended familiarity. Mapping attempts tend to produce contradictory results.

### 9th Floor — Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical systems, clockwork, warforged subsystem research, and precision fabrication. Parts arrive from the 8th floor and completed systems are sent to the 10th. What happens to them after that is above the clearance level of 9th floor staff.

### 10th Floor — Innovation & Transformation *(IT)*

New arcane technologies, experimental transmutation applications, and cross-disciplinary innovation projects. The walls between floors are not always walls here.

IT is the primary operational front for Trust activity within the College of Drystone. Its research mandate provides cover for a substantial intelligence infrastructure: personnel movements, communications intercepts, enchantment procurement, and special acquisitions. When IT submits a purchase order, it is processed without scrutiny at every level of the college. When IT's name comes up in an investigation, the investigation stops.

All Trust operatives stationed in the college — including those in Administration, Shipping & Receiving, and Facilities — ultimately report up through IT's chain of command. Umpeen Bafflestone (L3, Administration) reports to a Level 4 Sub-Director based on the 10th floor whose name does not appear in any college directory.

### 11th Floor — Research Support

Administrative and logistical support for the 12th floor. What exactly requires an entire floor of support infrastructure is not communicated to anyone below the 12th.

### 12th Floor — The Deans' Floor

Six deans. What they do is their business. Meetings here are not recorded in any accessible log. Access is by appointment; appointments are not easily made.

---

## 13th Floor

Floor 13 is a myth. The building has twelve floors. Anyone who claims to have visited the 13th is either confused, lying, or no longer available to discuss it.

The external stonework of the tower, measured from the outside, suggests the building is taller than twelve floors account for.
