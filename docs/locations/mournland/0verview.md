### Mournland

The Mournland is the dead land that was central Cyre, destroyed by the Mourning at the end of the Last War.

### Function and Purpose

The Mournland is the heart of what was once Cyre, a land of dreamers devoted to arts, sciences and philosophy, destroyed in a single catastrophe called the Mourning. The Treaty of Thronehold established Cyre as a fallen nation. Its survivors scattered, and Prince Oargev's refugees were granted the land now called New Cyre by Breland's King Boranel. Eastern Cyre became Valenar and southern Cyre became Darguun before the Mourning.
