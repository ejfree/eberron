### Sharn

Sharn is the City of Towers, the vast vertical metropolis of Breland.

### Function and Purpose

Sharn is a sprawling city of towers in Breland, within the Duchy of the Hilt, built on a cliff above a chasm cut by the Dagger River. Its layers run from the sunlit upper towers of the wealthy, through the mid-level towers of merchants and crafters, down to the Lower Wards, where "grit, grime, and mystery intertwine". Bridges of stone and magic, skycoaches and the lightning rail link the districts. Twenty-five noble families are represented in Sharn; with thirty-five other powerful families they form the Sixty, the city's social nobility, though they do not rule it.
