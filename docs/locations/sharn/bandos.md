### Bandos

Peri-Peri Skysquirrel: A Sharn Delight

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: In Tavick’s Marketplace of Sharn's Tavick’s Landing district, on a ledge that overlooks the bustling thoroughfares below.

### Services and Offerings

- Skysquirrel Peri-Peri Skewers: Freshly caught squirrel skewered and grilled to perfection, basted in a secret peri-peri sauce that has a hint of dragonfruit and local herbs.
- Peri-Peri Skysquirrel Wrap: Tender squirrel meat, shredded and wrapped in a soft, thin bread made from soarwheat, accompanied by fresh vegetables and a generous drizzling of peri-peri sauce.
- Sky Spice Fries: Thinly sliced tubers, fried until crisp and sprinkled with a unique blend of spices found only in the towers of Sharn. Served with a side of peri-peri mayo.
- Elemental Fizz: A sparkling beverage infused with elemental crystals from the depths of Khyber, providing a refreshing counterpoint to the spicy dishes.

### Signature Items or Specialties

- Every full moon, Zil'ora offers the Flaming Moon Skysquirrel: a special dish where the squirrel meat is marinated overnight in a peri-peri sauce mixed with moonberries, giving it an extra kick and a slight magical buzz.
