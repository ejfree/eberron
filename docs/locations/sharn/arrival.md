### Sharn Arrival

Arrival in Sharn via the Lightning Rail.

### Function and Purpose

The rhythmic clatter of the Lightning Rail against its conductor stones begins to slow as the city of Sharn looms on the horizon. The dense forest gives way, gradually, to the sprawling urban expanse. Sharn, the City of Towers, stands before the adventurer like a vertical labyrinth, piercing the sky with its impossibly tall spires.

The train emerges from a wooded hillside, revealing the vast chasm of the Dagger River below. Over the water, soaring bridges of stone and magic stretch between city districts, serving as arterial pathways for the city's bustling inhabitants. Skycoaches, sleek and elegant, glide between towers, their elemental rings leaving shimmering trails in their wake.

From this distance, the adventurer can see the layers of the city. The higher reaches of Sharn, where sunlight touches stone, are home to the affluent and influential, their towers crowned with lush gardens and gleaming balconies. Mid-level towers house the city's heart, buzzing with merchants, crafters, and daily hustle. Beneath them, in the shadowed depths, lie the Lower Wards, where grit, grime, and mystery intertwine.

As the Lightning Rail approaches its station, the intricate architecture of Sharn becomes evident. Gothic arches, balconied walkways, floating platforms, and arcane sigils merge seamlessly. Here, magic and engineering are entwined as intimately as history and legend. Every tower, bridge, and cobblestone tells a story – of ambition, of intrigue, or of the ancient Draconic Prophecy.

As the Lightning Rail pulls into the station, a massive, rune-etched platform suspended hundreds of feet above the ground, the adventurer steps out, greeted by a bustling crowd of every race and disposition – from scheming warforged and nimble halflings to mystic kalashtar and curious tourists.

The air is thick with a blend of scents – spiced foods from open-air markets, the smoky aroma of forges, and the distant hint of sea salt from the Dagger River. The cacophony of Sharn engulfs the adventurer: street performers playing exotic instruments, hawkers advertising their wares, and the distant hum of elemental energies powering the city.
