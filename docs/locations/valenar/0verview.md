### Valenar

Valenar is the eastern realm of the Tairnadal elves, who took the lands of eastern Cyre.

### Function and Purpose

Valenar was formed from eastern Cyre. Its elves, the Tairnadal, are devoted to their ancestors and see war as a path to honor. The Tairnadal live by ancestor veneration, with warriors who bind the ancestors' spirits to their weapons. Valenar was lost to Cyre almost four decades before 998 YK, and its capital is Taer Valaestas.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Southeastern Khorvaire, east of the Mournland.
- District/adjacency: Borders the Mournland to the west, the Talenta Plains to the north and Q'barra to the east.
- Exterior/architecture: Hills and plains with elven war-camps and the Blade Desert.
