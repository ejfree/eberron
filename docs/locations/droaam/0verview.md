### Droaam

Droaam is the monster-ruled nation of western Khorvaire, led by three hags, the Daughters of Sora Kell.

### Function and Purpose

Droaam is a nation of creatures most humans consider monsters: gnolls, trolls, ogres, harpies, medusas, minotaurs and more. It gathered under the Daughters of Sora Kell, the hag-rulers who united the creatures of the Barrens, and was recognized by the Treaty of Thronehold in 996 YK. The gnolls of the Znir Pact make up a large share of its population. It lies west of Breland across the Graywall Mountains, and its rise cost Breland counties in the west.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Western Khorvaire, along the western coast.
- District/adjacency: Borders Breland to the east, the Eldeen Reaches to the north and the Shadow Marshes to the northwest.
- Exterior/architecture: The Barrens: stony, hilly country, with the Graywall Mountains on the Brelish border.
