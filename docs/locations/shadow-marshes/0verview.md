### Shadow Marshes

The Shadow Marshes are the western swamp region that is the home of House Tharashk.

### Function and Purpose

The Shadow Marshes lie on the far western coast of Khorvaire, a swampy region that is the home of House Tharashk and its dragonmark of Finding. The marshes are populated by orcs, half-orcs and humans, many of them hunters and trackers, and House Tharashk uses its mark to run a business of finders and bounty hunters.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: The far western coast, along the Barren Sea.
- District/adjacency: Lies south of the Eldeen Reaches and north of Droaam.
- Exterior/architecture: Swamp, marsh and stilted settlements.
