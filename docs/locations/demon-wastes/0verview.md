### Demon Wastes

The Demon Wastes are the blighted northwestern peninsula, said to be the sanctuary of the Lords of Dust.

### Function and Purpose

The Demon Wastes are a blighted, desolate land in the far northwest of Khorvaire. They are said to be the sanctuary of the Lords of Dust, and the demon city of Ashtakala lies hidden in them. Fiends and barbarian tribes hold the wastes.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: The far northwestern peninsula of Khorvaire.
- District/adjacency: Lies at the north-western end of the continent, beyond the Towering Wood of the Eldeen Reaches.
- Exterior/architecture: Barren, blighted and rocky wasteland.
