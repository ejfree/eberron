### Thrane

Thrane is the devout kingdom of northern Khorvaire, a theocratic state governed in the name of the Church of the Silver Flame.

### Function and Purpose

Thrane is the home of the Church of the Silver Flame, the great faith born in Flamekeep, and the Church shapes law, daily life and the military. Its people are known for their devotion and discipline. Thrane borders the Mournland to the south and watches it warily.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Northern Khorvaire.
- District/adjacency: Borders Aundair to the west, Breland to the south and west, Karrnath to the east and the Mournland.
- Exterior/architecture: Farmland, grand cathedrals and fortresses, with Flamekeep as its heart.
