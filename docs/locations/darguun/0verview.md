### Darguun

Darguun is the goblinoid nation of south-central Khorvaire, carved from southern Cyre by the hobgoblin warlord Haruuc.

### Function and Purpose

Darguun was formed when goblinoid clans, led by the warlord Haruuc, took the southern part of Cyre during the Last War. It is a land of clans and warlords, built on the ruins of the old Dhakaani Empire. Its capital is Rhukaan Draal. Darguun is recognized as a nation. Darguun and Valenar were lost to the Cyran crown almost four decades before 998 YK.
