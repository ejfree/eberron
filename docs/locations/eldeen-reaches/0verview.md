### Eldeen Reaches

The Eldeen Reaches is the druidic wilderness of western Khorvaire, which seceded from Aundair during the Last War.

### Function and Purpose

The Eldeen Reaches is a land of great forests, including the Towering Wood, held by druidic sects. It split from Aundair after a rift between the farmers of the west and the cities of the east, a rift that led first to the west's embrace of the Pure Flame sect and then to secession. Aundairian nobles whose domains were lost are called the Lost Lords. The Reaches are recognized as an independent nation.
