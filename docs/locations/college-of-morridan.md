### Morridan

Morridan is the Library's mathematics academy, a lattice of angular halls where arcane geometry and numerical divination are practiced.

### Function and Purpose

Pure and applied mathematics, arcane geometry, numerical divination, and the theoretical underpinnings of magical systems. Artificers and military planners prize Morridan scholars. It is said to be the most competitive of the eight for admissions, with an entrance examination known as **the Gauntlet**.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Library Zone, Korranberg (#2 on the city map)
- District/adjacency: One of the eight colleges arranged around the Ellipse; see [Library of Korranberg](library-of-korranberg.md)
- Exterior/architecture: A cluster of interlocking prismatic halls in grey and white stone, with windows set at slightly wrong angles. Courtyard slates are chalked with open problems that students are expected to leave unerased.

### Interior and Layout

#### Atrium — The Great Ledger

Open to the public. A wall-sized running tally of unsolved problems, with prizes posted beside each. Visitors may attempt problems and leave solutions in a locked box.

#### Pure Mathematics Halls

Proof halls and lecture rooms. Chalk-covered slate walls. Seating is arranged so that every student can see every other student's mistakes.

#### Arcane Geometry Wing

Rooms whose measurements are disputed by faculty. Measured from the outside, the wing is smaller than from the inside. Students carry plumb lines.

#### Numerical Divination — The Oracle Engine

A dedicated chamber for probability casting and forecasting, used for weather, markets, and military odds. Time on the Engine is rationed.

#### Applied Computation & Logistics

Contracted work for artificers and military planners, including House Cannith and Brelish logistics offices. Many post-war reconstruction schedules were calculated here.

#### Restricted — The Cipher Office

Cryptography and code-breaking. Works with the Notaries Guild and, quietly, the Trust.

### Operations

- Hours/open access: Atrium open daily; classes by enrollment.
- Procedures/queues/permits: The Gauntlet examination for admission; contracts for Applied Computation via the Bursar.
- Dependencies (couriers, portals, guard): Contract clients; Library security.
