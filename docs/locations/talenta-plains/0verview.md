### Talenta Plains

The Talenta Plains are the vast grasslands of eastern Khorvaire, home to the nomadic halfling tribes.

### Function and Purpose

The Talenta Plains are open grasslands where nomadic halflings ride dinosaurs and hold to tribal traditions. Their tribes gather in seasonal sites, and the plains are a source of trade goods and mounts. They sit between the Mournland, Karrnath and the Mror Holds.

### Location Details

- Neighborhood: Eastern Khorvaire.
- District/adjacency: Lies east of the Mournland, south-east of Karrnath, and west of the Mror Holds.
- Exterior/architecture: Open grassland, seasonal camps and dinosaur herds.
