### Aundair

Aundair is the arcane, chivalrous kingdom of northwestern Khorvaire, one of the Five Nations that once made up Galifar.

### Function and Purpose

Aundair is the most arcane of the Five Nations. Its people prize wit, knowledge and arcane talent, hold closely to the old traditions of Galifar, and have a romantic view of their nobility, with strong notions of noblesse oblige and chivalry. Dueling has a long history here. Many noble estates sit near manifest zones tied to Thelanis, the fey realm, and many old families claim ancient agreements with the fey.

Over the last two centuries a rift grew between the farmers of the west and the grand cities of the east. It led the west to embrace the Pure Flame sect and ended in the secession of the Eldeen Reaches. Nobles whose domains were lost in the secession are known as the "Lost Lords".
